Important facts

  • RBI 1 अप्रैल 1935 को स्थापित, 1 अक्टूबर 1949 को nationalised
  • 1969 में 14 बैंकों का nationalisation, 1980 में 6 additional बैंकों का nationalisation
  • SARFAESI Act 2002 — collateral enforcement without court intervention
  • Basel III — CAR ≥8%, LCR, NSFR, Leverage Ratio introduced post-2008 crisis
  • Priority Sector Lending: PSBs 40%, Private Banks 40%, Foreign Banks 22% of ANBC
  • UPI 2016 में launch, NPCI द्वारा संचालित, 24x7 instant payments
  • Banking Ombudsman Scheme 1995 introduce, 2006 revise, 2021 amendment
  • IBC 2016 passed, 2017 effective — resolution vs liquidation distinct
  • Payment Bank — deposit limit ₹2 लाख, no lending allowed, 75% must invest in govt securities
  • Small Finance Bank — PSL 75%, max loan ₹5 लाख per borrower
  • CBDC-R pilot November 2022, CBDC-W wholesale trial
  • IFSCA headquartered GIFT City, Gandhinagar — regulates offshore financial services
  • CIBIL score range 300-900, ≥750 considered good
  • NPA classification: Sub-standard ≤12 months, Doubtful >12 months, Loss — uncollectible
  • NHB 1988 में establish, अब RBI के under merged
  • Repo Rate vs Reverse Repo Rate — direction of liquidity operation confuse मत करो
  • CRR = cash reserve with RBI (0% interest), SLR = liquid assets held (govt securities/gold)
  • Narasimham Committees: 1991 (I) और 1998 (II) — दोनों banking reforms के cornerstone
  • Three-tier cooperative banking: National → State → District
  • Vostro Account — foreign bank's rupee account maintained in Indian bank

Formulas and relationships

CAR (Capital Adequacy Ratio) = (Tier 1 Capital + Tier 2 Capital) / Risk-Weighted Assets ≥ 8%
CET1 Ratio = Common Equity Tier 1 Capital / Risk-Weighted Assets (minimum 4.5% under Basel III)
Leverage Ratio = Tier 1 Capital / Total Exposure ≥ 3% (Basel III)
LCR (Liquidity Coverage Ratio) = HQLA / Net Cash Outflows over 30 days ≥ 100%
NSFR (Net Stable Funding Ratio) = Available Stable Funding / Required Stable Funding ≥ 100%
CRR % = (Cash Reserve with RBI / Net Demand + Time Liabilities) × 100
SLR % = (Liquid Assets / Net Demand + Time Liabilities) × 100
FOIR = (Total Monthly Obligations / Monthly Income) × 100 — typically bank expects FOIR ≤ 50%
LTV = (Loan Amount / Collateral Value) × 100 — max allowed varies by asset type
EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1], where P=Principal, r=monthly rate, n=number of months
PSL Target = PSL Eligible Advances / Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC) × 100 — PSBs: 40%, Private: 40%, Foreign: 22%

Common mistakes

  • Repo Rate और Reverse Repo Rate में direction confuse करना — Repo = RBI se borrow, Reverse Repo = RBI को deposit
  • CRR और SLR को interchangeably use करना — CRR cash form में RBI के पास, SLR liquid assets (securities/gold) में
  • NPA classification के time limit — 90 days overdue का concept याद रखना ضرूर है, नहीं तो question गलत answer हो जाता है
  • NBFC को bank समझकर पढ़ना — NBFC deposit नहीं ले सकती, RBI regulation अलग है
  • Payment Bank lending कर सकता है — गलत धारणा, Payment Bank को lending नहीं करनी चाहिए
  • IBC को nationalisation से जोड़ना — ये दो अलग concepts हैं, IBC insolvency resolution का code है
  • NHB का establishment year — 1988 याद रखो, 1982 नही (NABARD 1982 में बना)
  • PSL targets को सभी banks के लिए एक समान मानना — PSBs, Private, Foreign banks के targets अलग-अलग हैं
  • UPI को RBI directly operate करता है — गलत, NPCI operates, RBI regulate करता है
  • RBI Governor का term 6 years मानना — term 5 years or age 65 (whichever earlier)
  • Sarfaesi Act 1980 या 1995 याद करना — सही year 2002 है
  • CIBIL score का range — 300-900, not 0-1000 या 100-500
