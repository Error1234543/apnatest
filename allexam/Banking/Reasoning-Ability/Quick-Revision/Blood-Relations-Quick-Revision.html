Blood Relations — Quick Revision (Banking Reasoning | Hindi)

BankingHindiQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Rakt Sambhandh (Blood Relations) vyakaranik shabdavali hai jo prashno mein do ya adhik vyaktiyon ke beech rishton ko prastut karti hai. Bank PO/Clerk exams mein is chapter se normally 1-2 questions aate hain, aur inme speed aur accuracy dono maayne rakhti hain.
  • Rishton ki dikhayi jaane wali vyavastha do prakar ki hoti hai: (1) Seedha/Pratyaksh — kisi vyakti ke sapeksh doosre ka rishta diya gaya ho, jaise 'A, B ka pita hai'; (2) Apratyaksh/Sandharbh — ek anjaan vyakti ke sandarbh mein poocha gaya ho, jaise 'uske pitaji ke ekmatra putr ki putri' jaisa vakya.
  • Ek saral rule: Har rishta hamesha kisi ek mool vyakti (Anchor Person) ke sapeksh hota hai. Question padhte samay sabse pehle woh vyakti pehchano jiske sapeksh sab kuch define ho raha hai. Uske baad hi rishte aage badhao.
  • Rakt sambandh mein gender ka dhyan rakhna anivarya hai — Putra/Putra = Purush, Putrika/Duhitara = Stri; Pita = Purush, Mata = Stri; Pati = Purush, Patni = Stri. Galat gender maan lena hi sabse badi galti hai jo options ke chakkar mein ho jaati hai.

Key concepts

  • Mool parivarik shabdavali (Hindi ↔ English): पिता/Father, माता/Mother, भाई/Brother, बहन/Sister, पुत्र/Son, पुत्री/Daughter, पति/Husband, पत्नी/Wife, जेठा/Eldest brother, छोटा/Youngest brother, भतीजा/Nephew (brother's son), भतीजी/Niece (brother's daughter), चुचेरा/ maternal uncle का पुत्र (cousin via uncle), चाचा/Uncle (father's younger brother), ताऊ/Uncle (father's elder brother), मौसा/Aunt (mother's sister), फूफा/Uncle (mother's brother), फौजी नहीं — मामा/Uncle (mother's brother), नाना/ maternal grandfather, नानी/maternal grandmother, ददा/paternal grandfather, दादी/paternal grandmother, पौत्र/grandson, पोती/granddaughter, साला/Brother-in-law (wife's brother), साली/Sister-in-law (wife's sister), दुहुनर/ Brother's wife, भाभी/ Elder brother's wife, देवर/ Husband's younger brother, देवरानी/Husband's younger brother की पत्नी।
  • Gender identification rules (सबसे महत्वपूर्ण): केवल 'putra, bhrata, pati, bhaisal' jaise shabdon se vyakti PURUSH hai. 'Putri, bhanija, bhain' jaise shabdon se STRI hai. Jis shabd se gender na pata chale (jaise bhanja, phupha, mamu, nana, dada), unke liye option dekhte samay dono gender sambhavit maango aur answer match karo.
  • Statement-by-statement breakdown technique: Bade vakyon ko todkar har line ko alag-alag likho. Har line ke baad apne paper par diagram banao — circle aur square ya short lines se rishta dikhao. Yeh technique bank exam mein fastest tarika hai kyunki 3-4 line vakya ko seedha dimaag mein todne se galti easy hai.
  • Point-to-point approach (मुख से शुरू करो): 'वह मेरा पिता का एकमात्र पुत्र है' jaise vakya mein 'उसके पिता' ko anchor banao, uska ekmatra putra nikalo, phir aage badho. Kabhi bhi sabse pehle antimal shabd par mat jaao; mool anchor se start karo.
  • Reverse-reading questions: Kuch questions ulte tareeke se likhe hote hain — jaise 'A, B ki puzi ki patni hai'. Yahan B se shuru karo: B → putri → A dusri stri se vivahit. Phir pooche gaye rishte ka uttar do. Seedha aur ulta, dono pattern regularly practice karo.
  • Elimination by gender and generation: Agar option A strili hokar bhi purush jaisa rishta hai (ya vice versa), use immediately delete kar do. Isi tarah generation check karo — agar rishta grandparent shreani ka hai par option peer generation ka milta hai, toh galat hai. Yeh shortcut 2 options remove kar deta hai, average time 10-15 second bachata hai.

Important facts

  • Banking Prelims aur Mains dono mein Blood Relations se 1-2 questions aate hain. Prelims mein zyada tar 1 question, Mains reasoning mein kabhi-kabhi 2. Toh average 15-20 second per question target rakho.
  • High-yield traps jo repeatedly aate hain: 'ekmatra putra/daughter', 'ekmatra bhai/behen', 'patni/pati ka akela bachcha', 'divorce liya hua', 'step-parent'. Inme gender ya count change hota hai, isliye 'ekmatra' wale shabd ko highlight karke padho.
  • Cousin relations ke Hindi terms confuse karte hain: चाचा/मामा/फूफा अलग-अलग uncles hain; भाती/भतीजा/चचेरा अलग-अलग cousins. Inke beech formal parikhyan se behtar practice-based distinction yaad rakho.
  • Common error source: do same-name relatives. Jab do log same naam ke hon (jaise 'Rahul' do baar aaye), toh unhe diagram mein alag circle ya label dekar chinhao, warna rishta atak jaata hai.
  • Diagram symbol convention: ♂ = Purush (square/line with label), ♀ = Stri (circle). Rishta: horizontal line = spouses, vertical line = parent-child, double horizontal = marriage/engagement depending on context.
  • Speed benchmark: acche student 1 question 20 second mein, average 30-40 second, average se kam speed waale 60+ second lete hain. Target consistently 25-30 second per Blood Relation question rakhna chahiye.

Memory aids

  • Rishte ko yaad rakhne ka short mnemonic: PM-BP (Pita-Maa-Bhai-Putri) se seedha nuclear family start, FMP (Father-Mother-Spouse) se married link, GF-MF (GrandFather-GrandMother) do taraf se — in teen groups ko alag-alag yaad karo.
  • Gender tag rule yaad rakhne ke liye phrase: 'Putra putrika bhed — putrak ko 'a', putrika ko 'aa'' jaise short sound association use karo. Final check ke liye dimaag mein ek line bol lo: 'Har rishta ke aakhiri shabd ka gender check.'
  • Direction mnemonic jaisa simple trick: 'Upar badho toh senior, neeche aao toh junior' — vertical lines upar jayein toh mool vyakti se bada (ancestor), neeche jaye toh chhota (descendant). Horizontal = same generation (spouse/sibling/cousin).
  • Question padhte waqt anchor point ko dimaag mein chipkao: shabd 'मेरे/उसके/तुम्हारा' jo bhi mool anchor de, pehli line mein wahi pakdo. Baaki saara safar usi se start hota hai.

Exam tips

  • Exam hall mein pehle gender check karo, phir generation, phir exact rishta. Is sequence se 50% galatiyan prevent ho jaati hain.
  • Diagram ko short aur saaf rakho. Full diagram mein fat jaana time waste hai. Sirf nodes aur lines ka skel banao, names chhote likho.
  • Agar 45 second mein answer na nikle, toh ek intelligent guess ke saath agle question par chalo. Blood Relation 15-20 second ka question hona chahiye — zyada time lene ka matlab technique me phasna.
  • Option elimination ki pehli check: agar 2 options gender ke hisaab se match hi nahi kar rahe, use turant bahar kardo. Aise hi generation match na ho toh turant hata do.
  • Practice plan: rozana 8-10 questions, usme se kam se kam 3 apratyaksh/sandharbh type. 10 din regular practice se average speed 25 second/question tak pahunch sakti hai.

Common traps

  • Trap 1 — Anchor confuse karna: 'वह उसके पिता के एकमात्र पुत्र की पुत्री है' jaise vakya mein 'उसके' se shuru karna chahiye, par student aksar 'वह' se start kar dete hain aur ulta chalte hain.
  • Trap 2 — Gender miss karna: 'भाभी का पुत्र है' ko padhkar sirf 'putra' dekh kar male मान लेte hain, par 'भाभी' female hai — rishta 'bhabhi ka putra' = nephew (भतीजा).
  • Trap 3 — Ekmatra (only) ka meaning ghuma dena: 'एकमात्र पुत्र' matlab sirf ek hi putr hai, toh anya sibling option automatically out. Student aksar isse ignore karke dusre options chalane lagte hain.
  • Trap 4 — Same-name duplicate: Do Rahul ya do Priya aane par unhe alag track karna padta hai. Diagram par name likhna zaroori hai, warna rishta galat match ho jaata hai.
  • Trap 5 — Option mein se answer match karte samay blind selection: Diagram se nikala hua rishta aur option ka rishta match karo, par gender aur generation dono milne chahiye. Koi ek mismatch ho toh option galat.
