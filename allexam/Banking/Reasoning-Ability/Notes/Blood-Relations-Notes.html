Blood Relations क्या है और Banking में इसका महत्व

Blood Relations (रक्त संबंध) Reasoning Ability का एक मूलभूत और उच्च-आवृत्ति वाला टॉपिक है। Banking परीक्षाओं (IBPS PO/Clerk, SBI PO/Clerk, RBI Grade B, IBPS RRB) में इससे प्रति सेट आमतौर पर 1 से 3 प्रश्न पूछे जाते हैं। ये प्रश्न अंक देने वाले होते हैं यदि सही तरीके से हल किए जाएं, क्योंकि इन्हें लॉजिकल ड्रॉइंग या फैमिली ट्री बनाकर आसानी से सुलझाया जा सकता है।

Blood Relations के प्रश्न दो मुख्य प्रकार के होते हैं:
1) परिचय वाले प्रश्न (Introduction-type): "वह मुझसे कैसे संबंध रखती है?" जैसे प्रश्न जहां किसी ने किसी को معرفی किया हो।
2) पुंज/पहेली वाले प्रश्न (Puzzle-type): पूरे परिवार का चार्ट बनाने वाले, जहां कई लोगों के बीच संबंध दिए जाएं और फिर कोई विशिष्ट संबंध पूछा जाए।

Banking में इनका difficulty level आसान से मध्यम होता है। समय सीमा 1-2 मिनट प्रति प्रश्न होती है, इसलिए तेजी सेFamily Tree बनाना सीखना जरूरी है।

मूल रक्त संबंध शब्दावली (हिंदी–अंग्रेजी)

Blood Relations के प्रश्नों को हल करने के लिए संबंध शब्दों की स्पष्ट समझ जरूरी है। नीचे प्राथमिक और द्वितीयक सभी महत्वपूर्ण शब्द दिए गए हैं।

■ प्राथमिक (Primary) संबंध:
• Father / पिता — P
• Mother / माता — Q
• Husband / पति — ©
• Wife / पत्नी — ®
• Son / पुत्र — +
• Daughter / पुत्री — −
• Brother / भ्राता — △ (या +)
• Sister / भगिनी — ◇ (या −)
• Spouse / सहवर्ती — ©/@

■ द्वितीयक (Secondary) संबंध (जो अक्सर पूछे जाते हैं):
• Maternal Uncle / मामा — mother का भ्राता
• Maternal Aunt / मौसी — mother की भगिनी
• Paternal Uncle / चाचा — father का भ्राता
• Paternal Aunt / साली/ताई — father की भगिनी
• Nephew / भतीजा — brother/sister का पुत्र
• Niece / भतीजी — brother/sister की पुत्री
• Son-in-law / दामाद — daughter का पति
• Daughter-in-law / बहू — son की पत्नी
• Grandfather / दादा/नाना — parent का पिता
• Grandmother / दादी/नानी — parent की माता
• Grandson / पोता — son/daughter का पुत्र
• Granddaughter / पोती — son/daughter की पुत्री
• Cousin / चाचा/मामा का पुत्र-पुत्री
• Brother-in-law / देवर/जेठ/साला — spouse का भ्राता या partner का भ्राता
• Sister-in-law / भाभी/साली — spouse की भगिनी या partner की भगिनी

■ Banking में अक्सर भ्रम पैदा करने वाले शब्द:
• "Only child" = जिसके भाई-बहन नहीं हैं
• "Eldest/youngest" = क्रम बताता है, लेकिन gender नहीं बताता जब तक स्पष्ट न हो
• "Child" = son या daughter दोनों हो सकते हैं, context से पहचानो
• "Parent" = father या mother दोनों, gender पता नहीं होता जब तक संदर्भ न मिले
• "Sibling" = brother या sister दोनों

संबंध निर्धारण के तीन तरीके और जबڑी संकेत

Blood Relations के प्रश्नों को हल करने के तीन मानक तरीके हैं, जिनमें से Banking exams में सबसे अधिक उपयोगFamily Tree Method होता है।

1)Family Tree Method (सबसे विश्वसनीय):
• हर व्यक्ति का एक प्रतीक बनाओ
• Male = □ या +, Female = ○ या −
•夫妻关系 = — या ©, Divorce = !=
•_generation_gap = ऊपर-नीचे की पंक्तियाँ
• Steps: (a) सबसे पहले जो pair सबसे स्पष्ट हो उसे पकड़ो, (b) धीरे-धीरे बाकी जोड़ो, (c) जहाँ gender अनिश्चित हो वहाँ ? लगाओ, (d) cuối سوال की शर्त के अनुसार gender eliminate करो।

2) Pointing / Introduction Type Formula Method:
जब कोई point करके कहता है: "वह मेरी मँहती की एकांत पुत्री है" — ऐसे प्रश्नों में back-to-back relationship निकालने वाला formula काम आता है:
• Husband of my mother = ਮਾ (Mother’s husband) = Mama / paternal-maternal depending context
• Wife of my brother = भगिनी-पत्नी = भाभी
Formula shortcut: reverse direction से count करो। example: "She is the daughter of my mother’s only son" → my mother’s only son = मेरा भाई → his daughter = मेरी भतीजी।

3) Table Method (for large puzzle-type):
• Rows = individuals, Columns = properties (gender, generation, relationship)
• Banking में यह method तब useful होता है जब 6-8 लोगों का chain दिया जाए

जबड़ी संकेत (Key Hints):
• "Only son/daughter" → वही single child है
• "Eldest son" → first-born male
• "A has no sister" → A male हो सकता है या female (only brother है)
• "B is the mother of A" → B обязательноFemale
• "C is the father of D" → C обязательноMale

Family Tree Construction — चरण-दर-चरण प्रक्रिया

प्रत्येक Blood Relations प्रश्न कोFamily Tree में बदलना एक systematic process है। इसे 5 चरणों में याद रखो:

Step 1 —锚点 (Anchor) खोजो: सबसे पहले वह व्यक्ति खोजो जिसके बारे में सबसे ज्यादा information दी गई हो। usually ये "I/my mother/my father/my brother" वाला person होता है।

Step 2 —Generation निर्धारित करो: प्रश्न में mention हुए सभी people को generation wise arrange करो। पहली पीढ़ी = grandparents, दूसरी = parents, तीसरी = children, चौथी = grandchildren।

Step 3 —Symbol लगाओ:
• Male: □, +, या M
• Female: ○, −, या F
• Unknown gender: △ या ?
• Marriage: — वा © between spouses
• Siblings: — between brothers/sisters on same line
• Parent-child:vertical line ↓

Step 4 —Elimination करो: जहाँ gender पता चल जाता हो वहाँ ? हटाओ। "Only son" मिलते ही siblings में से female eliminate हो जाती है।

Step 5 —Verification: अंत में पूरा diagram check करो कि क्या सभी given statements satisfy हो रही हैं या नहीं।

उदाहरण (Worked):
"अमित ने कहा, 'वह मेरी पत्नी के पिता की एकमात्र पुत्री की माँ है।'
→ Amit ki patni = wife
→ Wife ke pita = wife's father
→ Wife's father ki ekmatra putri = wife (since only daughter)
→ Uski maa = wife's mother = Amit ki saas
✓ Answer: सास

सबसे अधिक पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न पैटर्न (Banking)

Banking exams में Blood Relations से जो पैटर्न नियमित रूप से आते हैं उन्हें 6 श्रेणियों में बाँटा जा सकता है:

1) Pointing-type (सबसे अधिक आवृत्ति):
• Person A points to a photo and says: "This is the son of my mother's only daughter." What is the relation?
• Trick: "my mother's only daughter" = either the speaker herself (if female) or her sister. If speaker is male → sister. Then "son of sister" = nephew.
• इसमें direction reversed करना सीखो।

2) Coded Blood Relations:
• Symbols दिए जाएँ: e.g., A + B means A is father of B; A − B means A is mother of B; A × B means A is sister of B; A ÷ B means A is brother of B.
• Question: P ÷ Q + R − S × T. Find relation of T w.r.t P.
• Strategy: left-to-right decode करो, फिर family tree बनाओ।
• Trick: symbol → relation mapping याद रखो और direction ध्यान से follow करो।

3) Statement-Based (Assertion pairs):
• दो कथन दिए जाएँ: (I) A is father of B. (II) C is brother of B.
• Find relation of A w.r.t C.
• Answer depends on whether you can combine both — yes if they share a common person.

4) Generation-gap specific:
• "How is A related to D if A is the grandmother of B, B is the father of C, and C is the sister of D?"
• Use generation counting: A = gen 1, B = gen 2, C & D = gen 3. A is grandmother of D.

5) Only-child / No-sibling traps:
• "A has no brother. B is A's only sibling." → B could be sister.
• "C is the only daughter of D." → D may have sons too, but C is only daughter.
• ये traps examiners अक्सर डालते हैं — "only" शब्द पर विशेष ध्यान दो।

6) Multi-step complex chains:
• 5-6 लोग involved हों, जिनमें से एक person का relation दूसरे से पूछा जाए।
• Complete family tree बनाना ही एकमात्र reliable तरीका है।

समस्या-समाधान: worked examples with full derivation

Example 1 (Pointing type):
प्रश्न: एक व्यक्ति किसी photograph की ओर इशारा करते हुए कहता है, "यह मेरी माता के पुत्र की पत्नी है।" Photograph वाली व्यक्ति का उस व्यक्ति से क्या संबंध है?

Solution:
• His mata ke Putra = Uska bhai (brother)
• Bhai ki patni = भाभी (sister-in-law)
✓ Answer: भाभी

Example 2 (Coded):
प्रश्न: यदि A $ B means A is father of B, A # B means A is mother of B, A @ B means A is brother of B, तो P @ Q $ R # S में S का P से क्या संबंध है?

Solution:
• P @ Q → P is brother of Q
• Q $ R → Q is father of R
• R # S → R is mother of S
So P is brother of Q (who is father of R, who is mother of S).
Thus P is maternal uncle of S. ✓

Example 3 (Complex chain):
प्रश्न: आर्यन ने कहा, "मेरी पत्नी के भाई की पत्नी की दादी मेरी माता हैं।" आर्यन की पत्नी और उस भाई की पत्नी के बीच क्या संबंध है?

Solution:
• Aryaan ki patni ke bhaeei ki patnee ki daadee = aryaaN kii maataa
• So Aryaan's mother = wife of brother-in-law's grandmother
• Brother-in-law's grandmother = paternal grandmother of brother-in-law = same as Aryaan's mother (since Aryaan's mother is also the grandmother of her own son's wife's husband's grandmother...)
Let me re-analyse:
• My patnee ke bhaeei = brother-in-law (patni ke side)
• Brother-in-law ki patnee = sister-in-law (by marriage)
• That sister-in-law ki daadee = Aryaan ki maataa
→ So Aryaan's mother = brother-in-law's paternal grandmother
→ Brother-in-law's father = Aryaan's maternal grandfather's son = Aryaan's mother's son = Aryaan himself? No...
Re-do properly:
• Let Aryaan = A, his wife = W
• W's brother = B
• B's wife = C
• C's grandmother = A's mother = M
→ C's grandmother is M → C's parent is M's child → that child could be A or A's sibling
→ B is W's brother, so B is also W's sibling
→ C is B's wife
→ C's grandmother M means C's parent is M's child
→ If M's child = A, then C's parent = A → A is father of C
→ But C is B's wife → contradiction unless A = B which is impossible
So M's other child (sibling of A) must be C's parent → C is daughter of A's sibling
→ Thus C is niece/nephew of A? No — C is wife of B, and B is W's brother...
Actually simpler: A's mother M is grandmother of C. So C's parent is child of M. M's children include A. If A is NOT C's parent, then some other child of M is C's parent. But B is W's brother (W is A's wife), so B is not related by blood to A. Therefore B cannot be C's parent through M.
Wait — C's parent must be A himself. That would mean A is father of C. But C is B's wife. So B married his father's daughter? That's impossible in normal kinship.
Therefore the only consistent reading: C's grandmother M → C is granddaughter of M → C's parent is one of M's children. M's child could be A or A's sibling. If it's A's sibling, say S, then S is parent of C. B is W's brother. No direct link. Unless we assume standard nuclear family where M's children are A and possibly others.
Actually the intended simple answer: "My wife's brother's wife's grandmother is my mother" → My mother = grandmother of (wife's brother's wife). This makes wife's brother's wife = granddaughter of my mother. Since wife's brother is my wife's brother, and his wife is married into my family... this is a known puzzle pattern. The answer typically is: My wife and her brother's wife are sisters-in-law (by marriage to two brothers or to same brother).
But let me give the clean banking-style answer:
Relationship: Aryaan की पत्नी और B की पत्नी C के बीच कोई सीधा रक्त संबंध नहीं है — वे just姐妹-in-law (सسرाल की भाभी/सास related)। However the exact question asks about Aryaan's wife and C.
Aryaan's wife = W, C = B's wife. W and B are siblings. So C is W's brother's wife = W की साली / भभाई (sister-in-law by marriage).
✓ Answer: W और C sisters-in-law हैं (विवाह सम्बन्धी भगिनी)।

Example 4 (Quick puzzle):
प्रश्न: रihan के पिता का एकमात्र पुत्र मोहन है। मोहन की पुत्री रिद्धिमा है। रिद्धिमा का रihan से क्या संबंध है?

Solution:
• Rihan ke pita ka ekmatra putra = Mohan → Mohan is Rihan's father OR Rihan himself (if Rihan is male and only son). "Rihan ke pita ka" = father's only son = Rihan (if Rihan is male) or Rihan's brother (if Rihan has brother). Given "one only son" and Rihan is mentioned as having a father, Rihan IS the only son.
• Mohan = Rihan
• Mohan ki putri = Rihan ki putri = Ridhima is Rihan's daughter.
✓ Answer: कन्या (Daughter)

कूटबध्द Blood Relations (Coded) — strategy और rules

Coding-Decoding के Blood Relations version में अक्षर या चिन्ह दिए जाएँ। इसका systematic approach:

■ Rule 1 — Decode each pair one at a time:
P $ Q → P is father of Q (direction: P ↑ Q)
P # Q → P is mother of Q (direction: P ↑ Q)
P @ Q → P is brother of Q (same generation, P ↔ Q)
P % Q → P is sister of Q (same generation, P ↔ Q)
P & Q → P is husband of Q (P ↔ Q, married)
P * Q → P is wife of Q (P ↔ Q, married)
(These symbols vary per exam — always check the given key.)

■ Rule 2 — Left to Right chaining:
Read expression left to right. Each middle term links two relationships.
Example: A $ B @ C # D
→ A $ B: A is father of B
→ B @ C: B is brother of C (so A is also father of C)
→ C # D: C is mother of D (so A is grandfather of D)

■ Rule 3 — Gender determination:
• If X is father/brother/husband → X is male
• If X is mother/sister/wife → X is female
• If X appears as child/son/daughter without parent label → gender unknown unless stated

■ Rule 4 — Reverse reading for "X is ___ of Y":
If asked "How is Y related to X?", reverse the chain or trace from Y backward.
Example: In A $ B @ C, how is C related to A? A is father of B, B is brother of C → C is daughter or son of A. If C is female → daughter; if male → son. Without further info → either son or daughter.

■ Common coded traps:
• Using same symbol for different relations in different questions (always re-read key)
• Hidden gender switches: "A is brother of B" doesn't tell B's gender
• Extra persons inserted to confuse (A $ B @ C % D — find A to D, skip irrelevant links)
• Negative coded: "A is NOT father of B" — elimination technique needed

अक्सर गलत होने वाले बिंदु और examination traps

Banking exams में Blood Relations से अम्बार candidates गलत उत्तर देते हैं। इन常见 traps को समझकर बचो:

1) "Only son" trap:
• "Rohan is the only son of Mona." → Rohan is male, only child. But Mona could have daughters? No — "only son" means she may have daughters but no other sons. Many students incorrectly think "only son" means "only child."

2) Gender ambiguity trap:
• "A is the parent of B." → A could be father or mother. Unless further info given, you cannot determine A's gender.
• "B is the child of A." → B could be son or daughter.

3) Self-reference trap:
• "This is my mother's only daughter." If speaker is female → it's herself. If male → it's his sister. Students often miss the speaker's gender clue.

4) Spouse swap trap:
• "A is the husband of B's mother." → A is B's father. But if question says "A is the father of B's mother" → A is B's maternal grandfather. Read carefully!

5) Generation skip trap:
• "Grandfather" could be paternal or maternal. If question says "my grandfather" without specifying, either is valid unless context eliminates one.

6) Direction reversal trap:
• Question: "How is A related to B?" vs "How is B related to A?"
• If A is father of B, then B is son/daughter of A. Never mix these up.

7) Marriage vs blood trap:
• In-law relationships (bhabhi, salaa, saali) are MARITAL, not blood. Questions asking "blood relation" will exclude in-laws.
• "Son-in-law" =女儿的丈夫, not daughter's brother.

8) Multi-statement consistency trap:
• When two statements are given (I and II), check if BOTH are needed or either is sufficient individually. This is common in Banking reasoning sets.

Practice-Ready Summary: exam-weightage और final tips

■ Banking में Blood Relations का weightage:
• IBPS PO Prelims: 1-2 questions (approx. 1-2 marks)
• IBPS Clerk Prelims: 1-2 questions
• SBI PO Prelims: 1-2 questions
• SBI Clerk: 1-2 questions
• IBPS RRB PO/Clerk: 1-2 questions
• Total expected time investment for mastery: 4-6 hours of focused practice

■ High-Yield Sub-topics (ज़्यादा पूछे जाते हैं):
1) Pointing-type introduction questions (सबसे ज़्यादा)
2) Coded blood relations (बार-बार)
3) Family tree puzzle integration (sometimes combined with seating arrangement)
4) Gender-trap questions (constant source of errors)

■ Final preparation tips:
1) Practice drawing family trees quickly — use single-letter initials (A, B, C...) and standard symbols. Don't write full names.
2) Memorize the Hindi-English relationship terms because many Banking exams use Hindi medium questions with English options or vice versa.
3) Solve at least 30-40 Blood Relation problems from previous years and mock tests to recognize patterns.
4) For coded types, create your own symbol table and practice decoding reverse (from conclusion to premise).
5) Always double-check: Am I answering "A related to B" or "B related to A"? This reversal mistake costs more marks than any other single error in this topic.
6) In data-sufficiency format (常見 in SBI PO Mains), practice identifying whether Statement I alone, Statement II alone, or both together are sufficient.

■ Recommended practice set size before exam:
Minimum 50 practiced questions covering all 6 pattern types listed above, with a target accuracy of 85%+ and average solving time under 90 seconds per question.

  • Coded Blood Relations में symbol definition हमेशा question के साथ दी जाती है — ignore मत करो।
  • "Only son" का अर्थ "only child" नहीं है — mother के दूसरे बच्चे female हो सकते हैं।
  • Gender ambiguity सबसे बड़ा trap है — parent/child/sibling relationships में gender तब तकUnknown रखो जब तक explicitly ना मिले।
  • A से B का relation पूछें vs B से A का relation पूछें — direction reversal में भ्रम न हो।
  • In-law relationships (bhabhi, salaa, saali, dasaad) blood relations नहीं होते।
  • Coded type में chain को हमेशा left-to-right decode करो, reverse न करो।
