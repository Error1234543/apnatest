Inequality — Banking Reasoning PYQ-STYLE PRACTICE (Part 1 of 6)

BankingHindiPYQ

Banking reasoning inequalities mein do statements di jaati hain jisme symbols (≥, ≤, >, <, =) ka upyog hota hai. Har statement ke baad ek ya do samadhan (conclusions) diye jaate hain. Aapko yeh nishchit karna hai ki kaun sa samadhan statements se tarkik roop se anuprant hota hai ya nahi. Neeche diye gaye symbols ke arth hain: - 'P ≥ Q' ka arth: P, Q se bada ya barabar hai (P ≥ Q) - 'P ≤ Q' ka arth: P, Q se chhota ya barabar hai (P ≤ Q) - 'P > Q' ka arth: P, Q se adhik hai (P > Q) - 'P < Q' ka arth: P, Q se kam hai (P < Q) - 'P = Q' ka arth: P aur Q samान हैं (P = Q) Answer key: Correct option mark karein jo statements ke anusaar nishchit roop se sahī hai. PYQ-STYLE PRACTICE — koi bhi sawal actual previous-year question nahi hai; inhein sirf practice ke liye banaya gaya hai.
