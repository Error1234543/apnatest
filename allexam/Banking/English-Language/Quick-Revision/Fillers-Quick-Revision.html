Fillers — Quick Revision (Banking English)

BankingHindiQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Fillers (Fill in the Blanks) banking English ke wo questions hote hain jahan ek ya do sentences ke beech ka word ya words chhoda gaya hota hai. Student ko di gayi options mein se wo option chunna hota hai jo sentence ke grammatical sense aur meaning dono ke liye sahi ho.
  • Ye questions do tarah ke hote hain: (a) Single Blank Filler — ek sentence mein ek jagah khali, (b) Double/Triple Blank Filler — ek ya do sentences mein multiple jaghein khali, jahan har blank ke liye alag options milte hain.
  • Banking Prelims aur Mains donon mein English Section ka filler-related portion important mana jata hai — yeh Vocabulary + Grammar ke combination ka test hota hai.

Important facts

  • Banking English mein Fillers section usually 5-10 marks ka hota hai — zyadatar single aur double blank type ke.
  • Preposition-verb aur adjective-preposition pairs sabse zyada aate hain: interested in, afraid of, proud of, responsible for, accustomed to, capable of, similar to, different from, connected with, devoted to.
  • Conjunction choice ka rule: 'despite' ke baad noun/gerund, 'although' ke baad full clause (subject+verb). Dono same meaning dete hain lekin grammar alag hoti hai.
  • Articles ka simple rule: pehli baar generic mention = a/an, doosri baar ya specific = the, general plural/uncountable = zero article.
  • Fixed collocations ko sentence ke context se hi solve karo — rote memorization se zyada useful context recognition hota hai.
  • Double blank questions mein pehle blank ko solve karo, fir doosre blank ke options mein se us blank ke saath fit hone wala chuno — sequence matter karta hai.
  • Phrasal verbs ke particle ko yaad rakhna zaroori hai: up (complete/stop), down (reduce/cancel), off (cancel/separate), out (finish/expel), over (finish/control), through (exhaust/inspect).
  • Banking-specific filler context: RBI, inflation, interest rate, liquidity, bailout, fiscal deficit, repo rate, CRR, SLR, GDP growth — ye sab terms fillers mein a sakte hain.

Memory aids

  • Preposition yaad karne ka trick: RAFT — Responsible (for), Afraid (of), Interested (in), Fond (of). Har letter ke saath ek common pair juda hai.
  • Despite/Although ka fark yaad rakho: 'Despite the rain, we went out' vs 'Although it was raining, we went out.' Ek noun ke saath, doosra clause ke saath.
  • Much vs Many ka mantra: 'Many counts, Much amounts' — many=ginne layak (countable), much=matra wali (uncountable).
  • Double blank ka approach yaad rakho: 'First blank fixes the direction, second blank confirms it.' Pehla blank sentence ka mood batata hai, doosra confirm karta hai.
  • Fixed expression yaad karne ke liye mini-story banao: 'Sherlock Holmes always did an ACID TEST on evidence before making a decision.'

Exam tips

  • Time management: Fillers par 1-1.5 minute se zyada mat lagao. Pehle easy blanks solve karo, difficult ko last mein chhodo.
  • Process of elimination: Agar do options same lag rahe hon, to sentence mein kisi ek word ko delete karke dekho — kaunsa option meaning ko sahi banata hai?
  • Context clue hunting: Sentence ke doosre hisson se clues dhoondo — koi adjective, adverb, ya contrasting word filler ka sahi option bata sakta hai.
  • Read the FULL sentence before looking at options: Options ko pehle dekh kar guess mat karo — pehle sentence samjho, phir options mein se match karo.
  • For double/triple blanks: pehla blank fix karo, uske baad doosre ke options dekho. Doosra blank pehle wale ke context se decide ho sakta hai.
  • Vocabulary fillers ke liye daily reading zaroori hai — editorial padhne se natural feel aati hai ki kaunsa word kis jagah fit hoga.
  • Mock tests mein apne wrong filler answers ka reason note karo — usually wahi trap repeat hoti hai dusre question mein.
  • Banking English meinFillers ke liye 'sense of language' banana sabse important hai — grammar rules se zyada natural flow ki feeling kaam aati hai.

Common traps

  • Trap 1: 'Despit' vs 'Although' — kaafi students inhein interchangeably use kar dete hain, lekin grammar alag hai. 'Despit' ke baad noun phrase, 'Although' ke baad clause aata hai.
  • Trap 2: 'Few' vs 'A few', 'Little' vs 'A little' — 'Few/little' negative sense dete hain (almost none), 'a few/a little' positive (some). Iska fark question ka answer badal sakta hai.
  • Trap 3: Preposition confusion — 'Angry with' (person) vs 'Angry at' (thing), 'Listen to' (not listen for), 'Wait for' (not wait till). Ye pairs specifically test hote hain.
  • Trap 4: Article overuse — students kabhi-kabhi har jagah 'the' laga dete hain, jabki general statements mein zero article sahi hota hai.
  • Trap 5: Phrasal verb ke literal meaning par bharosa karna — 'Give up' ka matlab physically up dhakelna nahi, balki 'chhod dena' hai. Idiomatic meaning yaad rakhna zaroori hai.
  • Trap 6: Tense mismatch — sentence mein past tense hai to filler bhi past/relevant tense mein hona chahiye. Kabhi-kabhi 'already', 'yet', 'since', 'for' jaise signal words tense batate hain.
  • Trap 7: Double blank mein order ignore karna — pehla blank jo option chuno, uske hisaab se doosra blank select karna chahiye, randomly donon ko alag-alag nahi dekhna chahiye.
