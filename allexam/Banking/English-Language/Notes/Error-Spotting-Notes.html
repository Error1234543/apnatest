Error Spotting — Banking (हिंदी)

Error Spotting क्या है और Banking में इसका महत्व

Error Spotting एक ऐसा प्रश्न प्रकार है जिसमें एक वाक्य दिया जाता है जिसे चार भागों (A, B, C, D) और एक भाग (E) No Error में विभाजित किया जाता है। उम्मीदवार को यह पहचानना होता है कि वाक्य का कौन-सा भाग व्याकरणिक दृष्टि से त्रुटिपूर्ण है। IBPS, SBI PO/Clerk और अन्य बैंकिंग परीक्षाओं में Error Spotting से 3-5 प्रश्न पूछे जाते हैं, जो कि तेजी से अंक प्राप्त करने वाले प्रश्न होते हैं यदि नियम स्पष्ट हों। यह खंड केवल याद रखने योग्य बातें नहीं, बल्कि व्याकरण की अभ्यास-समझ माँगता है; इसलिए नियमित practice के बिना इस भाग में स्थिर अंक पाना मुश्किल है।

सबसे पहले क्या check करें — Subject-Verb Agreement

यह Banking Error Spotting का सबसे अधिक आने वाला नियम है। नियम सरल हैं पर पकड़ में रखना जरूरी: (1) Singular subject के साथ singular verb और plural subject के साथ plural verb आता है। (2) 'Each, every, either, neither' जैसे single-consideration वाले words के बाद हमेशा singular verb आता है, चाहे subject के साथ कौन-सा modifier आए। जैसे: Each of the boys plays cricket. (NOT play) (3) 'And' से जुड़े दो subjects plural verb लेते हैं, लेकिन जब they कोई single idea दर्शाएं तो singular रह सकता है — bread and butter IS my breakfast. (4) Collective nouns (team, committee, jury, government) context पर depend करते हैं; अगर समूह एक ही action कर रहा है तो singular, अगर सदस्य अलग-अलग काम कर रहे हैं तो plural। जैसे: The jury was divided in opinion. (5) Distance, money, time, weight आदि जब compound unit माने जाएं तो singular verb लेते हैं — Ten miles is a long distance. (6) There is/are के बाद अगला noun verb decide करता है — There is a pen on the table; There are pens on the table. (7) Relative clause में who/which का verb antecedent से match होना चाहिए — The man who lives here is kind.

Tense Consistency और Time Markers

Banking में अक्सर त्रुटि यह मिलती है कि past, present या future की mixing गलत जगह हो जाती है। तीन मुख्य बातें याद रखें: (1) एक ही sentence या paragraph में tense consistent होना चाहिए除非 context change हो। जैसे: He said that he was going. (Reported speech में past के बाद past tense रहता है।) (2) Time markers बहुत महत्वपूर्ण हैं — Yesterday, last week, ago जैसे words present tense के साथ गलत लग सकते हैं: I saw him yesterday (NOT I see him yesterday). (3) Perfect tenses का प्रयोग experience, completed action या duration के लिए होता है — I have lived here since 2010; She has finished her work. (4) Conditionals में tense का matching mandatory है — If I had known, I would have come (Third conditional); If it rains, I will stay home (First conditional); If I were you, I would study (Second conditional). इन सभी में verb form का mismatch ही error spot होने वाला मुख्य कारण बनता है।

Prepositions — सबसे ज्यादा आने वाले गलत उपयोग

Preposition Error Spotting में बहुत common हैं क्योंकि ये rote memorization की मांग करते हैं। सबसे आमतौर पर टेस्ट होने वाले preposition patterns ये हैं: (1) Ability/Inability — good at, fond of, proud of, capable of, afraid of, interested in. (2) Direction/Location — depend on, listen to, wait for, agree with/to, belong to, suffer from. (3) Verb + preposition combinations — accuse of, apologize for, congratulate on, complain about, insist on, object to, succeed in, rely on. (4) Adjective + preposition — responsible for, grateful to/for, aware of, similar to, different from/than, proud of. (5) Common traps: 'According to' सही है, 'According with' गलत है। 'Unlike' का प्रयोग comparison में होता है, 'Dislike' नहीं। 'Except' और 'Except for' में nuanced difference होता है — Except for errors, your essay is good. (6) 'Since' vs 'For' — since point of time के लिए, for duration के लिए: I have been waiting since morning; I have been waiting for two hours.

Articles (a, an, the) और शून्य Article का नियम

Article error अक्सर नज़र से छूट जाता है। ध्यान रखें: (1) 'A' consonant sound से पहले आता है और 'An' vowel sound से — a university (yُ sound), an hour (h silent), an MBA degree, a one-time event. (2) Singular countable noun kabhi bhi article के बिना नहीं आ सकता — I saw a boy (NOT I saw boy). (3) Unitaire/general concept के लिए zero article — Love is blind; Honesty is the best policy. (4) Plural और uncountable nouns general meaning में article के बिना आते हैं — Dogs bark; Money is important. (5) Specific/reference के लिए 'the' — the Ganga, the Himalayas, the Sun, the Internet, the past/future. (6) Musical instruments के सामान्य प्रयोग में 'the' — Play the piano. (7) Meals, subjects, languages में general sense में article नहीं — I had breakfast; She studies English; We studied history. (8) Proper nouns में आमतौर पर article नहीं — India, Mumbai, Rahul, Tuesday. (9) Unique things — the moon, the sky, the environment, the universe.

Parallel Structure, Comparisons और Redundancy

ये तीन नियम एक साथ पढ़ें क्योंकि Banking में ये अक्सर combined error के रूप में आते हैं: (1) Parallel Structure — समान grammatical form का use करना चाहिए जब दो या अधिक items list में हों। जैसे: She likes reading, writing and to draw (गलत); correct: She likes reading, writing and drawing. Similarly: He is smart, hardworking and honest (सभी adjectives — सही)। (2) Comparison के साथ最常见的 error: 'More better', 'most easiest', 'more highest' जैसे double comparative/superlative गलत हैं। बेहतर: better, easiest, highest. (3) When using comparative, 'than' चाहिए — She is taller than me. (4) 'Comparatively more easier' redundant है — comparatively easier सही है। (5) Redundancy/traditional tautology — free gift (gift already free), end result (result is inherently end), basic principle (principle is basic), past history (history implies past), repeat again (repeat means do again). (6) 'Due to' vs 'Because of' — due to = because of, पर due to को verb/be verb के बाद attributively ही use करें: The cancellation was due to rain (सही); Because of rain, the match was cancelled (सही)।

Pronouns, Relative Clauses और Antecedent Agreement

Pronoun-related errors Banking में बार-बार पूछे जाते हैं: (1) Pronoun-antecedent agreement —pronoun singular/plural में अपने antecedent से मेल खाए। Everyone should bring his book (NOT their book在传统 rule में)। However, modern usage increasingly accepts singular 'their'। Banking exams में usually 'his' या 'his or her' preferred होता है। (2) Who vs Whom — who subject है, whom object है — The man who called you is here; The man whom you called is here. (3) That vs Which — restrictive clause में that, non-restrictive में which (comma के साथ) — The book that I bought is good; My car, which is red, is fast. (4) One another vs Each other — two entities के लिए each other, three+ के लिए one another (पर modern usage में interchangeable)। (5) Possessive pronouns — its (possessive) vs it's (it is); their vs there vs they're; your vs you're — ये सभी classic error sources हैं। (6) Reflexive pronouns misuse — Himself came to the office (गलत); He himself came to the office (सही)।

Conditional Sentences, Inversion और Advanced Structures

ये patterns थोड़े advanced हैं पर Banking (विशेषकर SBI PO/Mains) में आ सकते हैं: (1) Conditionals के four types ध्यान से याद रखें — Zero: If you heat water, it boils. First: If it rains, I will stay. Second: If I had money, I would buy. Third: If I had studied, I would have passed. (2) Inversion after negative adverbials — Never have I seen such a sight; Seldom does he come late; Hardly had I reached when it rained; No sooner had he left than it started raining. (3) Subjunctive mood — I suggest that he be present; It is essential that she arrive on time; If I were you, I would... (4) Wish/If only — present unreal: I wish I were rich; past unreal: I wish I had studied harder. (5) Participle clauses — Walking down the street, I met an old friend (subject same होना चाहिए otherwise dangling modifier error)। (6) Appositives — My friend Rohan, a doctor, lives in Delhi. (7) Ellipsis — She can sing and (she) can dance too.

Confusable Words — जो Banking में बार-बार आते हैं

ये शब्द जोड़े exam setters के favorite होते हैं — अक्सर एक word दूसरे word की जगह use हो जाता है: (1) Affect (verb) vs Effect (noun) — The news affected him; The effect was immediate. (2) Lie (lie-lay-lain) vs Lay (lay-laid-laid) — I lie down; I lay down yesterday; I have lain down. (3) Rise (rise-rose-risen) vs Raise (raise-raised-raised) — The sun rises; I raise my hand. (4) Say vs Tell — She said that...; She told me that... (5) Speak vs Talk — He speaks English; They talked about it. (6) Look vs See vs Watch vs Observe — Look = directed attention; See = perception; Watch = observe over time. (7) Between (two) vs Among (three+) — Choose between tea and coffee; Share among the students. (8) Fewer (countable) vs Less (uncountable) — Fewer people; Less water. (9) Could vs Should vs Would — Could = ability possibility; Should = advice; Would = habit/conditional. (10) Certain vs Sure — I am certain that...; I am sure that... (11) Farther (physical) vs Further (metaphorical) — Walk farther; Discuss further.

Error Spotting Solve करने की Technique और Practice Tips

सिर्फ नियम जानना काफी नहीं, apply करना भी जरूरी है। एक effective strategy: (1) पहले पूरा sentence एक बार पढ़ें ताकि整体 sense समझ आए। (2) हर option को check करते समय focus करें — Subject-Verb agreement (सबसे पहले), फिर Tense, फिर Preposition/Article, फिर Comparison/Parallelism, फिर Pronoun/Redundancy। (3) जब कुछ न दिखे तो 'No Error' पर विचार करें, पर पहले सब options thoroughly check करें। (4) Familiar patterns याद रखें — confusable words, preposition collocations, inversion structures आदि। (5) हर mistake को note करें और उस rule को revise करें जो टूटा था। Error log बनाना बहुत helpful होता है। (6) Daily 10-15 questions practice करें — consistency > intensity. (7) Speaking/reading English regularly से intuition better होता है, जिससे error spot करना आसान हो जाता है। (8) Mock tests में Error Spotting section को strictly time-bound practice करें — average 45-60 seconds per question target रखें।

Important facts

  • Each/Every + singular noun = singular verb — Each of the students has a book.
  • Bread and butter IS a meal — दो nouns मिलकर एकเดียว idea दें तो singular verb.
  • There is/are के बाद noun के number से verb match होना चाहिए — There are many reasons.
  • Since + point of time; For + duration — since 2020; for five years.
  • Who subject है, Whom object — The girl who won vs The girl whom I saw.
  • That restrictive clause में, Which non-restrictive (comma के साथ).
  • Never, Seldom, Hardly, No sooner जैसे negative adverbials के साथ inversion होती है.
  • Affect (verb) / Effect (noun); Lie (intransitive)/Lay (transitive); Rise/Raise.
  • Farther = physical distance; Further = metaphorical/degree.
  • Say + that clause; Tell + person + that clause — She said she was ill; She told me she was ill.
  • Due to = because of, पर be verb के बाद attributive position में ही.
  • Redundant expressions avoid करें — free gift, repeat again, end result, basic fundamentals.
  • Collective noun singular है जब group единый行动; plural जब members individually act.
  • Subjunctive: I suggest that he be present; It is vital that she arrive on time.
  • No Error option तभी चुनें जब सभी parts व्याकरणिक रूप से सही साबित हों.

Formulas and relationships

Subject (singular) + Verb(singular); Subject (plural) + Verb(plural) — Basic Agreement Rule
Each/Every + singular noun + singular verb — Example: Each of them is ready.
There is + singular/un­countable; There are + plural — Example: There is hope; There are hopes.
If + past perfect, ... would have + past participle — Third Conditional
If + simple past, ... would + base verb — Second Conditional
If + simple present, ... will + base verb — First Conditional
Never/Seldom/Hardly + auxiliary + subject + main verb — Inversion Pattern
No sooner + had + subject + past participle + than + clause — Inversion
Suggest/Insist/Recommend + that + subject + base verb — Subjunctive
Compare: fewer + countable noun; less + uncountable noun
Between + two; Among + three or more
Like + noun (comparison); As + clause (manner)

Common mistakes

  • Each of the boys play —錯誤：play应为plays。Each singular subject देता है।
  • The list of items are on the table —错误：are应为is。主语是list (singular)।
  • He is good in music —错误：in应为at。Good at is the correct collocation।
  • She is the most richest girl —错误：richest suffixed है, most रिडंडेंट है।Simple superlative suffice करता है।
  • I have living here since 2015 —错误：living应为lived।Present perfect requires past participle।
  • Neither of the two boys have come —错误：have应为has।Neither singular माना जाता है।
  • He told that he was tired —错误：told के बाद direct object (me/him etc.) चाहिए या said use करें।
  • Me and Rahul went there —错误：Rahul and I correct है, क्योंकि subject position में है।
  • The police is coming —错误：police plural noun है, इसलिए are use होगा।
  • My sister and her friend has arrived —错误：has应为have, কারণ compound subject plural है।
  • He works since morning —错误：since morning के साथ present perfect continuous चाहिए — has been working।
  • He is the better of the two brothers — सही pattern: better, NOT best (दो में comparison)।
  • The information are wrong —错误：information uncountable है, इसलिए is use होगा।
  • One of the students who is honest — error: who clause antecedent students (plural) है, इसलिए are चाहिए।
  • Despite of his poverty —错误：Despite alone sufficient है, of नहीं चाहिए। OR use 'In spite of'।
