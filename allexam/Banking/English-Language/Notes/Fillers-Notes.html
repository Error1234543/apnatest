Fillers क्या हैं? (What Are Filler Questions?)

Banking examinations में English Language section का एक बहुत महत्वपूर्ण हिस्सा "Fillers" या "Sentence Completion / Fill in the Blanks" है। इसमें एक वाक्य (या दो-तीन वाक्यों वाला set) दिया जाता है जिसमें एक या अधिक खाली जगहें (blanks) होती हैं। उम्मीदवार को चार विकल्प दिए जाते हैं और सही विकल्प चुनना होता है जो वाक्य के संदर्भ, व्याकरण और शब्दावली की दृष्टि से उपयुक्त हो।

Filler questions तीन मुख्य प्रकार की होती हैं:
- Single Blank Fillers: एक वाक्य में एक खाली जगह।
- Double/Triple Blank Fillers: दो या तीन खाली जगहें, जहाँ सभी blanks के लिए सही set चुनना जरूरी होता है।
- Paragraph Fillers: छोटे पैराग्राफ में कई blanks, जहाँ संदर्भ पुरा करने की कौशल माँगी जाती है।

IBPS PO/Clerk और SBI PO/Clerk दोनों ही इन प्रश्नों को नियमित रूप से पूछते हैं। इस विषय में Vocabulary, Grammar, Collocations, Idioms & Phrases और Contextual Usage सब मिलकर test होते हैं।

Filler Questions के Types और他们在 Banking Exams में उनका Weightage

Banking exams में Fillers के प्रश्न आमतौर पर 5 से 10 के बीच आते हैं (Prelims में कम, Mains में ज़्यादा)। Types इस प्रकार हैं:

1. Vocabulary-based Fillers: किसी blank के लिए सही शब्द चुनना, अक्सर synonyms/antonyms या word usage (correct/incorrect) से जुड़े होते हैं।
2. Grammar-based Fillers: Articles (a/an/the), Prepositions (in/on/at/by/for/etc.), Conjunctions (but/because/although/unless/while), Auxiliaries, Relative Pronouns आदि की सही choice।
3. Collocation-based Fillers: कुछ शब्द आपस में हमेशा साथ आते हैं — make a decision, do homework, pay attention, take care etc. इन collocations की जानकारी जरूरी है।
4. Context/Logic-based Fillers: वाक्य का अर्थ समझकर blank भरना, जैसे contrast, cause-effect, addition, concession आदि दिखाते शब्दों की पहचान।

Tip: Double/triple blank वाले प्रश्नों में सबसे पहले उस blank को देखो जिसका option सबसे स्पष्ट हो, फिर बाकी blanks उसी से match करो। अक्सर एक भी गलत शब्द पूरे set को गलत बना देता है।

Fillers में最常出现的 Grammar Topics — Articles, Prepositions, Conjunctions

Articles (a/an/the):
- 'a' consonant sound के पहले, 'an' vowel sound के पहले (उदा. a university, an hour)।
- 'the' specific/reference के लिए; first time mention पर 'a/an', dubara mention पर 'the'।
- Generic singular plural nouns में article नहीं लगता। "Cats are cute." ठीक; "The cats are cute." तभी जब specific cats हों।
- Superlative degree, unique things (the sun, the earth), rivers/mountains/SEAS के साथ the लगता है।

Prepositions (बहुत महत्वपूर्ण):
- Time: at (specific time — at 5 pm), on (days/dates — on Monday), in (months/years/periods — in July, in 2024, in the morning)।
- Place: at (point), on (surface), in (enclosed space)।
- Important fixed prepositions: depend on, interested in, good at, afraid of, proud of, responsible for, because of, in spite of, on behalf of, with regard to, regarding, concerning。
- Verb + preposition combinations: account for, approve of, belong to, consist of, deal with, listen to, look after, look forward to, object to, refer to, insist on, apologize for。
- Adjective + preposition: acquainted with, aware of, capable of, famous for, familiar with, interested in, proud of, responsible for, satisfied with。

Conjunctions:
- Coordinating (FANBOYS): for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so — equal weight के clauses जोड़ते हैं।
- Subordinating: although/though/even though (concession), because/since/as (reason), if/unless (condition), when/while/as soon as (time), so that/in order that (purpose), provided/providing (condition)。
- Correlative pairs: either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also, both...and, whether...or。
- 'Unless' का अर्थ है 'if not' — Unless you study, you will fail = If you do not study...。
- 'Despite' और 'In spite of' के बाद noun/gerund आता है, clause नहीं (Despite of = गलत)。
- 'Although' के बाद clause आता है, noun phrase नहीं सीधे।

Collocations और Fixed Expressions जो Banking Exams में बार-बार आते हैं

High-frequency collocations ( verbo + noun ):
- take part in, make an effort, keep pace with, catch a glimpse, pay a visit, pay heed/care/attention, bear in mind, meet with success/reception, render assistance, pose a threat/challenge, gain ground/momentum, come to light, draw a conclusion, form an opinion, establish relations, play a role/part, break the ice, break the record, lose heart, lend support, run a risk。

Adjective + noun collocations:
- keen interest, strict rules, fierce competition, heavy traffic, strong argument, valid point, rapid growth, severe congestion, acute shortage, major factor, significant contribution, profound impact, vague idea, clear understanding, deep concern, broad range, full potential, high standard。

Verb + preposition + noun (fixed phrases):
- come up with (idea), fall behind (progress), go through (experience), look into (investigate), put forward (proposal), bring about (change), carry out (survey/research), deal with (problem), face up to (reality), get rid of (doubt), hold on to (hope), make up for (loss), pass away (died), stand for (represent), turn down (reject), wipe out (destroy)。

Idiomatic expressions frequently tested:
- beat around the bush, cost an arm and a leg, kill two birds with one stone, burn the midnight oil, once in a blue moon, miss the boat, cut corners, hit the nail on the head, call it a day, be over the moon, under the weather, a piece of cake, back to square one, bread and butter, by the skin of one's teeth, cry over spilled milk, give someone the benefit of the doubt, have a bone to pick, let the cat out of the bag, make ends meet, see eye to eye, sit on the fence, strike while the iron is hot, take with a pinch of salt。

इन सभी को याद रखने का best तरीका: unbroken sentences में रटो नही — context में USE करो।जब तुम real sentence में देखोगे तो exam में भूलोगे नही।

Solving Strategy: Step-by-Step Method for Filler Questions

Filler question solve करने का systematic approach:

Step 1 — Read the full sentence(s) first। Blank को ignore करो, पूरा meaning समझो।

Step 2 — Identify the type of blank। क्या वहाँ नाम (noun) चाहिए? क्रिया (verb)? विशेषण (adjective)? संयोजक (conjunction)? Preposition? Article?

Step 3 — Look for clue words। Contrast clues: but, however, although, despite, yet, nevertheless, conversely, on the contrary, even though, instead, rather than। Addition clues: and, moreover, furthermore, additionally, also, besides, not only...but also। Cause/Effect clues: because, since, as, therefore, consequently, hence, thus, so, accordingly, as a result, due to, owing to।

Step 4 — Eliminate wrong options first। अगर किसी option में कोई ऐसा शब्द है जो grammatically impossible है (जैसे wrong preposition use हुई हो), use discard करो।

Step 5 — Double/triple blanks में strongest blank से start करो। दो में से एक blank ऐसे हो सकता है जिसके options बहुत restrictive हों — वहाँ से शुरू करो, फिर बाकी match करो।

Step 6 — Final check: blank में answer रखो और whole sentence को aloud padhो। Natural lagani chahiye। यदि sentence strange lage, option galat हो सकता है।

Step 7 — Time management: एक filler पर 45-60 seconds से ज्यादा न लगे। अगर फंस जाओ तो mark करके आगे बढ़ो, end में return करो।

Common Errors and Traps in Filler Questions

Exam में अक्सर ये traps दिए जाते हैं — इन्हें पहचानना जरूरी है:

Trap 1: Similar-looking words का confusion। उदा. affect vs effect, rise vs raise, lay vs lie, sit vs set, advise vs advice, practice vs practise, complement vs compliment, its vs it's, your vs you're, their vs there vs they're, whose vs who's।

Trap 2: Wrong preposition習慣। उदा. "discuss about" गलत है, "discuss" alone सही है। "meet with" != "meet"। "suggest to go" गलत, "suggest going" सही है। "accuse of" सही, "accuse for" गलत।

Trap 3: Subject-verb agreement traps। "Each of the students ___ present." answer है "was", न कि "were" — because subject "Each" singular है।

Trap 4: Conjunction misuse। "Despite of" हमेशा गलत है — শুধু "Despite" या "In spite of" सही है। "Although" और "but" एक साथ न आएं। "Because" और "so" एक साथ न आएं (Indian English में यह common mistake है)।

Trap 5: Article traps। "An United States" गलत है क्योंकि 'United' का sound consonant /j/ से शुरू होता है → "A United States" सही है। "Honest man" में 'h' silent है → "An honest man" सही है।

Trap 6: Redundancy / double meaning। "Return back" गलत, "return"本身就是 वापस आना। "End result" redundant है (result本身就 끝)。"Free gift" redundant。「consist of」और「be made up of」= same meaning, don't mix。

Trap 7: One option looks correct but doesn't fit ALL blanks। Especially in double/triple blanks — all blanks must match simultaneously。

In filler questions, recognizing these words in context can quickly eliminate wrong options। For example, "The RBI decided to _____ interest rates to control inflation" — answer would be "raise/increase", not "lower/decrease"। Direction of policy matters।

Practise Techniques: How to Master Fillers for Banking Exams

Fillers में proficiency पाने के लिए ये practical steps follow करो:

1. Daily 10 new words सीखो + their collocations। अकेला word मत रटो — "take responsibility", "bear responsibility"这样的搭配 memorize करो。

2. Weekly one error log बनाओ — जिन filler questions में तुम गलत हुए, उन्हें note करो और reason लिखो कि क्यों गलत हुआ।

3. Previous Year Papers solve करो — IBPS PO, SBI PO, IBPS Clerk, SBI Clerk के पिछले 5-7 वर्षों के papers से fillers निकालो और unacademy/vidhyasagar/official sources से compare करो।

4. Read editorials (The Hindu, Indian Express) — editorial में complex sentences मिलेंगे जिनमें prepositions, conjunctions, relative clauses properly used हुए हैं। इससे contextual sense मजबूत होगा。

5. Mock tests में filler section को separate track में attempt करो — पहले comfortable questions, फिर tough ones留到最后。

6. Speed drill: 10 filler questions को 8 मिनट में solve करने का目标 रखो।

7. Word families सीखो — e.g., decide → decision, decisive, decisively, indecisive → इससे blank मेंどの形式 चाहिए पता चलता है。

8. Confusable pairs का dedicated list बनाओ और daily revise करो。

9. Collocation dictionaries जैसी चीज़ें use करो (online free resources available) — "what goes with what" देखो。

10. Self-test method: खुद filler questions बनाओ — एक paragraph likho और उसमें 5 blanks place करो, phir answer key banao। इससे出题er ka mindset samajh mein aayega।

Quick Revision Cheat Sheet for Fillers

Articles Quick Rules:
- a + consonant sound, an + vowel sound
- University, European, one-sided → 'a' (sound /j/)；hour, honour, heir → 'an' (silent h)
- the + superlative, the + ordinal, the + unique事物
- Generic plural/uncountable = no article；Specific = the

Preposition Quick List (Banking Frequently Tested):
- account of, afraid of, asleep at, aware of, because of, capable of, composed of, consistent with, devoted to, engaged in, familiar with, fond of, grateful for, incapable of, interested in, jealous of, liable to, opposite to/against, proud of, renowned for, responsible for, suitable for, thanks to, unaware of, worse than

Conjunction Quick Guide:
- However/though/even though → concession
- Although + clause ; Despite/In spite of + noun/gerund
- Unless = if not
- Neither...nor ; Either...or ; Not only...but also ; Both...and
- So...that ; Such...that

Common Confusables:
- Affect (verb) vs Effect (noun)
- Rise (intransitive) vs Raise (transitive)
- Lay (put something down) vs Lie (recline)
- Further (degree) vs Farther (physical distance)
- Less (uncountable) vs Fewer (countable)
- Might of vs May have (modal perfect)
- Suppose/Suppose that → followed by base form/should

Important facts

  • Filler questions in Banking English test Vocabulary + Grammar + Collocations + Context together.
  • Double/triple blank fillers require ALL blanks to match — one wrong eliminates the whole option.
  • Despite/In spite of + noun/gerund (NOT clause). Although + clause.
  • Unless = If not. Do NOT use 'but' with 'although'.
  • 'Discuss' takes direct object — 'discuss about' is incorrect.
  • 'Suggest' is followed by gerund or that-clause, not infinitive: suggest going / suggest that he go.
  • 'A' before consonant SOUND, not letter: a university, a one-time offer. 'An' before vowel SOUND: an hour, an MBA.
  • 'Less' for uncountable nouns; 'Fewer' for countable plural nouns.
  • Rise (intransitive) vs Raise (transitive): Prices rose. He raised his hand.
  • Lay (put something down) needs object; Lie (recline) does not. Laid vs Lain vs Lied.
  • Common trap: 'Despite of' is always WRONG. Correct: 'Despite rain' or 'In spite of rain'.
  • In banking context: NPA, CRR, SLR, Repo Rate, CPI, GDP are frequent vocabulary items.
  • Key verb patterns: accuse OF, apologize FOR, consist OF, belong TO, depend ON, insist ON, succeed IN, participate IN, suffer FROM, prefer TO, object TO, react TO, respond TO.
  • Parallel structure after correlative conjunctions: not only X but also Y — X and Y must be same grammatical form.
  • Reading editorials daily improves contextual filler performance more than rote memorization.
