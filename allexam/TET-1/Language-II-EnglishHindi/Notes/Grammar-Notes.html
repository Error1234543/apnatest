Section 1: Parts of Speech — Noun (નામ) અને તેના પ્રકારો

Part of Speech ના પહેલા અને સૌથી મૂળભૂત ભાગ Noun છે. Noun એટલે કોઈ પણ વ્યક્તિ, સ્થળ, વસ્તુ અથવા ભાવનું નામ. TET-1 માં Noun નીચે મુજબના પ્રકારોમાં વહેંચાયેલો છે:

• Common Noun (સામાન્ય નામ): કોઈ પણ સામાન્ય વ્યક્તિ/સ્થળ/વસ્તુનું નામ. ઉદા: boy, city, table.
• Proper Noun (વિશેષ નામ): ચોક્કસ વ્યક્તિ, સ્થળ કે વસ્તુનું નામ. ઉદા: Ram, Ahmedabad, Ganga. Proper Noun દરેક વખતે Capital Letter થી શરૂ થાય છે.
• Collective Noun (સંહિતાબોધક નામ): એક જ શબ્દથી વ્યક્તિઓના સમૂહનું બોધ થાય. ઉદા: team (ટીમ), flock (પંખીઓનો ગોળો), army (સેના), jury (વિક્ત/જ્યુરୀ).
• Abstract Noun (અભાવિક નામ): દૃશ્યમાન નહીં પરંતુ મનમાં અનુભવાતો ભાવ, ગુણ, અવસ્થા. ઉદા: honesty, love, happiness, courage.
• Material Noun (દ્રવ્યબોધક નામ): જે વસ્તુઓના બનેલી હોય તેનું સામગ્રી-નામ. ઉદા: gold, wood, steel, milk.
• Countable Noun (ગણનાયોગ્ય નામ): જેને આપણે ગણી શકીએ એવા. ઉદા: one book, two chairs. Countable Noun singular/plural બંને રૂપ ધરાવે છે.
• Uncountable Noun (અગણનાયોગ્ય નામ): જેને સીધું ગણી શકાય નહીં, માત્ર પ્રમાણ/સામગ્રી રૂપે. ઉદા: water, sugar, knowledge, advice. Uncountable Noun સામાન્ય રીતે Singular Verb લે છે.

Singular → Plural બનાવવાના નિયમો (મહત્વના):
• સામાન્ય રીતે s ઉમેરો: book→books, pen→pens.
• -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o અંતમાં es: bus→buses, box→boxes, hero→heroes.
• y બદલાઈને ies: baby→babies, country→countries.
• -f/-fe → -ves: leaf→leaves, wife→wives, life→lives. (કેટલાક બાકાત: roof→roofs, chief→chiefs, belief→beliefs).
• Anomalies (અસામાન્ય): man→men, woman→women, foot→feet, tooth→teeth, child→children, mouse→mice, sheep→sheep, deer→deer, fish→fish.
• Foreign plurals: phenomenon→phenomena, criterion→criteria, analysis→analyses, datum→data, thesis→theses, basis→bases, axis→axes, oasis→oases.

Exam tip: TET-1 માં Common/Material/Abstract/Collective Noun વચ્ચેનો તફાવત અને Uncountable Noun સાથે સંબંધિત Subject-Verb Agreement ના પ્રશ્નો બહુ લોકપ્રિય છે.

Section 2: Pronoun (સર્વનામ), Adjective (વિશેષણ) અને Article (વિધેય)

Pronoun (સર્વનામ):
Pronoun એવો શબ્દ જે Noun ની જગ્યાએ આવે છે. પ્રકારો:
• Personal Pronoun: I, you, he, she, it, we, they (subject form); me, him, her, us, them (object form); my/mine, your/your’s, his, her/hers, its, our/ours, their/theirs (possessive).
• Reflexive Pronoun: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves.
• Reciprocal Pronoun: each other, one another.
• Demonstrative Pronoun: this, that, these, those.
• Relative Pronoun: who, whom, whose, which, that.
• Indefinite Pronoun: someone, anybody, everyone, nothing, all, some, many, few, several.
• Interrogative Pronoun: who, whom, whose, which, what.

Adjective (વિશેષણ):
Adjective Noun বা Pronoun નો ગુણ/ભાવ/પ્રમાણ વર્ણવે છે. પ્રકારો:
• Descriptive: good, tall, red.
• Quantitative: many, little, enough, several.
• Demonstrative: this, that, such.
• Possessive: my, your, his, their.
• Distributive: each, every, either, neither.
• Interrogative: which, what, whose.
Comparative અને Superlative degree:
• One syllable: fast–faster–fastest.
• Two syllables ending in -y: happy–happier–happiest.
• Two+ syllables: beautiful–more beautiful–most beautiful.
• Irregular: good–better–best, bad–worse–worst, much–more–most, little–less–least, far–farther/further–farthest/furthest.

Section 3: Verb — Tenses, Agreement અને Modal Help

Verb એ_sentence_ માં _action_ અથવા _state_ વર્ણવે છે. TET-1 માં _Tense,_ _Subject–Verb Agreement_ અને _Modal Verbs_ મહત્વપૂર્ણ છે.

Ten Tenses નો સરળ ભાગ:
સામાન્ય રીતે Ten Tenses ને ત્રણ કાળ (Present/Past/Future) અને ચાર aspect (Simple/Continuous/Perfect/Perfect Continuous) ના સંગઠનથી સમજી શકાય.

1. Present Indefinite (Simple Present): હવેની સામાન્ય આદત/સત્ય.
Pattern: V1 / V1+s (he/she/it singular subject સાથે)
ਉદા: He plays cricket. The sun rises in the east.
ધ્યાન રાખો: He/she/it + verb + s/es. plural subject સાથે base verb.

2. Present Continuous: હવે ચાલુ થતું કામ.
Pattern: am/is/are + V1+ing
ઉદા: She is reading a book.

3. Present Perfect: બનેલું કામ, પરિણામ હવે લાગુ.
Pattern: have/has + V3
ઉદા: I have finished my work.

4. Present Perfect Continuous: શરૂ થયું અને હવે સુધી ચાલુ.
Pattern: have/has + been + V1+ing
ઉદા: She has been working since morning.

5. Past Indefinite: વીતેલા સમયમાં થયું.
Pattern: V2
ઉદા: He went there yesterday.

6. Past Continuous: વીતેલા સમયમાં ચાલુ.
Pattern: was/were + V1+ing
ઉદા: They were playing when I reached.

7. Past Perfect: એક વીતેલા કામની પહેલાં બીજું થયું.
Pattern: had + V3
ઉદા: The train had left before I arrived.

8. Past Perfect Continuous: વીતેલા સમયમાં ચાલુ થઈને કોઈ બીજા કામની પહેલાં રોકાઈ.
Pattern: had + been + V1+ing
ઉદા: He had been sleeping for two hours when the bell rang.

9. Future Indefinite: ભવિષ્યમાં થશે.
Pattern: will/shall + V1
ઉદા: I will help you.

10. Future Perfect Continuous:
Pattern: will have been + V1+ing
ઉદા: By next year, she will have been teaching for ten years.

Subject–Verb Agreement ના મુખ્ય નિયમો:
• Singular subject + singular verb; plural subject + plural verb.
• He/She/It (Present) + s/es; I/You/We/They + base verb.
• Each/every/somebody/noone જેવા indefinite singular subject સાથે singular verb: Everyone has come. Nobody knows.
• And ની જોડણી સામાન્ય રીતે plural verb આપે: Ram and Shyam are coming. (એક જ વિચાર/વ્યક્તિ દર્શાવતી જોડણી જ pure singular verb આપે: Bread and butter is my breakfast. The thief and robber is one man.)
• Either/or, Neither/nor, Not only/but also – verb nearest subject સાથે match થાય: Neither the boys nor the teacher is coming.
• A number of + plural noun = plural verb; The number of + plural noun = singular verb.
• Units of time/money/distance જ્યારે એકંદર રકમ સૂચવે ત્યારે singular: Five rupees is enough. Ten kilometres is a long walk.
• Everyone, somebody, nobody, anyone જેવા singular indefinite pronoun સાથે singular verb: Someone is waiting.

Modal Verbs:
Can/could, may/might, will/would, shall/should, must, ought to, need, dare.
• Modals બાદ base verb આવે (to નહીં): He can swim. She should study. You must obey.
• May/Might permission possibility બતાવે છે; Must compulsion; Ought to moral duty; Could past ability possibility; Would习惯/ polite request.
• Should have + V3 = past regret/unrealised expectation: You should have informed me.
• Must not = prohibition; Need not = absence of obligation: You need not come tomorrow.

Exam tip: TET-1 માં tense transition, error identification (wrong tense/verb form) અને modal choice સાચવવાના પ્રશ્નો વારંવાર પૂછાય છે.

Section 4: Voice (કારક) — Active અને Passive

Voice એટલે _કર્તા_ (subject) action કરે છે કે action કરાવે છે તેનું દર્શન. Two voices હોય છે: Active (કર્તૃવાચ્ય) અને Passive (કર્મકર્તૃક). TET-1 માં transformation rules નીચે મુજબ છે:

Transformation ના સામાન્ય નિયમો:
• Object → Subject; Subject → by + object (-agent).
• Main verb V3 રૂપમાં passive માં રહે છે.
• Tense ને અનુસરીને be verb બદલાય છે:
– Present Indefinite → is/am/are + V3
– Present Continuous → is/am/are + being + V3
– Present Perfect → is/am/are + been + V3
– Past Indefinite → was/were + V3
– Past Continuous → was/were + being + V3
– Past Perfect → had + been + V3
– Future Indefinite → will/would + be + V3
– Future Perfect → will/would + have + been + V3

Important examples:
• Active: She writes a letter. Passive: A letter is written by her.
• Active: They are building a house. Passive: A house is being built by them.
• Active: He has finished the job. Passive: The job has been finished by him.
• Active: The cat killed the mouse. Passive: The mouse was killed by the cat.
• Active: I shall finish it. Passive: It shall be finished by me.

જે Passive માં નથી બનતી:
• Intransitive verbs જેનું object ન હોય: He sleeps. (No passive)
• Heads/I etc. જેમાં object ન હોય.
• Certain idiomatic/intransitive use cases.
• Some modal constructions જ્યાં meaning changes (must = must be done / cannot but do).

Special passive patterns:
• Have/get something done (causative): I had my hair cut.
• Let → be + V3: Let him go. → He be allowed to go (કમ્પ્લેટર passive નહીં; સામાન્ય રીતે TET માં allow/permit use થાય).
• Bid/order/force/request જેવા verbs માટે pattern: I was ordered to leave.

Exam tip: Passive transformation માં tense નીચે be verb બદલાવો એ સૌથી મોટી ભૂલ છે. દરેક tense માં be verb નીચે યાદ રાખો: Simple present → is/am/are; Continuous → being; Perfect → been.

Section 5: Narration (આઘાતકથન) — Direct અને Indirect

Direct Speech: વક્તાની અક્ષરશઃ વાત કાવડમાં (quotes) દર્શાવવી.
Indirect (Reported) Speech: વક્તાની વાત paraphrase માં, quotes વિના, સામાન્ય રીતે clause દ્વારા દર્શાવવી.

Basic shift rules:
• Reporting verb present/future: tense shift થતું નથી.
• Reporting verb past: tense shift થાય છે.

Tense shift (when reporting verb is past):
• Present Indefinite → Past Indefinite: He says, "I play." → He said that he played.
• Present Continuous → Past Continuous: "I am reading." → He said that he was reading.
• Present Perfect → Past Perfect: "I have come." → He said that he had come.
• Present Perfect Continuous → Past Perfect Continuous: "I have been working." → He said that he had been working.
• Past Indefinite → Past Perfect: "I went." → He said that he had gone.
• Past Continuous → Past Perfect Continuous: "I was walking." → He said that he had been walking.
• Past Perfect / Past Perfect Continuous → unchanged.
• Future Indefinite (will) → conditional (would): "I will help." → He said that he would help.
• would/could/might → unchanged.

Pronoun shift (સામાન્ય નિયમ):
• First person (I/we) → reporting subject ના અનુસાર.
• Second person (you) → reported object ના અનુસાર.
• Third person → બદલાતું નથી.

Time/Place words shift:
• now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, yesterday → the day before, here → there, this → that, these → those, ago → before, latterday → henceforth.

Sentence types in Reported Speech:
• Statement → that + clause: He said that he was ill.
• Question → asked + if/whether (Yes/No) કે_wh-word_+ clause (Wh- question):
– "Are you coming?" → He asked if I was coming.
– "Where do you live?" → He asked where I lived.
• Command/Request → told/asked/order + object + to_infinitive:
– "Sit down." → He told me to sit down.
• Exclamation → exclaimed with joy/sorrow + that + clause:
– "Alas! I am ruined." → He exclaimed with sorrow that he was ruined.
• Optative/Benediction → wished + that/clause:
– "Long live the nation." → He wished long live the nation.

Direct → Indirect માં ધ્યાન રાખવાની વાતો:
• Report verb માં tense નીચે shift બદલાઈ શકે છે: says → said.
• Question word order નો બદલાય છે: "Where are you?" → He asked where I was. (પ્રશ્નાર્થી ક્રમ નહીં, declarative ક્રમ).
• Must → had to (જરૂરિયાત); Should → should (unchanged).

Exam tip: TET-1 માં direct-indirect transformation માં tense shift, pronoun shift અને question word order ત્રણેય ટ્રેપ સામાન્ય રીતે આપવામાં આવે છે.

Section 6: Conjunctions (સંયોજક), Prepositions (સાધક) અને Interjections (વિસ્મયબોધક)

Conjunctions (સંયોજક):
Conjunction બે શબ્દો/વાક્યોને જોડે છે. પ્રકારો:
• Coordinating: for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so (FANBOYS). Same grammatical status ધરાવતા ઘટકોને જોડે છે.
– I came but I could not enter.
– He studied hard yet he failed.
• Subordinating: because, although, though, while, if, unless, until, since, as, when, wherever, whenever, provided that, in order that, so that.
– Although he is poor, he is honest.
– I will go if you come.
• Correlative pairs: either…or, neither…nor, not only…but also, whether…and, so…that, such…that.
– Neither Ram nor Shyam is here.
– Not only did he sing, but he also danced.

Common error traps:
• Either/or ની સાથે verb nearest subject સાથે match થાય.
• Not only…but also માં inversion: Not only did he… (past simple માં).
• Because कारण સાથે so નો ઉપયોગ સામાન્ય રીતે ભૂલપૂર્વક થાય છે. Use either because કે because…so નું જોડાણ ન કરવું (બ્રિટિશ/American both માં correct usage differs, પણ TET માં because alone use recommended).

Prepositions (સાધક):
Preposition noun/pronoun/gerund સાથે relation દર્શાવે છે.
Common prepositions: in, on, at, by, with, without, under, above, between, among, beside, beyond, during, except, for, from, inside, outside, off, over, through, towards, upon, within, against, around, before, after, across, along, amid, amidst.

Fixed/important collocations:
• Afraid of, angry with (person)/at (situation), ask for, away from, because of, capable of, proud of, responsible for, superior to, inferior to, equal to, similar to, different from, interested in, devoted to, accustomed to, look forward to, used to, prefer…to, devote oneself to, object to, lead to, contribute to, cater to, turn to.
• In – month/year (in 2024, in January), inside container; On – day/surface (on Monday, on the table); At – specific point/time (at 5 pm, at the door).
• Between – two objects; Among – more than two.
• Since – start point (since 2010); For – duration (for two years).

Interjections (વિસ્મયબોધક):
શબ્દ કે જે ચોક્કસ ભાવ દર્શાવે છે: Alas! (શોક), Ah! (આનંદ/વિસ્મય), Bravo! (અભિનંદન), Oh! (વિસ્મય), Hurrah! (ઉત્સાહ), O! (સંબોધન), Wow! (આશ્ચર્ય).
Punctuation સાથે interjection બાદ comma અથવા exclamation mark આવે છે.

Exam tip: Preposition choice માં fixed phrases યાદ રાખો; Conjunction માં correlative pairs ના verb agreement અને structure ધ્યાન રાખો.

Section 7: Clauses, Phrases અને Sentence Types

Clause vs Phrase:
• Clause: Minimum subject + verb ધરાવતું group. બે પ્રકાર: Independent (main) અને Dependent (subordinate).
• Phrase: Subject/verb વગરનું group, single grammatical function. ઉદા: in the morning, a book of poems, very happy.

Types of Clauses:
• Noun clause: sentence માં noun ની જેમ કામ કરે છે.主语/કર્મ/કર્મકારક દર્શાવે છે.
– What he said is true. (Subject)
– I know that he is honest. (Object)
– The question is whether he will come. (Complement)
• Adjective (Relative) clause: Noun નો ગુણ વર્ણવે છે. Relative pronoun વાપરે છે.
– The boy who won the race is my friend.
– This is the house where I was born.
– The girl whom you met is my sister.
– Note: Whom = object case; Who = subject case; Which = thing/animal; That = people/thing (informal/general);
• Adverb clause: When, where, why, how, because, although, if, unless, till, since, as, so that જેવા subordinating conjunction થી શરૂ થાય છે.
– I will come when you invite me.
– Though he is rich, he is honest.
– He studies so that he may succeed.

Main sentence types:
• Simple sentence: એક main clause; no dependent clause.
• Compound sentence: બે અથવા વધુ independent clauses coordinatıng conjunction થી જોડાયેલા.
• Complex sentence: એક main + ઓછામાં ઓછો એક subordinate clause.
• Compound-complex: બે independent + ઓછામાં ઓછો એક dependent.

Punctuation ની મૂળભૂત નિયમો:
• Full stop (.): Statement, imperative, polite request.
• Question mark (?): Direct question.
• Comma (,): List, after introductory phrase/clause, before coordinating conjunction joining independent clauses, non-restrictive relative clause.
• Semicolon (;): બે closely related independent clauses જોડવા.
• Colon (:): Explanation, list, quotation પહેલાં.
• Apostrophe (‘): Possession (Rama’s book); contraction (don’t, it’s). Its = possessive; It’s = it is.
• Quotation marks (“ ” / ‘ ’): Direct speech, title, special emphasis.

Exam tip: Clause type identification માં clue = relative pronoun/conjunction જેના બાદ subject+verb હોય તો નક્કી કરો. Non-restrictive relative clause માં comma વપરાય છે, restrictive માં નહીં.

Section 8: Errors, Idioms, Transformation અને Revision Checklist

Common Error Patterns (TET-1 માટે):
• Subject–Verb Agreement ભૂલો:
– Each of the boys have… (错) → Each of the boys has… (સાચું)
– The number of students are… (错) → The number of students is… (સાચું)
– Neither Ram nor the boys is… (错) → Neither Ram nor the boys are… (સાચું)
• Tense mismatch: "He goes there yesterday." (错) → "He went there yesterday."
• Wrong preposition: "He is good in maths." (错) → "He is good at maths."
• Double comparison: "more better", "most best" → ભૂલ. Correct: better, best.
• Wrong article: "He is an honourable man." (honourable starts with vowel sound) → સાચું; "an hour" → સાચું; "a European" → સાચું (y_sound).
• Ironic/incorrect modals: "You must to come." (错) → "You must come."
• Confusion between its/it’s, their/there/they’re, affect/effect, loose/lose, principal/principle.
• Redundancy: "Free gift", "end result", "future plans", "repeat again" → ભૂલ. Use just gift/result/repeat/future plan.
• Split infinitive: "to boldly go" → colloquial; formal writing માં avoid.
• Dangling modifier: "Walking down the street, the trees looked beautiful." (错) → "Walking down the street, I saw the trees."

Sentence transformation types:
• Affirmative ↔ Negative (double negative → affirmative): I never dislike him. = I like him very much.
• Positive ↔ Comparative/Superlative: He is the best player in the class. → No other player is as good as him in the class.
• Simple ↔ Complex ↔ Compound: He is too weak to work. → He is so weak that he cannot work.
• Exclamatory ↔ Assertive: What a beautiful flower it is! → It is a beautiful flower.
• Direct ↔ Indirect (ઉપર જુઓ).

Gerund vs Infinitive:
• Gerund (-ing) નું કામ: after preposition, after certain verbs (enjoy, avoid, finish, miss, practice, suggest, consider, admit, deny, keep, risk):
– I enjoy swimming. She avoided meeting him. He suggested going early.
• Infinitive (to + V1) નું કામ: after certain verbs (want, hope, wish, decide, agree, refuse, promise, offer, learn, manage, pretend, fail):
– I want to go. He promised to help. She refused to lie.
• Bare infinitive (વગર to): let, make, have ( causative ), help (optional to), see/hear/feel (perception):
– Let him go. She made me cry. I saw him run.

Punctuation and capitalisation quick rules:
• Proper nouns, names, beginning of sentence, titles (Mr, Dr, Mrs, Ms), days/months/seasons, languages, nationalities capitalize.
• Quote punctuation: Full stop/comma stays inside quotation marks in US style; outside in UK style. TET માં general standard follow કરો.

Important facts

  • Noun છે સામાન્ય નામ (Common), વિશેષ નામ (Proper), સંહિતાબોધક (Collective), અભાવિક (Abstract), દ્રવ્યબોધક (Material), ગણનાયોગ્ય (Countable), અગણનાયોગ્ય (Uncountable).
  • Pronoun ના 7+ પ્રકાર: Personal, Reflexive, Reciprocal, Demonstrative, Relative, Indefinite, Interrogative.
  • Adjective ના 7 પ્રકાર; Comparative/Superlative માં -er/-est કે more/most; Irregular: good–better–best, bad–worse–worst, much–more–most, little–less–least.
  • Ten Tenses: Present/Past/Future × Simple/Continuous/Perfect/Perfect Continuous. Present简单 માં he/she/it + s/es.
  • Subject–Verb Agreement માં Singular subject + singular verb; Each/every/somebody = singular; Either/or નીચે nearest subject; A number of = plural, The number of = singular.
  • Active–Passive માં Object → Subject, main verb V3, be verb tense अनुसार बदलते ਹੈ. Continuous માં being, Perfect માં been.
  • Direct–Indirect માં Reporting verb past হলে Tense shift: Present → Past, Past → Past Perfect, Will → Would. Questions માં word order declarative બને છે.
  • Conjunction types: Coordinating (FANBOYS), Subordinating (because, although, if, when…), Correlative (either…or, neither…nor, not only…but also).
  • Preposition collocations એ fix છે: good at, proud of, responsible for, superior to, look forward to, be used to. Between two, Among many.
  • Clause: subject+verb ધરાવતું group. Independent(main)+Dependent(subordinate). Types: Noun, Adjective(Relative), Adverb.
  • Sentence types: Simple(1 main), Compound(≥2 independent), Complex(1 main + ≥1 dependent), Compound-complex.
  • Error spotting常见patterns: double comparison(more better), article misuse(a/an), wrong preposition(good in→good at), redundant expressions(free gift).
  • Gerund(-ing) after preposition અને specific verbs(enjoy, avoid, finish); Infinitive(to+V1) after specific verbs(want, decide, promise). Bare infinitive after let/make/have/help/perception verbs.
  • Idioms commonly tested: bolt from the blue, beat around the bush, break the ice, spill the beans, once in a blue moon, spare the rod.
  • Transformation types: Affirmative↔Negative, Positive↔Comparative/Superlative, Simple↔Complex, Direct↔Indirect, Exclamatory↔Assertive.

Formulas and relationships

Tense = Tense-marker(form/aspect) + Main Verb(root/V1/V2/V3) + Auxiliary(is/am/are, was/were, have/has/had, will/shall) — દરેક tense માં auxiliary + participle pattern ફરજિયાત યાદ રાખો.
Active→Passive = Object + appropriate form of 'be' + V3 + (by + Agent). Continuous માં be+being+V3; Perfect માં be+been+V3.
Degree conversion: Positive = as + adj + as; Comparative = adj-er / more + adj + than; Superlative = the + adj-est / the most + adj.
Subject–Verb Agreement rule: Singular subject ↔ singular verb; Plural subject ↔ plural verb. Nearest-subject rule for either/or, neither/nor, not only/but also.
Reported speech tense shift (reporting verb past): Present→Past, Past→Past Perfect, Future(will)→Conditional(would). Questions: retain wh-word, change word order to declarative.
Correlative conjunction subject–verb agreement: verb agrees with the noun/pronoun nearest to it(either the boys or the teacher IS; neither the teacher nor the boys ARE).
Preposition after adjective pattern: [adjective] + [preposition] + [noun/gerund]. Fixed collocations must be memorised as whole units(good at, afraid of, proud of).
Gerund vs Infinitive selection: after preposition → gerund; after certain verbs → infinitive(bare or to). Perception/causative verbs take bare infinitive(I saw him run).
Transformations pattern: Affirmative↔Negative(double negative=affirmative); Positive↔Comparative(Superlative); Simple↔Complex(replace relative/adverb clause).
Punctuation-basic mapping: full-stop(statement); question-mark(question); comma(list, introductory clause, non-restrictive relative); semicolon(close related independent clauses); colon(explanation/list).

Common mistakes

  • He/she/it સહિત singular subject સાથે base verb(વગર s/es) વાપરવા. Correct: He plays, She goes, It runs.
  • Double comparison વાપરવું: more better, most best, very better — બધા ખોટા. Correct: better, best.
  • Either/or, neither/nor માં verb nearest subject સાથે match ન કરવો. Correct: Neither the girls nor the boy IS here.
  • A number of vs The number of ગૂંચવી જવું. A number of students ARE…; The number of students IS…
  • Article misuse: a vs an માં sound-based choice ન કરવું. Honourable, hour → an; university, European → a.
  • Preposition_fixed_collocation ને ભૂલવી: good_in→good_at, afraid_of, proud_of, interested_in, responsible_for.
  • Direct–Indirect માં question word order બદલાવાની ગફલત: "Where are you?" → "He asked where were you"(ખોટું) → "He asked where I was"(સાચું).
  • Passive transformation માં be verb નું tense match ન કરવું: Present continuous માં is/am/are+being+V3; Past perfect માં had+been+V3.
  • Each/every/somebody/nobody જેવા indefinite singular subject સાથે plural verb વાપરવો. Correct: Everyone HAS, Nobody KNOWS.
  • Idiom literal અર્થ લેવો: 'break the ice' એટલે બરફ તોડવો નહીં, 'સંભ્રમ તોડવો'. Idiom meanings literal ન લેવા.
  • Redundant expressions: free gift, end result, repeat again, future plans — redundant word દૂર કરવા.
  • Split infinitive formal writing માં avoid ન કરવું. Better: to boldly go → boldly to go / to go boldly.
  • Dangling modifier: sentence શરૂઆતમાં participle phrase નો subject main clause સાથે match ન થાય. Correct: Walking down the street, I saw…
  • Its vs It’s, their vs there vs they’re, affect vs effect, loose vs lose — spelling/usage confusion.
