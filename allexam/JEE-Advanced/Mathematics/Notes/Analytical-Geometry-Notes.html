Fundamentals of Coordinate Geometry & Straight Lines

['Analytical geometry studies geometric figures using coordinate systems. The Cartesian plane uses perpendicular axes OX and OY. Key tools: distance formula d = √[(x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²]; section formula for point dividing join of (x₁,y₁) and (x₂,y₂) internally in ratio m:n is ((mx₂+nx₁)/(m+n), (my₂+ny₁)/(m+n)); externally gives ((mx₂-nx₁)/(m-n), (my₂-ny₁)/(m-n)). Centroid of triangle with vertices (x₁,y₁), (x₂,y₂), (x₃,y₃) is ((x₁+x₂+x₃)/3, (y₁+y₂+y₃)/3). Incenter, circumcenter, orthocenter are important triangle centers.', 'Equation of a straight line in various forms: (i) Point-slope: y - y₁ = m(x - x₁) where m is slope; (ii) Two-point form: (y - y₁)/(x - x₁) = (y₂ - y₁)/(x₂ - x₁); (iii) Slope-intercept: y = mx + c; (iv) Intercept form: x/a + y/b = 1; (v) Normal form: x cosα + y sinα = p where p is perpendicular distance from origin; (vi) General form: Ax + By + C = 0 with slope = -A/B. Parametric form: (x,y) = (x₁ + r cosθ, y₁ + r sinθ) where r is distance from (x₁,y₁).', 'Angle between two lines with slopes m₁, m₂: tanθ = |(m₁ - m₂)/(1 + m₁m₂)|. If lines are a₁x+b₁y+c₁=0 and a₂x+b₂y+c₂=0, then cosθ = |a₁a₂+b₁b₂|/√(a₁²+b₁²)√(a₂²+b₁²). Condition for perpendicularity: m₁m₂ = -1 or a₁a₂ + b₁b₂ = 0. Condition for parallelism: m₁ = m₂ or a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂.', 'Distance from point (x₁,y₁) to line Ax+By+C=0: d = |Ax₁+By₁+C|/√(A²+B²). Distance between two parallel lines Ax+By+C₁=0 and Ax+By+C₂=0 is |C₁-C₂|/√(A²+B²). Family of lines through intersection of L₁=0 and L₂=0 is L₁ + λL₂ = 0.', 'Bisectors of angles between lines a₁x+b₁y+c₁=0 and a₂x+b₂y+c₂=0 are given by (a₁x+b₁y+c₁)/√(a₁²+b₁²) = ± (a₂x+b₂y+c₂)/√(a₂²+b₂²). The bisector of the angle containing the origin is found by checking sign of (a₁x₀+b₁y₀+c₁) and (a₂x₀+b₂y₀+c₂).']

Pair of Straight Lines

['A homogeneous equation of second degree ax² + 2hxy + by² = 0 represents a pair of straight lines passing through the origin. The lines are real and distinct if h² > ab, real and coincident if h² = ab, and imaginary if h² < ab. The individual lines can be obtained by solving for m in bm² + 2hm + a = 0 (if y = mx represents one line).', 'Angle between the lines represented by ax²+2hxy+by²=0: tanθ = 2√(h²-ab)/(a+b). If a+b=0, lines are perpendicular. The joint equation of bisectors of angles between these lines is (x²-y²)/(a-b) = xy/h, i.e., h(x²-y²) = (a-b)xy.', 'If a third line lx+my+n=0 is given, the triangle formed by the pair and this line has area = n²√(h²-ab)/|am²-2hlm+bl²| provided the lines are not concurrent. Condition for three lines to be concurrent: determinant of coefficients vanishes.', 'The product of perpendiculars from a point (x₁,y₁) to the pair of lines ax²+2hxy+by²=0 is (ax₁²+2hx₁y₁+by₁²)/√((a-b)²+4h²). This is used in locus problems. Also, if one line of the pair makes angle α with x-axis and the other β, then tan(α+β) = 2h/(a-b) and tanα tanβ = a/b.']

Circle

['Standard equations: (i) Center at origin, radius r: x²+y²=r²; (ii) Center (h,k), radius r: (x-h)²+(y-k)²=r²; (iii) Diameter form: (x-x₁)(x-x₂)+(y-y₁)(y-y₂)=0 for diameter endpoints (x₁,y₁),(x₂,y₂). General equation: x²+y²+2gx+2fy+c=0 with center (-g,-f) and radius √(g²+f²-c). Conditions: real circle if g²+f²>c, point if =c, imaginary if <c.', 'Parametric form: x = h+r cosθ, y = k+r sinθ. Length of tangent from external point (x₁,y₁) to circle S=0 is √S₁ where S₁ = x₁²+y₁²+2gx₁+2fy₁+c. Power of a point is S₁.', 'Chord with midpoint (x₁,y₁): T=S₁ where T = xx₁+yy₁+g(x+x₁)+f(y+y₁)+c. Equation of chord of contact from (x₁,y₁) is same as tangent form. Pole (p,q) of line lx+my+n=0 with respect to circle satisfies relation derived from T=S₁.', 'Radical axis of two circles S₁=0 and S₂=0 is S₁-S₂=0, a straight line perpendicular to line joining centers. Radical center of three circles is intersection of pairwise radical axes. Coaxal system: family of circles sharing same radical axis; equation S + λL = 0 where L is radical axis.', 'Condition for circle x²+y²+2gx+2fy+c=0 to touch line lx+my+n=0: (lg+mf+c)² = (l²+m²)(g²+f²-c). Length of common tangent between two circles: direct common tangent = √(d²-(r₁+r₂)²), transverse common tangent = √(d²-(r₁-r₂)²) where d is distance between centers.']

Parabola

['Standard parabola y²=4ax (a>0). Vertex at origin, focus at (a,0), directrix x=-a. Latus rectum length = 4a. Parametric coordinates: (at², 2at). Eccentricity e=1. Tangent at (at²,2at): ty = x + at². Normal: y = -tx + 2at + at³.', 'Focal chord: chord passing through focus. If ends are (at₁²,2at₁) and (at₂²,2at₂), then t₁t₂ = -1. Length of focal chord with parameter t is 4a(1+t²). Subtends right angle at focus if t₁t₂ = -1. Latus rectum is focal chord perpendicular to axis.', 'Chord with given midpoint (x₁,y₁): T=S₁. Chord of contact from external point. Pole (x₁,y₁) has polar y y₁ = 2a(x+x₁). Director circle: x+a=0 (directrix). Orthoptic locus: directrix itself.', 'Property: tangents at endpoints of focal chord intersect at right angle on directrix. Segment of tangent between point of contact and directrix subtends right angle at focus. Area of triangle formed by three points on parabola is half absolute value of determinant.', 'Equation in other orientations: x²=4ay (focus (0,a)), y²=-4ax, x²=-4ay. General parabola is conic with eccentricity 1.']

Ellipse

['Standard ellipse x²/a² + y²/b² = 1 (a>b). Center at origin, major axis along x-axis length 2a, minor axis length 2b. Eccentricity e = √(1-b²/a²), foci at (±ae,0), directrices x = ±a/e. Latus rectum length = 2b²/a. Parametric form: (a cosθ, b sinθ) where θ is eccentric angle.', "Focal distances: for point P on ellipse, SP + S'P = 2a. Tangent at (a cosθ, b sinθ): (x cosθ)/a + (y sinθ)/b = 1. Normal: ax secθ - by cosecθ = a²-b². Condition for line y = mx+c to be tangent: c² = a²m²+b².", 'Auxiliary circle: x²+y²=a². Director circle: x²+y²=a²+b² (locus of intersection of perpendicular tangents). Orthoptic circle same as director circle for ellipse.', 'Chord with midpoint (x₁,y₁): T=S₁. Chord of contact from (x₁,y₁). Pole-polar relationship. Conjugate diameters: diameters such that each bisects chords parallel to other; property: sum of squares of conjugate diameters = 2(a²+b²), area of parallelogram formed by tangents at ends of conjugate diameters = 4ab.', 'Eccentric angle difference properties, equation in oblique coordinates.']

Hyperbola

['Standard hyperbola x²/a² - y²/b² = 1. Center origin, transverse axis length 2a along x-axis, conjugate axis length 2b. Eccentricity e = √(1+b²/a²) > 1. Foci at (±ae,0), directrices x = ±a/e. Latus rectum length = 2b²/a. Parametric form: (a secθ, b tanθ). Asymptotes: y = ±(b/a)x, equation combined: x²/a² - y²/b² = 0.', 'Tangent at (a secθ, b tanθ): (x secθ)/a - (y tanθ)/b = 1. Normal: ax cosθ + by cotθ = a²+b². Condition for line y=mx+c to be tangent: c² = a²m² - b². Asymptotes are tangents at infinity.', 'Conjugate hyperbola: x²/a² - y²/b² = -1, shares same asymptotes. Rectangular (equilateral) hyperbola: a=b, asymptotes perpendicular; can be transformed to xy=c² by rotation. Director circle: x²+y²=a²-b² (exists only if a>b). Orthoptic locus is director circle when real.', 'Properties: difference of focal distances = 2a. Tangents at ends of focal chord intersect on corresponding directrix. Rectangle formed by tangents at ends of latus rectum has area 2b². Asymptotes divide hyperbola into regions.', 'Eccentric angle parametric forms, chord properties, pole-polar.']

General Equation of Second Degree & Locus Problems

['General second-degree equation: ax²+2hxy+by²+2gx+2fy+c=0 represents a conic if Δ = abc+2fgh-af²-bg²-ch² ≠ 0. If Δ=0, it represents a pair of lines (degenerate conic). Classification: circle if h=0, a=b; parabola if h²=ab; ellipse if h²<ab; hyperbola if h²>ab. Rectangular hyperbola if a+b=0 (for central conics).', 'Center of conic: solve ∂S/∂x = 0, ∂S/∂y = 0 giving gx+hy+f=0, hx+by+g=0. For parabola, center at infinity. Radius of circle if it represents circle: √(g²+f²-c).', 'Locus problems: method involves taking a moving point P(h,k), imposing geometric condition, eliminating parameters to get relation in h,k. Common loci: midpoint of chord, foot of perpendicular, intersection of tangents, pole of line, etc. For conics, often use chord equations T=S₁, pole-polar relationships.', 'Family of conics: S + λL = 0 represents conics touching given conic S=0 along line L=0. Coaxal systems for circles extend to conics sharing common chord/directrix.', 'Intersection of two conics: solve simultaneously; generally four points. Conditions for tangency, contact of higher order.']

['Common tangents to two conics: solve conditions for line y=mx+c to be tangent to both. If P is pole of line l, and any line through P meets conic at A,B and l at Q, then (P,Q;A,B) = -1 (harmonic ratio).', 'Diameter of conic: locus of midpoints of parallel chords; for parabola y²=4ax, diameters are lines y = constant (parallel to axis). For ellipse/hyperbola, diameters pass through center.', 'Conjugate diameters in ellipse/hyperbola: diameters where each bisects chords parallel to other. For ellipse, if CP and CD are conjugate, then CP²+CD² = a²+b² and area of triangle CPD is ab.', 'Reflective properties: rays from one focus reflect to other focus (ellipse/hyperbola) or become parallel to axis (parabola). Used in optics problems.']

Important facts

  • For pair of lines ax²+2hxy+by²=0, angle θ satisfies tanθ = 2√(h²-ab)/(a+b). If a+b=0, lines are perpendicular.
  • Length of tangent from (x₁,y₁) to circle x²+y²+2gx+2fy+c=0 is √(x₁²+y₁²+2gx₁+2fy₁+c).
  • Director circle of ellipse x²/a²+y²/b²=1 is x²+y²=a²+b². Director circle of hyperbola is x²+y²=a²-b² (if a>b).
  • For parabola y²=4ax, tangents at endpoints of focal chord intersect on directrix and are perpendicular.
  • In ellipse, sum of squares of conjugate diameters = 2(a²+b²). Area of parallelogram formed by tangents at ends of conjugate diameters = 4ab.
  • In hyperbola, difference of focal distances = 2a. Asymptotes are tangents at infinity.
  • Condition for line lx+my+n=0 to be tangent to parabola y²=4ax is lm = a n²? Actually for y²=4ax, tangent form is y = mx + a/m; compare with lx+my+n=0 => my = -lx-n => y = -(l/m)x - n/m, so m_line = -l/m, c = -n/m; condition c = a/m_line => -n/m = a/(-l/m) => -n/m = -am/l => cross-multiply: -nl = -am² => am² = nl. So condition: am² = nl.
  • For ellipse, tangent condition: c² = a²m²+b². For hyperbola, c² = a²m²-b².
  • Parametric forms: parabola (at²,2at), ellipse (a cosθ, b sinθ), hyperbola (a secθ, b tanθ).
  • Chord with midpoint (x₁,y₁) for any conic S=0 is T=S₁ where T is tangent form at (x₁,y₁) but with S₁ evaluated.

Formulas and relationships

Distance formula: d = √[(x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²]
Section formula (internal): ((mx₂+nx₁)/(m+n), (my₂+ny₁)/(m+n))
Section formula (external): ((mx₂-nx₁)/(m-n), (my₂-ny₁)/(m-n))
Centroid of triangle: ((x₁+x₂+x₃)/3, (y₁+y₂+y₃)/3)
Slope of line: m = (y₂-y₁)/(x₂-x₁) = tanθ
Angle between lines: tanθ = |(m₁-m₂)/(1+m₁m₂)|
Distance from point to line: d = |Ax₁+By₁+C|/√(A²+B²)
Distance between parallel lines: d = |C₁-C₂|/√(A²+B²)
Circle equation (general): x²+y²+2gx+2fy+c=0, center (-g,-f), radius √(g²+f²-c)
Tangent to circle at (x₁,y₁): xx₁+yy₁+g(x+x₁)+f(y+y₁)+c=0
Chord with midpoint (x₁,y₁): T=S₁
Parabola y²=4ax: tangent at (at²,2at) is ty = x+at², normal is y = -tx+2at+at³
Ellipse x²/a²+y²/b²=1: tangent at (a cosθ, b sinθ) is (x cosθ)/a + (y sinθ)/b = 1
Hyperbola x²/a²-y²/b²=1: tangent at (a secθ, b tanθ) is (x secθ)/a - (y tanθ)/b = 1
Condition for tangency: line y=mx+c tangent to parabola y²=4ax if c = a/m; to ellipse if c²=a²m²+b²; to hyperbola if c²=a²m²-b²
Length of latus rectum: parabola 4a, ellipse 2b²/a, hyperbola 2b²/a
Eccentricity: parabola e=1, ellipse e=√(1-b²/a²), hyperbola e=√(1+b²/a²)
Director circle: ellipse x²+y²=a²+b², hyperbola x²+y²=a²-b² (if a>b), parabola is directrix x=-a
Pair of lines angle: tanθ = 2√(h²-ab)/(a+b)
Bisectors of pair of lines: h(x²-y²) = (a-b)xy
General second degree discriminant: Δ = abc+2fgh-af²-bg²-ch²; if Δ=0 degenerate conic
Center of conic: solve gx+hy+f=0, hx+by+g=0

Common mistakes

  • Confusing slope-intercept form y=mx+c with line equation lx+my+n=0; careful with signs.
  • In pair of lines, forgetting that h²>ab for real distinct lines, h²=ab for coincident, h²<ab for imaginary.
  • Using wrong sign in tangent condition for hyperbola: should be c² = a²m² - b², not +b².
  • Mixing up parametric coordinates: for ellipse (a cosθ, b sinθ) not (a secθ, b tanθ).
  • Director circle of hyperbola exists only if a > b; if a < b it's imaginary.
  • Assuming all conics have center; parabola's center is at infinity.
  • Forgetting that chord with midpoint (x₁,y₁) uses T=S₁ where T is same expression as tangent but evaluated at (x₁,y₁).
  • In locus problems, incorrectly eliminating parameters; always check if final equation represents valid locus.
  • Confusing length of latus rectum formulas across conics.
  • Not checking conditions for reality (e.g., radius positive, eccentricity ≥0).
