d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}

Symbols: d = distance between P(x₁,y₁) and Q(x₂,y₂)

Unit: length units (same as coordinate units)

When to use: Finding distance between any two points in the plane

Conditions: Applies in standard Cartesian plane; extension to 3D adds (z₂-z₁)² term

Common mistake: Forgetting to square differences before adding, or mixing up x and y pairs

P = \left(\frac{mx_2+nx_1}{m+n},\; \frac{my_2+ny_1}{m+n}\right)

Symbols: P divides segment joining A(x₁,y₁) and B(x₂,y₂) internally in ratio m:n

Unit: coordinate units

When to use: Section formula for internal division

Conditions: m:n > 0 for internal division; for external use m:n with negative sign convention

Common mistake: Using external formula when question asks for internal, or swapping m and n with x₁ and x₂

P_{ext} = \left(\frac{mx_2-nx_1}{m-n},\; \frac{my_2-ny_1}{m-n}\right)

Symbols: P externally divides AB in ratio m:n (m ≠ n)

Unit: coordinate units

When to use: External division — point lies outside the segment on the line extended

Conditions: m ≠ n; if m = n, point is at infinity

Common mistake: Confusing signs — numerator is mx₂ − nx₁ not mx₁ − nx₂

G = \left(\frac{x_1+x_2+x_3}{3},\; \frac{y_1+y_2+y_3}{3}\right)

Symbols: G = centroid of triangle with vertices (x₁,y₁), (x₂,y₂), (x₃,y₃)

Unit: coordinate units

When to use: Centroid divides each median in ratio 2:1 from vertex

Conditions: Valid for any triangle in the plane

Common mistake: Using only two vertices' coordinates instead of all three

\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\big|x_1(y_2-y_3)+x_2(y_3-y_1)+x_3(y_1-y_2)\big|

Symbols: Area of triangle with given vertices

Unit: square units

When to use: Computing area without needing base and height explicitly

Conditions: Positive value always taken; collinear points give area = 0

Common mistake: Omitting absolute value (area is always positive)

A,B,C \text{ collinear} \iff mA_1+mB_1+nC_1=0 \text{ and } mA_2+mB_2+nC_2=0 \text{ (eliminating m,n)}

Symbols: Condition for collinearity of three points

Unit: N/A

When to use: Checking if three given points lie on one line

Conditions: Equivalently: slope AB = slope BC, or determinant of coordinates equals zero

Common mistake: Not checking that denominators are non-zero when using slope form

\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1 \quad (a>b), \qquad e=\sqrt{1-\tfrac{b^2}{a^2}}, \qquad S=(\pm ae,\,0)

Symbols: Ellipse: a = semi-major axis, b = semi-minor axis, e = eccentricity, S = foci

Unit: a, b, ae in length units

When to use: Standard ellipse with major axis along x-axis

Conditions: e ∈ (0,1); for vertical major axis swap a↔b positions

Common mistake: Writing e = √(1+b²/a²) instead of minus; confusing a and b for latus rectum

LLR_{ellipse} = \frac{2b^2}{a}

Symbols: Length of latus rectum of ellipse

Unit: length units

When to use: Focal chord perpendicular to major axis

Conditions: Applies to x²/a² + y²/b² = 1

Common mistake: Using 2a²/b instead (which belongs to hyperbola)

T \equiv 0 \text{ at } (x_1,y_1) \Rightarrow \frac{xx_1}{a^2}+\frac{yy_1}{b^2}=1

Symbols: Tangent to ellipse at point (x₁,y₁) on it

Unit: N/A

When to use: Finding tangent when point of contact is known

Conditions: (x₁,y₁) must satisfy ellipse equation

Common mistake: Forgetting to substitute the point into T to get equation; writing yy₁ instead of yy₁/b²

y = mx \pm \sqrt{a^2m^2+b^2}

Symbols: Tangent to ellipse in slope form; m = slope

Unit: N/A

When to use: When tangent slope is known but point of contact is not

Conditions: Real tangents exist for all real m

Common mistake: Forgetting ± sign or wrong sign under radical

\frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1 \quad (a>0,b>0), \quad e=\sqrt{1+\tfrac{b^2}{a^2}}>1, \quad S=(\pm ae,\,0)

Symbols: Hyperbola: a = transverse semi-axis, b = conjugate semi-axis, e = eccentricity, S = foci

Unit: length units for a, b, ae

When to use: Standard hyperbola opening left-right

Conditions: e > 1 always; conjugate hyperbola: −x²/a² + y²/b² = 1

Common mistake: Using ellipse eccentricity formula; forgetting e > 1

LLR_{hyperbola} = \frac{2b^2}{a}

Symbols: Length of latus rectum of hyperbola

Unit: length units

When to use: Focal chord perpendicular to transverse axis

Conditions: Applies to x²/a² − y²/b² = 1

Common mistake: Same formula as ellipse by coincidence — verify by substituting x = ae

y = mx \pm \sqrt{a^2m^2-b^2}

Symbols: Tangent to hyperbola in slope form

Unit: N/A

When to use: Finding tangent when slope m is given

Conditions: Real tangent only if a²m² − b² ≥ 0, i.e. |m| ≥ b/a

Common mistake: Applying this when |m| < b/a (no real tangent exists in that case)

y = \pm \frac{b}{a}\,x \quad \Longleftrightarrow \; \frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2}=0

Symbols: Asymptotes of hyperbola x²/a² − y²/b² = 1

Unit: N/A

When to use: Asymptotes are tangents at infinity; used for rectangle construction

Conditions: Always pass through origin; rectangle sides are x = ±a, y = ±b

Common mistake: Swapping a and b in slope; asymptotes are NOT part of the hyperbola

y^2 = 4ax \quad (a>0), \quad S=(a,0), \quad e=1, \quad \text{Directrix: } x = -a

Symbols: Parabola: a = distance from vertex to focus, S = focus, directrix is vertical line

Unit: a in length units

When to use: Standard right-opening parabola

Conditions: For y² = −4ax open left; x² = 4ay open up; x² = −4ay open down

Common mistake: Using focus = (−a,0) for y² = 4ax or confusing directrix sign

LLR_{parabola} = 4a

Symbols: Length of latus rectum of parabola y² = 4ax

Unit: length units

When to use: Focal chord perpendicular to axis of symmetry

Conditions: Endpoints are (a, ±2a)

Common mistake: Writing 2a instead of 4a

y = mx + \frac{a}{m} \quad (m \neq 0)

Symbols: Tangent to parabola y² = 4ax in slope form

Unit: N/A

When to use: When slope m of tangent is given

Conditions: m ≠ 0 (horizontal tangent is x-axis, y = 0)

Common mistake: Writing a/m² or ± — only one tangent for each nonzero slope

T \equiv 0 \Rightarrow yy_1 = 2a(x+x_1) \text{ at } (x_1,y_1) \text{ on } y^2=4ax

Symbols: Tangent to parabola at known point (x₁,y₁) on curve

Unit: N/A

When to use: Point of contact given, find tangent equation

Conditions: (x₁,y₁) must satisfy y₁² = 4ax₁

Common mistake: Using 4a instead of 2a coefficient

Normal to y^2=4ax \text{ at } (at^2,\,2at): \; y+tx = 2at+at^3

Symbols: t = parameter; normal at parametric point (at², 2at)

Unit: N/A

When to use: Finding normal when parameter t is known or derived

Conditions: Three normals can be drawn from any point; cubic in t gives them

Common mistake: Confusing slope of normal (−t) with slope of tangent (1/t)

x^2+y^2=r^2

Symbols: Circle: centre = origin O(0,0), radius = r

Unit: r in length units

When to use: Basic circle centred at origin

Conditions: Standard form; general form: x²+y²+2gx+2fy+c=0, centre = (−g,−f), radius = √(g²+f²−c)

Common mistake: Sign error: centre is (−g,−f), not (g,f)

T \equiv xx_1+yy_1=g(-x_1)+f(-y_1)+c \Rightarrow xx_1+yy_1+g(x+x_1)+f(y+y_1)+c=0

Symbols: Tangent to circle x²+y²+2gx+2fy+c=0 at point (x₁,y₁) on circle

Unit: N/A

When to use: Point of contact known; use T = 0

Conditions: (x₁,y₁) must satisfy circle equation

Common mistake: Writing xx₁ + yy₁ = r² for general circle (only true for centred-at-origin)

\text{Length of tangent from } P(x_1,y_1) \text{ to } x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0: \; \sqrt{x_1^2+y_1^2+2gx_1+2fy_1+c}

Symbols: LT = length from point P to point of contact of tangent from P to circle

Unit: length units

When to use: Distance from external point to tangent point

Conditions: Point must be outside circle: S₁ > 0

Common mistake: Forgetting square root; using without verifying S₁ > 0

\text{Radical axis of } C_1=0 \text{ and } C_2=0: \; C_1 - C_2 = 0 \; (\text{coefficient of }x^2,y^2 \text{ equalized})

Symbols: Locus of points having equal power wrt both circles

Unit: N/A

When to use: Common chord of intersecting circles; radical axis is perpendicular to line of centres

Conditions: Always a straight line; if circles touch, it's the common tangent

Common mistake: Subtracting without making x² and y² coefficients equal first

Symbols: λ is parameter; every member passes through the two intersection points

Unit: N/A

When to use: Constructing a circle through two fixed points given two circles

Conditions: λ = −1 gives the radical axis (line), not a circle

Common mistake: Not handling λ = −1 as special case

\theta = \tan^{-1}\left|\frac{m_1-m_2}{1+m_1m_2}\right|, \qquad \perp \Leftrightarrow m_1 m_2 = -1, \qquad \parallel \Leftrightarrow m_1=m_2

Symbols: θ = acute angle between two lines of slopes m₁ and m₂

Unit: radians or degrees

When to use: Angle between two lines, condition for perpendicularity/parallelism

Conditions: 1 + m₁m₂ ≠ 0 for perpendicular check; one line vertical → use direction cosines

Common mistake: Forgetting absolute value gives obtuse angle; sign errors with m₁m₂

ax+by+c=0 \equiv y = mx+c \text{ (slope form),} \qquad \text{Two-point: } \frac{y-y_1}{x-x_1}=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}

Symbols: General, slope-intercept, and two-point forms of a straight line

Unit: N/A

When to use: Writing line equation from different given data

Conditions: Vertical line: x = constant (slope undefined); horizontal: y = constant

Common mistake: Assuming y = mx+c works for vertical lines

\text{Distance from } P(x_1,y_1) \text{ to } ax+by+c=0: \; d = \frac{|ax_1+by_1+c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}

Symbols: Perpendicular distance from point to line

Unit: length units

Symbols: Polar line; pole is P(x₁,y₁)

Unit: N/A

When to use: Constructions involving poles and polars; chord of contact from external point

Conditions: If P is outside, polar is chord of contact of tangents from P

\text{Conjugate diameters of ellipse: } b^2m_1m_2 = -a^2 \quad \text{or} \quad \frac{b^2}{a^2} = -\frac{1}{m_1 m_2}

Symbols: m₁, m₂ = slopes of pair of conjugate diameters

Unit: N/A

When to use: Properties of conjugate diameters; area of parallelogram formed = 4ab

Conditions: Applies to x²/a² + y²/b² = 1

Common mistake: Using same relation for hyperbola (sign changes to +)

\text{Eccentric angle parametric form: } P(a\cos\theta,\; b\sin\theta) \text{ on ellipse; } P(at^2,\,2at) \text{ on parabola}

Symbols: θ or t = parameter representing position on curve

Unit: θ in radians

When to use: Parametric representation for finding tangents, normals, chords easily

Conditions: θ is NOT the geometric angle from centre for ellipse; t is slope-related for parabola

Common mistake: Treating θ as actual geometric angle; confusion between θ and t

\text{Director circle of ellipse: } x^2+y^2=a^2+b^2

Symbols: Locus of intersection of perpendicular tangents to ellipse

Unit: radius = √(a²+b²)

When to use: Finding locus where two perpendicular tangents meet

Conditions: Only defined when a² + b² > 0; same concept for hyperbola: x²+y²=a²−b² (real only if a > b)

Common mistake: Writing a²−b² for ellipse director circle

\text{Auxiliary circle of ellipse: } x^2+y^2=a^2

Symbols: Circle with diameter = major axis of ellipse

Unit: radius = a

When to use: Relating eccentric angle θ to point on ellipse via projection

Conditions: Used heavily in parametric constructions and pedal curves

Common mistake: Confusing auxiliary circle with director circle

\text{Rectangular/hyperbola: } xy=c^2 \quad \text{or} \quad x^2-y^2=a^2

Symbols: Special cases of hyperbola; xy = c² has asymptotes as coordinate axes

Unit: c, a in length units

When to use: Simpler forms for certain JEE problems; tangent at (ct, c/t) is x/t + yt = 2c

Conditions: xy = c² is equilateral rectangular hyperbola rotated 45°

Common mistake: Using standard hyperbola formulas directly on xy=c² without rotation

\text{Focus-directrix definition: } SP = e \cdot PM \quad (e \text{ = eccentricity}, M \text{ on directrix})

Symbols: SP = distance from point to focus; PM = perpendicular distance to directrix

Unit: N/A

When to use: Unifying definition of all conics; deriving equations from focus and directrix

Conditions: e < 1 → ellipse; e = 1 → parabola; e > 1 → hyperbola

Common mistake: Using PM as slant distance instead of perpendicular distance
