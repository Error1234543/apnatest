Rasayanik Gativigya - Flashcards | JEE Main Chemistry

JEE MainHindiFlashcards

Card 1

Rasayanik Gativigya (Chemical Kinetics) ka adhyayan kis baare mein hai?

Rasayanik Gativigya rasayanik abhisrayon ki druti (rate) aur unke liye zimma daar karon ka adhyayan hai. Yeh janta hai ki abhisraya kitni druti se hota hai aur kin-gyne parnishthiyon se uspar prabhav padta hai.

Card 2

Abhisray Druti (Rate of Reaction) ko kaise pratiflotit kiya jaata hai? Ek samikaran likhiye.

Abhisray druti = -d[R]/dt ya +d[P]/dt Yahan [R] aur [P] anukramik roop se abhikarak aur utpaad ka sankendran (molar concentration) hai, aur t samay hai. Druti ke ekak: mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹ (ya M s⁻¹)

Card 3

Sarith druti (Average Rate) vs Turant druti (Instantaneous Rate) mein antar bataiye.

• Sarith druti: dR/dr ke bich ke samay antarral ke liye (ΔR/Δt) → kam druti deta hai. • Turant druti: ek khaas samay par druti (dR/dr par lim Δt→0) → vah kshana par druti deta hai. • Turant druti hamesha sarith druti se adhik sathik (accurate) hoti hai.

Card 4

Abhisray Durgam (Rate Law / Rate Equation) kya hai? Ise kaise nirdharit kiya jaata hai?

Abhisray durgam abhikarakon ke sankendran ke padon ke roop mein abhisray druti ko dikhata hai: Rate = k[A]^x[B]^y • yahaan k = druti niyaank (rate constant) • x, y = abhikarak A, B ke liye kriya ka kram (order) — ye prayaogik roop se milein; sthita samikaran ke gunanko se nahi! Note: kriya ka kram (order) aur prakriyak kshamata (molecularity) alag-2 hain.

Card 5

Pratikriyak Kshamata (Molecularity) kya hai? Uske vidhayak gun kya hain?

Pratikriyak kshamata: ek saath tukdadon (molecules) ki sankhya jo abhikriya mein bhaag leti hai. Gun: • Hamesha ek chota purnank (integer) hota hai — 1, 2, ya 3. • Pratyek prakriya (elementary step) ki apni molecularity hoti hai. • Molecularity kabhi bhi shunya ya bhinn (fractional) nahi ho sakti. • Yeh sidhe taur par prayaog se nahi milta — kram se alag hai.

Card 6

Kriya ka Kram (Order of Reaction) kya hai? Iske mukhya gun bataiye.

Kriya ka kram: abhikarakon ke sankendran par druti ka nirbharata darshaak total eksponent hai. Rate = k[A]^m[B]^n; Order = m+n Gun: • Order prayaogik roop se milta hai. • Order shunya, purnank, bhinn, ya ऋणात्मक bhi ho sakta hai. • Zero-order: druti abhikarak ke sankendran se spasht-roop se anumapit nahi hoti. • First-order: druti ek abhikarak ke sankendran ke anupaatit hoti hai.

Card 7

Shoonya-kram (Zero Order) abhisraya ke liye: druti durgam, samikaranatmak druti, aur druti niyaank (k) ke ekak batayein.

• Druti durgam: Rate = k[A]⁰ = k • Samikaranatmak druti: [A] = [A]₀ - kt • druti niyaank k ka ekak: mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹ • aadha-ajiv (t₁/₂): [A]₀/(2k) — aadhikya [A]₀ ke anupaatit

Card 8

Pratham-kram (First Order) abhisraya ke liye samikaranatmak druti nikalein (integreated rate equation), aur k ka ekak batayein.

Samikaranatmak druti (Integrated Rate Equation): ln[A] = ln[A]₀ - kt ya k = (2.303/t) × log([A]₀/[A]) • k ka ekak: s⁻¹ (ya min⁻¹) • grāf: ln[A] vs t → seedhi rekha, ढलान = –k

Card 9

Dvitiiya-kram (Second Order) abhisraya ke liye samikaranatmak druti aur k ka ekak batayein. Case: [A]=[B] ho.

Case: Rate = k[A]² (ya [A]=[B] ho) Samikaranatmak druti: 1/[A] = 1/[A]₀ + kt • k ka ekak: L mol⁻¹ s⁻¹ (ya M⁻¹s⁻¹) • grāf: 1/[A] vs t → seedhi rekha, ढलान = k

Card 10

Aadha-ajiv (Half-life, t₁/₂) kya hai? Shunya, pratham, evṃ dvitiiya-kram ke liye formula batayein.

Aadha-ajiv woh samay hai jab abhikarak ka sankendran adhika hona aata hai. • Shunya-kram: t₁/₂ = [A]₀/(2k) • Pratham-kram: t₁/₂ = 0.693/k = ln2/k — ye [A]₀ se svatantra hota hai! • Dvitiiya-kram: t₁/₂ = 1/(k[A]₀) — ye [A]₀ ke vyutkram ke anupaatit hota hai.

Card 11

Pratham-kram abhisraya ke liye: 100% kshay it samay lene ke liye kitna hai? Ise pratham-kram aadha-ajiv se kaise jodkar batayein?

Pratham-kram abhisraya ke liye: • 100% kshay → anant samay; lekin prakritik roop se 99% kshay mein prathmik aadha-ajiv ≈ (ln100)/k ≈ 4.605/k = 6.64 × t₁/₂ • 90% kshay: t₉₀ = 3.32 × t₁/₂ • t₁₀₀% se adhik samay chahiye lekin 99% prathi kshay prathmik aadha-ajiv se pradhanyata se juda hai.

Card 12

Arhenius Samikaran (Arrhenius Equation) likhiye, sari charo ko varnit kariye.

k = A × e^(-Ea/RT) • k = druti niyaank (rate constant) • A = aavritti gunank (frequency factor) — pratitimriya vibhajan se juda hua • Ea = pravartak shakti (activation energy), J/mol • R = mahagath sthirank = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹ • T = aabhogik taapmaan (Kelvin mein) Yeh arhenius samikaran batata hai kaise druti niyaank taapmaan se badalta hai.

Card 13

Arhenius samikaran ka prayaugik swaroop (log form) likhiye. Do alag taapmaan par k use kaise nikalein?

Log swaroop: k = A × 10^(-Ea/(2.303×R×T)) Do taapmaan T₁ aur T₂ ke liye: log(k₂/k₁) = (Ea/2.303R) × [(T₂-T₁)/(T₁T₂)] Iska prayog krke pravartak shakti (Ea) ka mnaya ja sakta hai.

Card 14

Pravartak Shakti (Activation Energy, Ea) kya hai? Kya koi abhisraya shunya pravartak shakti ke saath ho sakta hai?

Pravartak shakti (Ea): utteshit avastha (activated complex) tak pahunchne ke liye aavashyak nyunatam shakti hoti hai. • Adhik Ea → mand druti, kam Taapmaan par bhi. • Shunya Ewalar: haan — kuch abhisray (jaise radikal sankalan) shunya ya negetive Ea dikhate hain. • Kuch abhisrayon ke liye Ea pratikarjak dravyon dwara kam ki jaati hai (prayog se).

Card 15

Taapmaan Gunank (Temperature Coefficient, Q₁₀ or μ) kya hai? Prakritik roop se ise kis liye istemal kiya jaata hai?

Q₁₀ = (druti at T+10°C) / (druti at T) • Prakritik roop se Q₁₀ ≈ 2 se 3 hota hai — yah 10°C pratisar taapmaan badhne se druti lagbhag do-gun ho jaati hai. • Q₁₀ ka prayog abhisrayon ke taapmaan-nirbharata ka ek saral sankheya maank deta hai. • Ye aam taur par Ei dene se milta nahi, par prakritik rupa se janne me madadgar hota hai.

Card 16

Sanghatan theori (Collision Theory) ke anusaar druti badhne ke liye kin teen sharton ka poora hona anivarya hai?

Sanghatan Theori ke anusaar druti badhne ke liye: 1. Sargat (Collision): abhikarak ke kalika (particles) aapas mein takrani honi chahiye. 2. Yogya drishya (Proper Orientation): takran ka drishya (orientation) yogya hona chahiye taaki abhikriya ho sake. 3. Nyunatam Shakti (Threshold/Activation Energy): talikalon ki pravegta shakti, taapkta shakti ya dono honi chahiye ≥ Ea. Yadi teeno shartein puri hoti hain to hi prabhavi sanghatan hota hai.

Card 17

Sanghatan theori (Collision Theory) ke anusar druti niyaank k ko kaise varnit kiya jaata hai?

Sanghatan theori ke anusar: k = Z × P × e^(-Ea/RT) • Z = sanghatan aavritti (collision frequency) — kalikon ke bich prati sekand sanghatan sankhya • P = pradarshan gunank (steric/orientation factor) — yogya drishya me sanbhavna (0<P≤1) • e^(-Ea/RT) = yogya shakti vale kaliko ka bhag A = Z × P hota hai. Drishtantiyon me P ko mukhya roop se smarniya hai.

Card 18

Mukhya roop se druti ke anupaat ko badhane walie karan kin-hai? (Karakon ka sammilith varnan)

Druti par prabhav dalne wale karan: • Abhikarak ka sankendran/concentration: badhne se druti badhti hai. • Taapmaan (T): badhne se druti badhti hai (e^(-Ea/RT) badhta hai). • Pravartak shakti (Ea): kam ho to druti badhti hai. • Prerarok (catalyst): Ea ko kam karke druti badhata hai. • Pratyabhabh (surface area) — kathan avastha ke liye. • Vibhaskaran — photo-rasayanik abhisrayon ke liye. • Avastha ke anusaar — gas/pravahi/drav me alag-druti.

Card 19

Ek Pratham-kram abhisraya ke liye 30% kshay ho jaane mein kitna samay lagega agar aadha-ajiv 100 sec hai? Calculation dikhayein.

Diya: t₁/₂ = 100 s, 30% kshay = [A]₀ - [A] = 0.3[A]₀ → [A]/[A]₀ = 0.7 k = 0.693/t₁/₂ = 0.693/100 = 6.93×10⁻³ s⁻¹ t = (2.303/k) × log([A]₀/[A]) = (2.303/6.93×10⁻³) × log(1/0.7) = 332.2 × 0.1549 ≈ 51.5 sec Uttar: ≈ 52 sec

Card 20

Drav me abhisraya druti ke liye ek pratham-kram abhisraya ka prathamik parikshan (experimental test) kya hoga?

Pratham-kram abhisraya ke liye prathamik parikshan: • ln[A] vs samay (t) ka grāf kheechkar dekhain — agar seedhi rekha aate hain to kriya pratham-kram ki hai. • Aarthik: log(A/B) vs time linear hona chahiye. • Druti niyaank k = - (slope) hota hai. • Yadi grāf seedha nahi hota to kriya pratham-kram nahi hai.

Card 21

Vishal-kram (Pseudo-first-order) abhisraya kya hai? Ek udaharan dein.

Vishal-kram (Pseudo-first-order) tab hota hai jab prakritik roop se dvitiiya-kram pratyabalya me se ek abhikarak ki matra bahut adhik ya dhruv rakhi jaati hai. • Udaaharan: ester ka jal-vighatan (ester hydrolysis) CH₃COOC₂H₅ + H₂O → CH₃COOH + C₂H₅OH • Yahaan H₂O bahut adhik matra me hota hai — isliye [H₂O] dhruv rehta hai. • Druti = k'[ester]^{1} jahaan k' = k[H₂O] • Yeh prathmik-kram jaisa drsti deta hai.

Card 22

Shoonya-kram (Zero Order) druti-grāf kaisa dikhta hai? Yadi aap [A] vs t grāf banayein to kya milta hai?

Shoonya-kram abhisraya ke liye: • [A] vs t grāf: seedhi rekha, ढलान = –k • Rate vs [A] grāf: [A] par nirbhar nahi — horizontal line (druti dhruv rehti hai) • Druti niyaank k = [A]₀ - [A])/t • Aadha-ajiv = [A]₀/(2k) — [A]₀ ke anupaatit

Card 23

Ek dvitiiya-kram abhisraya (Rate = k[A]²) ke liye ¾ kshay hone mein kitne aadhe-ajiv lagenge? Sparshik ginit kariye.

Dvitiiya-kram ke liye t₁/₂(n) = t₁/₂(1) × 2^(n-1) • pehla aadha-ajiv: [A]₀/2 tak • dusra aadha-ajiv: aadhe se aadha (¼ shesh) • tisra aadha-ajiv: ¼ se aadha (⅛ shesh) ¾ kshay = ¼ shesh baaki Yeh exactly dusre aadhe-ajiv ke baad hota hai. Uttar: 2 aadhe-ajiv

Card 24

Kisi abhisraya me prerarok (catalyst) hone se kya badalta hai aur kya nahin? Yeh spashht kariye.

Prerarok (Catalyst) ke prabhav: • BADHATA HAI: druti niyaank k, druti, Pravarthak Shakti (Ea) ko kam karta hai (naya marg pradan karta hai) • NAHI BADHATA: uttaapik sthitiyan (thermodynamic properties) jaise ΔG, ΔH, santulan mank (K) • naabhimukhya naya path pradan karke druti badhata hai, pratyavartan (equilibrium) par prabhav nahi padta.

Card 25

Abhisray Druti aur Prathmik Druti (Reaction velocity) me antar bataiye. Dono ke ekak kya hain?

Abhisray Druti: • Druti: d[R]/dt ya d[P]/dt — abhikarak ya utpaad ke sankendran ka parivaratan druti • Ekak: mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹ Prathmik Druti: • Yeh sirf ek hi abhikarak ya utpaad ke liye druti ko darshata hai. • Druti samikaran: v = k[A]^x[B]^y — prathmik druti yahi hoti hai. • Druti aur prathmik druti ke ekak same hote hain — sirf prakasha ka antar.

Card 26

Abhisraya druti ko maapne ke kin-kon se prayaugik padno ko nirdharit kiya jaata hai? Unka sandarbh likhiye.

Abhisraya druti naapne ke liye yeh prathmik parikalpanayein ki jati hain: • Parimanik roop se drutyapan (measuring rate) ke liye kisi ek abhikarak ya utpaad ke sankendran ko samay ke saath niptar karna hota hai. • Parikalpanayein: v = -(1/a)(d[A]/dt) = (1/p)(d[P]/dt) jahaan a, p sthiti samikaran ke gunanko se. • Vyavharik roop se: pH-metradarshan, pressure, volumetry, conductivity, spectrophotometry, titration.

Card 27

Arhenius graaf (ln k vs 1/T) se pravartak shakti (Ea) kaise nikali jaati hai? Yeh sparshik roop se varnit kariye.

Arhenius samikaran ka log swaroop: ln k = ln A - Ea/(RT) • ln k vs 1/T grāf → seedhi rekha, ढलान = -Ea/R • R = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹ Agar ढलान (slope) mil gaya to: Ea = -slope × R Prayogik roop se do k mano ke liye: log(k₂/k₁) = Ea/(2.303R) × (1/T₁ - 1/T₂)

Card 28

Kai abhisrayon me druti samikaran se sthiti samikaran ka bhav nahi milta. Ek udaharan dete hue iski varna kijiye.

Udaaharan: H₂ + I₂ → 2HI • Sthiti samikaran: H₂ + I₂ → 2HI (gunanak 1:1:2) • Druti samikaran: Rate = k[H₂][I₂] — is sthiti mein druti samikaran sthiti samikaran ke gunanko se anurup hai. Doosra udaharan: H₂ + 2NO → N₂O + H₂O • Druti samikaran: Rate = k[H₂][NO]² • Sthiti samikaran ke gunanko (1,2,1) ke anusaar druti samikaran nahi hota. Nishkarsh: druti samikaran hamesha prayaog se milta hai — sthiti samikaran se sidha anuman nahi kiya ja sakta.

Card 29

JEE Main ke liye Chemical Kinetics ke sabse mahattvapurna 5 formulas ko list kariye.

1. Pratham-kram: k = (2.303/t) log([A]₀/[A]) 2. Pratham-kram aadha-ajiv: t₁/₂ = 0.693/k (swatantra [A]₀) 3. Arhenius: k = A × e^(-Ea/RT) 4. Druti durgam: Rate = k[A]^x[B]^y 5. Shoonya-kram druti samikaran: [A] = [A]₀ - kt Yeh 5 formulas JEE Main ke zyaadatar prashnon ke liye kaafi honge.
