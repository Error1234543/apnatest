Card 1

p-ब्लॉक तत्वों का सामान्य परिचय एवं आवर्तिक प्रवृत्तियाँ

p-ब्लॉक में वह समूह हैं जिनमें अंतिम इलेक्ट्रॉन ns²np¹–⁶ कोशिकाओं में वर्ण होता है। यह समूह 13 से 18 तक विस्तारित हैं। इनमें धातुएँ, अधातुएँ और उभयधर्मी तत्व शामिल हैं। मुख्य प्रवृत्तियाँ: • आयनन ऊर्जा: समूह में नीचे जाने पर घटती है, आवर्त में बाएँ से दाएँ बढ़ती है। • तড়िद्विद्युत धनात्मकता: समूह में घटती, आवर्त में बढ़ती है। • त्रिज्या: समूह में नीचे जाने पर बढ़ती है (अंतः संक्रमण के कारण दक्षिणावर्त प्रभाव भी)। • ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था: समूह के अनुसार मुख्य ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था (समूह-18)⁺ होती है; निचले समूहों में inert-pair प्रभाव के कारण (n-2)⁺ अवस्था भी स्थिर होती है। • लोहादीप्ति: छोटे आकार वाले तत्व (B, C, N) covalent बंध बनाते हैं; बड़े तत्व आयनिक यौगिक बना सकते हैं।

Card 2

बोरॉन समूह (13वाँ समूह) – मुख्य तत्व एवं परिचय

समूह 13: B, Al, Ga, In, Tl • इलेक्ट्रॉनिक विन्यास: ns²np¹ • B अधातु, Baकी अन्य धातुएँ हैं, उभयधर्मी गुण दिखाती हैं। • प्रमुख विशेषताएँ: – B की आयनन ऊर्जा बहुत अधिक होने के कारण आयनिक यौगिक नहीं बनाता; सहायक बंध (coordinate bond) व बहु中心 bonds (3c‑2e) बनाता है। – Al, Ga, In, Tl धात्विक बंध बनाते हैं। • प्रवृत्तियाँ: ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था +3 सर्वत्र, परन्तु Tl में +1 अधिक स्थायी (inert‑pair प्रभाव)।Al, Ga, In, Tl की धात्विक प्रकृति बढ़ती है। Ga का द्रवणांक कम है (29.8 °C)।Boron是唯一 non‑metal; बाकी धातुएँ हैं।

Card 3

बोरॉन के महत्त्वपूर्ण यौगिक – बोरिक अम्ल व बोरैक्स

बोरिक अम्ल H₃BO₃ या B(OH)₃: • preparation: borite ya borax से H₂SO₄ या HCl के साथ treatment। • structure: layer lattice, mỗi B liên kết với 3 OH, có liên kết hydro giữa các lớp. • tính chất: – Là acid Lewis (chấp nhận pair electron) → [B(OH)₄]⁻ trong nước. – Khi đun nóng: H₃BO₃ → HBO₂ (metaboric) → B₂O₃ (boric oxide). • ứng dụng: chất chống cháy, thủy tinh borosilicate, gốm sứ, dược phẩm. Borax Na₂B₄O₇·10H₂O: • Preparation: từ minery kernite hoặc từ ore colemanite. • Cấu trúc: ion [B₄O₅(OH)₄]²⁻ chứa cả B sp² và sp³. • Tính chất: tạo dung dịch kiềm do thủy phân → dùng làm chất tẩy rửa, chuẩn độ (với methyl orange). • bead test: B₂O₃ + muối kim loại → perolixit màu đặc trưng.

Card 4

बोरॉन के हाइडाइड – बोरैन (बोरॉन हाइड्राइड्स)

बोरॉन हाइड्राइड्स = बोरैन (BₙHₙ₊₆ या BₙHₙ₊₄). • सबसे सरल: diborane(6) B₂H₆. • Structure of B₂H₆: – दो bridging H (3c‑2e bonds) और चार terminal H (normal 2c‑2e bonds). – B atoms sp³ hybridized. • Preparation: BF₃ + LiH → [LiBH₄] → thermal decomposition at 200 °C gives B₂H₆ + LiF + H₂. • Properties: – Pyrophoric in air → burns to B₂O₃ + H₂O. – Hydrolyzes rapidly: B₂H₆ + 6H₂O → 2B(OH)₃ + 6H₂. – Reacts with NH₃ → addition compound then borazine (inorganic benzene). • Larger boranes: B₅H₉, B₁₀H₁₄ etc. (cluster hydrides). • Uses: rocket fuel, synthesis of organoboron compounds.

Card 5

एल्यूमीनियम के महत्त्वपूर्ण यौगिक – एलुमिनियम ऑक्साइड, हाइड्राक्साइड व एलums

Aluminium oxide Al₂O₃ (alumina): • Occurs as corundum, ruby, sapphire. • Amphoteric: reacts with acids and bases. • Preparation: Bayer process from bauxite → Al(OH)₃ → calcination → Al₂O₃. • Uses: extraction of Al, abrasive, ceramic, catalyst support. Aluminium hydroxide Al(OH)₃: • Amphoteric – dissolves in excess NaOH to form [Al(OH)₄]⁻. • Preparation: precipitation from Al³⁺ salts with NH₄OH. • Used as antacid, water purifier. Alums (double sulphates): • General formula: M⁺M³⁺(SO₄)₂·12H₂O where M⁺=K,Na,NH₄; M³⁺=Al,Cr,Fe. • Potash alum KAl(SO₄)₂·12H₂O: used in water purification, as mordant, in fire extinguishers. • Formation: mix equimolar sulphates, crystallize. • Isomorphous with other alums.

Card 6

कार्बन समूह (14वाँ समूह) – मुख्य तत्व एवं ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ

समूह 14: C, Si, Ge, Sn, Pb • इलेक्ट्रॉनिक विन्यास: ns²np² → 4 valence electrons. • Main oxidation states: +4 and +2; +2 becomes more stable down the group due to inert-pair effect. • C, Si are non-metals; Ge is metalloid; Sn, Pb are metals. • Melting/boiling points decrease down the group (except between Si and Ge anomaly). • Allotropes: C (diamond, graphite, fullerenes); Si (crystalline, amorphous); others have metallic structures. • Catenation ability highest in C, decreases down group. • Chemistry dominated by covalent bonding.

Card 7

कार्बन के अपररूप – हीरा, ग्रेफाइट, फुलरीन

Diamond: • Each C is sp³ hybridized, tetrahedral network. • Hardest natural substance, insulator, high refractive index. • Prepared synthetically under high P and T. Graphite: • Each C is sp² hybridized, hexagonal layers held by van der Waals forces. • Good conductor of electricity, lubricant. • Layers can slide over each other. • Used in pencils, electrodes, nuclear reactors. Fullerenes (C₆₀, etc.): • Cage-like molecules, carbon atoms in pentagons and hexagons. • C₆₀ (buckminsterfullerene) has 12 pentagons and 20 hexagons. • Reactivity: undergoes addition reactions, can trap metal atoms (endohedral fullerenes). Other allotropes: amorphous carbon, carbon nanotubes, graphene.

Card 8

कार्बन के प्रमुख यौगिक – कार्बन मोनोऑक्साइड व कार्बन डाइऑक्साइड

Carbon monoxide CO: • Preparation: heating ZnO with coke or partial combustion of carbon. • Structure: triple bond (one σ, two π) with formal charges C≡O⁺; weak ligand in metal carbonyls. • Properties: colorless, odorless, highly toxic (binds to hemoglobin). Reducing agent in metallurgy. • Reacts with Cl₂ under UV to give phosgene (COCl₂). Carbon dioxide CO₂: • Preparation: combustion of carbon, thermal decomposition of carbonates, fermentation. • Structure: linear O=C=O, sp hybridized C. • Properties: colorless, non-combustible, slightly acidic oxide. Solid form = dry ice (sublimes at −78 °C). • Used in fire extinguishers, soft drinks, dry ice cooling. • Reacts with water to form carbonic acid, with bases to form carbonates/bicarbonates.

Card 9

सिलिकॉन के यौगिक – सिलिकॉन चार्लोराइड, सिलिकॉन डाइऑक्साइड व सिलिकेट्स

Silicon tetrachloride SiCl₄: • Prepared by heating SiO₂ with coke and chlorine. • Hydrolyzes violently: SiCl₄ + 2H₂O → SiO₂ + 4HCl. • Used to prepare pure silicon, silicone polymers. Silicon dioxide SiO₂ (silica): • Exists as quartz, cristobalite, tridymite. • Each Si is tetrahedrally bonded to four O atoms; each O bridges two Si. • Chemically inert, reacts with HF (used for glass etching) and hot concentrated NaOH. • Used in glass, ceramics, optical fibers. Silicates: • Built from SiO₄⁴⁻ tetrahedra sharing oxygen atoms. • Types: orthosilicates, pyrosilicates, chain silicates, sheet silicates, three-dimensional frameworks (e.g., quartz, feldspar, mica). • Zeolites: microporous aluminosilicates used as ion-exchange resins and catalysts.

Card 10

नाइट्रोजन समूह (15वाँ समूह) – मुख्य तत्व एवं आवर्तिक प्रवृत्तियाँ

समूह 15: N, P, As, Sb, Bi • Valence shell configuration: ns²np³ → 5 valence electrons. • Oxidation states: -3, +3, +5; -3 decreases down group, +5 becomes less stable (inert-pair effect makes +3 more stable for heavier elements). • N, P are non-metals; As, Sb metalloids; Bi is metal. • Catenation: N–N bond weak, but P shows moderate catenation. • Allotropes: N₂ (diatomic gas), P (white, red, black), As (grey, yellow), etc. • Basic character of hydrides decreases: NH₃ > PH₃ > AsH₃ > SbH₃ > BiH₃. • Boiling points increase down group except NH₃ anomaly due to hydrogen bonding.

Card 11

नाइट्रोजन के महत्त्वपूर्ण यौगिक – अमोनिया, नाइट्रिक अम्ल व नाइट्रोजन ऑक्साइड

Ammonia NH₃: • Haber process: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ (iron catalyst, 700 K, 200 atm). • Structure: trigonal pyramidal, sp³ hybridized N, one lone pair. • Properties: pungent smell, highly soluble in water (hydrogen bonding), basic (Lewis base). • Reacts with acids to form ammonium salts, with Cu²⁺ to form deep blue [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺ complex. • Used in fertilizers, explosives, refrigerants. Nitric acid HNO₃: • Ostwald process: oxidation of NH₃ to NO, then to NO₂, absorption in water. • Strong oxidizing agent, nitrating agent. • Passivates Fe, Al due to oxide layer. • Reacts with metals (except noble metals) to give NO or NO₂ depending on concentration. Nitrogen oxides: • NO: colorless gas, forms brown NO₂ on exposure to air. • NO₂: brown gas, dimerizes to N₂O₄ (colorless) on cooling. • N₂O: laughing gas, neutral oxide. • N₂O₅: anhydride of HNO₃, white solid. • NO₃⁻ ion: planar, resonance stabilized.

Card 12

फास्फोरस के अपररूप व यौगिक – सफेद व लाल फास्फोरस, फास्फाइन व फॉस्फोरस अम्ल

Allotropes: • White phosphorus P₄: tetrahedral, highly reactive, glows in dark (chemiluminescence), stored under water. Toxic. • Red phosphorus: polymeric chain structure, less reactive, used in matches. • Black phosphorus: most stable, layered structure like graphite. Phosphine PH₃: • Prepared by heating P₄ with浓 NaOH or CaH₂ with P₄. • Weakly basic, pyrophoric when impure (contains P₂H₄). • Reacts with halogens to form PX₃ and PX₅. Phosphorus acids: • Orthophosphorous acid H₃PO₃: diprotic, structure P(=O)(OH)₂(H). • Orthophosphoric acid H₃PO₄: triprotic, formed by oxidation of P₄ with conc. HNO₃. • Hypophosphorous acid H₃PO₂: monoprotic. • Anhydrides: P₄O₆ (for H₃PO₃), P₄O₁₀ (for H₃PO₄).

Card 13

ऑक्सीजन समूह (16वाँ समूह) – मुख्य तत्व एवं आवर्तिक प्रवृत्तियाँ

समूह 16 (chalcogens): O, S, Se, Te, Po • Valence shell: ns²np⁴ → 6 valence electrons. • Main oxidation states: -2, +2, +4, +6; -2 is common for lighter elements, +4/+↓ dominate for heavier. • O is non-metal, S, Se, Te are non-metals/metalloids, Po is metal. • Catenation: S shows extensive catenation (S₈ rings), Se, Te less so. • Allotropes: O₂ (paramagnetic, blue liquid), O₃ (ozone, bent structure); S₈ (rhombic, monoclinic), Se (grey metallic), etc. • Acidic character of oxides increases down group (basic for Po). • Hydrogen bonding in H₂O, not in H₂S.

Card 14

ऑक्सीजन के यौगिक – ओजोन, हाइड्रोजन परऑक्साइड व सल्फ्यूरिक अम्ल

Ozone O₃: • Bent structure, resonance hybrid, strong oxidizing agent. • Prepared by silent electric discharge through O₂. • Used for disinfection, bleaching, removal of organic impurities. • Reacts with Ag, PbS (blackening), KI solution (liberates I₂). • Damaged by CFCs. Hydrogen peroxide H₂O₂: • Structure: non-planar, skewed, weak acid. • Preparation: barium peroxide + H₂SO₄ → BaSO₄ + H₂O₂; anthraquinone process. • Properties: oxidizing and reducing agent, decomposes to H₂O + O₂ (catalyzed by light, dust). • Used as bleaching agent, rocket fuel, green oxidant. Sulfuric acid H₂SO₄: • Contact process: S → SO₂ → SO₃ (V₂O₅ catalyst) → absorbed in conc. H₂SO₄ (oleum) → diluted with water. • Strong diprotic acid, dehydrating agent, oxidizing agent when hot/concentrated. • Reacts with metals, non-metals, salts. • Important in fertilizers, detergents, batteries.

Card 15

सल्फर के महत्त्वपूर्ण यौगिक – सल्फर डाइऑक्साइड, सल्फेट्स व सल्फीदाइड्स

Sulfur dioxide SO₂: • Prepared by burning S in air, heating Cu₂S or Na₂SO₃ with H₂SO₄. • Bent molecule, resonance hybrid. • Properties: colorless, pungent, liquefies easily, reducing agent (decolorizes KMnO₄, converts Fe³⁺ to Fe²⁺, reduces Cr₂O₇²⁻ to Cr³⁺). • Used in refining oil, bleaching (temporary), preservative. • Pollutant causing acid rain. Sulfates: • Solubility rules: sulfates of Ba, Pb, Ca are insoluble; others soluble. • Barium sulfate used in X-ray imaging. • Mohr’s salt (ammonium iron(II) sulfate) is a double salt. Sulfides: • Metal sulfides often insoluble, colored (CuS black, ZnS white, CdS yellow). • H₂S is a weak acid, precipitates metal sulfides from acidic/alkaline solutions. • Acts as reducing agent.

Card 16

हैलोजन समूह (17वाँ समूह) – मुख्य तत्व एवं आवर्तिक प्रवृत्तियाँ

समूह 17 (halogens): F, Cl, Br, I, At • Valence configuration: ns²np⁵ → 7 valence electrons. • High electronegativity, strong oxidizing agents (F₂ strongest). • Exist as diatomic molecules. • Melting/boiling points increase down group (van der Waals forces). • Color: F₂ pale yellow, Cl₂ greenish-yellow, Br₂ red-brown, I₂ violet. • Oxidation states: -1 common, +1,+3,+5,+7 for Cl, Br, I (due to d orbitals). • Reactivity decreases down group; F₂ reacts explosively with many substances. • Hydrgen bonding only in HF.

Card 17

क्लोरीन के महत्त्वपूर्ण यौगिक – हाइड्रोक्लोरिक अम्ल, सोडियम क्लोराइड व क्लोरीन जल

Hydrochloric acid HCl: • Prepared by action of conc. H₂SO₄ on NaCl (Manheim process) or direct combination of H₂ and Cl₂. • Strong acid, fumes in moist air, forms azeotrope (20.2% HCl, bp 108.5 °C). • Reacts with metals, bases, carbonates. • Aqua regia (3 HCl : 1 HNO₃) dissolves Au, Pt. Sodium chloride NaCl: • Common salt, obtained from sea water or rock salt mines. • Used in food, production of Na, Cl₂, HCl, NaOH (chlor-alkali process). • Debye-Hückel theory studies its electrolytic behavior. Chlorine water: • Cl₂ dissolved in water gives mixture of HCl and HOCl (hypochlorous acid). • HOCl is unstable, decomposes to give nascent oxygen → bleaching action. • Used for disinfection, bleaching. • Reacts with ammonia to form NH₂Cl, further to NCl₃ (explosive).

Card 18

हैलोजन के ऑक्सीअम्ल – क्लोरीन के +1, +3, +5, +7 ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाओं के अम्ल

Hypochlorous acid HOCl (+1): • Unstable, exists only in solution, strong oxidizer, bleaching agent. • Salts: hypochlorites (ClO⁻). Chlorous acid HClO₂ (+3): • Unstable, salts: chlorites (ClO₂⁻). Chloric acid HClO₃ (+5): • Strong acid, unstable, salts: chlorates (ClO₃⁻). KClO₃ used in matches, fireworks. Perchloric acid HClO₄ (+7): • Strongest known acid, very unstable when hot/concentrated, powerful oxidizer. • Salts: perchlorates (ClO₄⁻), mostly insoluble (except NH₄⁺, K⁺). • Structure of oxyacids: oxoatoms of central halogen, acidity increases with oxidation state, bond strength decreases with size. • Thermal stability: HClO₄ > HClO₃ > HClO₂ > HClO.

Card 19

निष्क्रिय गैसें (समूह 18) – मुख्य तत्व, आयनन ऊर्जा व संयोजकता

Group 18: He, Ne, Ar, Kr, Xe, Rn • Valence configuration: ns²np⁶ (octet complete). • Highest ionization energy in their periods. • Monatomic, colorless, odorless, low boiling points. • Solubility in water increases down group. • Chemical inertness: He, Ne, Ar extremely unreactive; Kr, Xe form compounds with strong oxidizers (F₂, O₂) especially under pressure. • Xenon fluorides: XeF₂, XeF₄, XeF₆ (prepared by different conditions). • Xenon oxides: XeO₃ (explosive), XeO₄. • Hydrides not known. • Uses: He in balloons, diving; Ne in signs; Ar in welding, bulbs; Xe in lamps, anesthesia. • Radon (Rn) radioactive, health hazard.

Card 20

निष्क्रिय गैसों के यौगिक – ज़ेनॉन फ्लोराइड व ऑक्साइड्स की संरचना व ज्यामिति

Xenon fluorides: • XeF₂: prepared by heating Xe and F₂ (1:5) at 400 °C in Ni vessel. Linear, sp³d hybridization, 3 lone pairs on Xe. • XeF₄: Xe + F₂ (1:2) at 400 °C, 1 atm. Square planar, sp³d², 2 lone pairs. • XeF₆: Xe + excess F₂ at high P/T. Distorted octahedral due to lone pair. • Hydrolysis: XeF₂ + H₂O → Xe + O₂ + 2HF; XeF₄ + H₂O → Xe + XeO₃ + HF + O₂; XeF₆ + H₂O → XeO₃ + 6HF (or XeOF₄ if limited water). • XeF₆ acts as fluorinating agent. Xenon oxides: • XeO₃: prepared by hydrolysis of XeF₆ with limited water. Pyramidal, sp³, 1 lone pair. Highly explosive when dry. • XeO₄: tetrahedral, sp³, no lone pair. Less stable. • XeO₃ dissolves in water to give xenonic acid (H₄XeO₆) – orthoxenic acid, tetrahedral. • Xe-O bonds are strong, Xe-F bonds also strong.

Card 21

p-ब्लॉक तत्वों के सामान्य निदानात्मक प्रश्न – आवर्तिक अभिप्रवृत्तियाँ व अपवाद

1. Which element has the highest electronegativity? Fluorine. 2. Which p-block element is liquid at room temperature? Bromine. 3. Which element shows maximum catenation? Carbon. 4. Why does nitrogen not form pentahalide while phosphorus does? N lacks d-orbitals. 5. Why is bismuth the most metallic in group 15? Largest size, lowest ionization energy. 6. Why does helium not form compounds? Highest IE, no d-orbitals. 7. Why is ozone a stronger oxidant than oxygen? Unstable, releases nascent oxygen. 8. Why is HF a weak acid while HCl is strong? Strong H-bonding in HF, high lattice energy of HF. 9. Why is H₂S acidic but H₂O neutral? H₂S更易解离出H⁺。 10. Why does ClO₄⁻ have no lone pair while ClO₃⁻ has one? Different numbers of bonding pairs. 11. Why is HNO₃ a stronger acid than HNO₂? More O atoms, better stabilization of conjugate base. 12. Why is I₂ less soluble in water but soluble in KI? Forms I₃⁻ complex. 13. Why is PbO₂ a strong oxidizing agent? Pb⁴⁺ unstable, tends to reduce to Pb²⁺. 14. Why is SnCl₂ a reducing agent? Sn²⁺ oxidizes to Sn⁴⁺. 15. Why is BCl₃ a Lewis acid while NCl₃ is not? B empty p-orbital accepts electron pair; N has lone pair.

Card 22

p-ब्लॉक तत्वों के महत्त्वपूर्ण अभिक्रियाएँ – तड़ित निष्कासन, अम्लीय/क्षारीय व्यवहार

1. Borax bead test: B₂O₃ + metal oxide → metal metaborate (colored). Cr³⁺ → green bead (hot) / blue (cold); Co²⁺ → blue; Mn²⁺ → violet. 2. Boron reduction: B₂O₃ + 3Mg → 2B + 3MgO. 3. Aluminium corrosion: 2Al + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂; Al₂O₃ + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂O. 4. Carbon combustion: C + O₂ → CO₂; 2C + O₂ → 2CO. 5. Silica etching: SiO₂ + 4HF → SiF₄ + 2H₂O; SiF₄ + 2HF → H₂SiF₆. 6. Nitrogen fixation: N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ (Haber); N₂ + O₂ → 2NO (lightning). 7. Phosphorus burning: P₄ + 5O₂ → P₄O₁₀. 8. Sulfur burning: S + O₂ → SO₂; 2SO₂ + O₂ → 2SO₃ (Contact process). 9. Chlorine preparation: MnO₂ + 4HCl → MnCl₂ + Cl₂ + 2H₂O. 10. Halogen displacement: Cl₂ + 2Br⁻ → 2Cl⁻ + Br₂. 11. Xenon fluoride hydrolysis: XeF₂ + H₂O → Xe + ½O₂ + 2HF. 12. Ammonia oxidation: 4NH₃ + 5O₂ → 4NO + 6H₂O (Ostwald). 13. Nitric acid action on metals: Cu + 4HNO₃(conc) → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O. 14. Sulfuric acid as dehydrating agent: C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁ + H₂SO₄ → 12C + 11H₂O. 15. Aluminum in NaOH: 2Al + 2NaOH + 6H₂O → 2Na[Al(OH)₄] + 3H₂.
