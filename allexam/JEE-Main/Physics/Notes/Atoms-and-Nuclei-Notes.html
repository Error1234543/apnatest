Important facts

  • नाभिक का व्यास ~10⁻¹⁴ m, परमाण्र का ~10⁻¹⁰ m; नाभिकीय घनत्व लगभग सर्वत्र समान (~2.3×10¹⁷ kg/m³)
  • बोर त्रिज्या a₀ = 0.529 Å = 5.29×10⁻¹¹ m; नाभिकीय त्रिज्या सूत्र R = R₀A^(1/3), R₀ ≈ 1.2 fm
  • हाइड्रोजन ऊर्जा स्तर: E_n = −13.6 Z²/n² eV; H-α (6563 Å), Ly-α (1216 Å), Lyman limit (912 Å), Balmer limit (3646 Å)
  • α-कण प्रकीर्जन: impact parameter b = (Z₁Z₂e²/4πε₀K)cot(θ/2); closest approach r₀ = Z₁Z₂e²/4πε₀K
  • क्षय नियम: N = N₀e^(−λt); T₁/₂ = 0.693/λ; τ = 1/λ = 1.44T₁/₂; n आधा-जीवन बाद N = N₀(½)^n
  • बंधन ऊर्जा: BE = Δm × 931.5 MeV; BE/A peak at Fe-56 (~8.8 MeV/nucleon)
  • β⁻-क्षय: n→p+e⁻+ν̄ₑ (Z→Z+1); β⁺-क्षय: p→n+e⁺+νₑ (Z→Z−1); γ: केवल ऊर्जा transition
  • फिशन: heavy→medium (U-235, Pu-239); फ्यूजन: light→medium (D-T, pp-chain); दोनों में BE/A बढ़ने पर energy निकलती है
  • सpectral lines count from level n: n(n−1)/2 distinct transitions possible
  • α-ray: paper stops; β-ray: Al stops; γ-ray: Pb/cm-thick required; ionising power α > β > γ

Formulas and relationships

b = (Z₁Z₂e² / 4πε₀K) cot(θ/2) — impact parameter vs scattering angle
r₀ = Z₁Z₂e² / 4πε₀K — distance of closest approach
R = R₀ A^(1/3), R₀ ≈ 1.2 fm — nuclear radius
r_n = n² a₀ / Z, a₀ = 4πε₀ℏ² / (m_e e²) ≈ 0.529 Å — Bohr radius
v_n = Z e² / (4πε₀ n ℏ) = Z α c / n — electron speed in nth orbit
E_n = −13.6 Z² / n² eV — energy of nth orbit (hydrogen-like)
K_n = +13.6 Z² / n² eV; U_n = −27.2 Z² / n² eV — KE and PE
L = nℏ = nh/2π — quantised angular momentum
μ_B = eℏ/(2m_e) ≈ 9.274×10⁻²⁴ J/T — Bohr magneton; orbital magnetic moment μ = nμ_B
1/λ = RZ² (1/n₁² − 1/n₂²), R ≈ 1.097×10⁷ m⁻¹ — Rydberg formula
N(t) = N₀ e^(−λt); T₁/₂ = ln2/λ ≈ 0.693/λ; τ = 1/λ
A = λN — activity; 1 Ci = 3.7×10¹⁰ Bq; 1 Bq = 1 decay/s
Δm = [Z m_p + (A−Z) m_n] − M_nucleus — mass defect
BE = Δm × 931.5 MeV — binding energy (using amu); alternatively BE = Δm(c²) with Δm in kg
Q = (Σm_initial − Σm_final) × 931.5 MeV — Q-value of nuclear reaction
N after n half-lives: N = N₀ (½)^n; remaining fraction = (½)^(t/T₁/₂)
E_photon = hν = hc/λ = ΔE — photon energy from electronic transition
λ_dB = h/p = h/√(2mK) — de Broglie wavelength of particle with KE K
T_orbit = 2πr/v ∝ n³/Z³ — orbital period in nth Bohr orbit
