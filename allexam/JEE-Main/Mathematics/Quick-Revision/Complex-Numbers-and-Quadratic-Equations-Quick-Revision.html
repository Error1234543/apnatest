Complex Numbers and Quadratic Equations — Quick Revision (JEE Main)

JEE MainHindiQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Complex Number: Ek number jo a + ib ke roop mein likha jaye, jahan a aur b real numbers hain aur i = √(-1). Yahan a ko 'real part' (Re(z)) aur b ko 'imaginary part' (Im(z)) kehte hain.
  • Argand Plane: Complex numbers ko geometrically darshane ke liye xy-plane ka upyog karte hain, jahan x-axis real part aur y-axis imaginary part represent karti hai. Isse Argand plane ya complex plane kehte hain.
  • Modulus: z = a + ib ka modulus |z| = √(a² + b²) hota hai. Ye z ka origin se arthik doori hai.
  • Argument (Amplitude): z = a + ib ka argument arg(z) = θ hota hai, jahan tan θ = b/a. Principal value θ ∈ (-π, π] hoti hai.
  • Conjugate: z = a + ib ka conjugate z̄ = a - ib hota hai. Geometrically, ye real axis ke prati symmetric hota hai.
  • Quadratic Equation: ax² + bx + c = 0, jahan a ≠ 0. Iske roots α, β hote hain.
  • Cube Roots of Unity: 1, ω, ω², jahan ω = (-1 + i√3)/2. Inme ω³ = 1 aur 1 + ω + ω² = 0.

Key concepts

  • Euler's Form: z = r(cos θ + i sin θ) = re^(iθ), jahan r = |z| aur θ = arg(z). Isse multiplication/division bahut easy ho jati hai.
  • De Moivre's Theorem: (cos θ + i sin θ)^n = cos(nθ) + i sin(nθ). Roots nikalne me iska upyog hota hai.
  • Algebraic Properties: z₁ ± z₂ = (a₁±a₂) + i(b₁±b₂); z₁·z₂ = (a₁a₂-b₁b₂) + i(a₁b₂+a₂b₁); z₁/z₂ = z₁·z̄₂/|z₂|²
  • Triangle Inequality: |z₁+z₂| ≤ |z₁|+|z₂| aur |z₁-z₂| ≥ ||z₁|-|z₂||. Ye JEE me often asked hota hai.
  • Locus Problems: |z-z₁| = k|z-z₂| ek circle deta hai (Apollonius circle). |z-z₁| = |z-z₂| perpendicular bisector hoti hai.
  • Quadratic with Complex Coefficients: Agar coefficients complex hain to discriminant D ≥ 0 hone se roots real nahi hote. Real roots ke liye alag conditions apply hoti hain.
  • Nature of Roots (ax²+bx+c=0, a,b,c real): (i) D > 0 → distinct real roots; (ii) D = 0 → equal roots; (iii) D < 0 → complex conjugate roots; (iv) D > 0 aur perfect square → rational roots; (v) irrational coefficient → roots irrational pair.
  • Sum and Product of Roots: α + β = -b/a, αβ = c/a. Roots: x = [-b ± √(b²-4ac)]/2a.
  • Common Roots: Agar ax²+bx+c=0 aur a'x²+b'x+c'=0 ka ek common root α ho, to (a c' - a'c)/(a b'-a'b) = (a'b-ab')/(b c'-b'c). Dono common roots ke liye Δ/Δ' = 0.
  • Location of Roots: f(x)=ax²+bx+c leke f(α)·f(β) ka sign check karke roots ki position bata sakte hain. Condition: D≥0, -b/2a ∈ (α,β), aur af(α) > 0, af(β) > 0.
  • Graphical Rep of Quadratic Expression: y = ax²+bx+c ek parabola hai. a>0 ho to upar mukh, a<0 ho to neeche mukh. Vertex (-b/2a, -D/4a).
  • Quadratic Expression ka Min/Max: a>0 ke liye minimum value -D/4a (vertex par); a<0 ke liye maximum value -D/4a.

Important facts

  • ω³ = 1, 1 + ω + ω² = 0, ω̄ = ω². Powers cycle: ω^(3n)=1, ω^(3n+1)=ω, ω^(3n+2)=ω².
  • n-th roots of unity: 1^(1/n) = 1, ω, ω²,..., ω^(n-1). Ye ek regular n-gon banate hain unit circle par.
  • n-th roots of -1: (-1)^(1/n) = -1, -ω, -ω²,... agar n odd; agar n even to different set.
  • If one root of ax²+bx+c=0 is irrational p+√q then other is p-√q (rational coefficients condition).
  • If one root is a+ib then other is a-ib (real coefficients condition).
  • |z₁z₂| = |z₁|·|z₂|; |z₁/z₂| = |z₁|/|z₂|; |zⁿ| = |z|ⁿ.
  • z + z̄ = 2Re(z); z - z̄ = 2i Im(z); z·z̄ = |z|².
  • Argument principal range: (-π, π]. Sometimes written as [0, 2π) depending on convention.
  • Polar form multiplication: r₁e^(iθ₁) · r₂e^(iθ₂) = r₁r₂e^(i(θ₁+θ₂)). Division: divide moduli, subtract arguments.
  • Condition for three points z₁,z₂,z₃ to be collinear: (z₃-z₁)/(z₂-z₁) is real.
  • Equation of line through z₁,z₂: (z-z₁)/(z₂-z₁) = (z̄-z̄₁)/(z̄₂-z̄₁).
  • Equation of circle with diameter endpoints z₁,z₂: |z-z₁| = |z-z₂|... No, diameter circle: (z-z₁)(z̄-z̄₂)+(z̄-z̄₁)(z-z₂)=0 or |z-(z₁+z₂)/2| = |z₁-z₂|/2.
  • For equation ax²+bx+c=0, if roots are in ratio m:n then mc²+ma²=b(ac)... use condition: (α/β)=(m/n).

Memory aids

  • ω cycle yaad rakho: 1 → ω → ω² → 1. 'One Whole ω!' means ω³=1.
  • 1+ω+ω²=0 → 'W-O-I = Nothing' (We Obtain Identity = 0)... just remember: all three cube roots add to zero.
  • De Moivre: 'Copy the angle, multiply by n' — cos(nθ)+i sin(nθ).
  • Triangle inequality: 'Direct path shortest' — straight line (|z₁+z₂|) ≤ going via origin path (|z₁|+|z₂|).
  • For quadratic min/max: 'a>0 go down to bottom (min), a<0 go up to top (max)' — vertex y-coordinate = -D/4a.
  • Conjugate property: 'Same real, opposite imaginary' — like mirror image across real axis.
  • Modulus formula: 'Pythagoras for complex' — |z| = √(a²+b²), same as distance formula.

Exam tips

  • JEE Main me Complex Numbers se 1-2 questions regularly aate hain. Modulus-argument aur De Moivre se direct numerical aate hain — practice karo.
  • Cube roots of unity wale problems ko shortcut se solve karo: ω powers ko 3 se divide karke remainder lo.
  • Quadratic equations me 'condition for common root' wale formula yaad rakho: (ca'-c'a)/(a'b-ab') = (ab'-a'b)/(bc'-b'c).
  • Nature of roots wale MCQ me pehle discriminant calculate karo, phir options match karo — time saving.
  • Locus problems me geometric interpretation socho: |z-z₁|=k circle, perpendicular bisector, line — diagram draw karo.
  • Argument problems me quadrant ka khayal rakho: arctan(b/a) always -π/2 se π/2 deta hai, actual angle usse alag ho sakta hai.
  • Quick revision ke liye har formula ko ek example ke saath likho. Repeated practice se retention badhta hai.
  • Pythagorean triples ko complex numbers me use karo: |3+4i| = 5, |6+8i| = 10 — modulus calculations fast hongi.

Common traps

  • arg(z) ki principal value negative bhi ho sakti hai (4th quadrant me). Always check quadrant before taking arctan(b/a).
  • √(-a)·√(-b) = √(ab) galat hai generally. √(-a) = i√a, so product = -√(ab).
  • Quadratic equation ke roots 'real' maanjhne ke liye D≥0 zaroori hai, lekin complex coefficients me ye condition sufficient nahi.
  • Common root wala problem me cross-multiplication formula yaad rakhna: α = (bc'-b'c)/(c'a-ca') = (c'a-ca')/(ab'-a'b)... actually: α = (bk-cl)/(bl-bk) — use determinant method safely.
  • Location of roots problem me sirf f(α)·f(β)>0 dekhna kaafi nahi — D≥0 aur axis condition bhi check karo.
  • n-th roots of unity nikalte waqt general form: e^(i·2kπ/n), k=0,1,...,n-1. k=n yaad rakhna mat — bas n roots hote hain.
  • |z₁+z₂| = |z₁|+|z₂| tabhi hota hai jab z₁/z₂ real aur positive ho. Otherwise strict inequality.
  • Quadratic expression ax²+bx+c ka minimum value tabhi -D/4a hota hai jab D≥0 (real roots exist karein). If D<0 aur a>0, expression always positive, no real minimum at vertex within reals.
