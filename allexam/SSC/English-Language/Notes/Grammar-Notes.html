Tenses (काल) – पूर्ण अर्थ एवं संरचना

English में तिन मुख्य काल होते हैं – Past (भूतकाल), Present (वर्तमानकाल) और Future (भविष्यकाल)। प्रत्येक के चार रूप होते हैं: Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous। कुल मिलकर 12 तenses बनाते हैं जो किसी भी क्रिया के समय, दशा और अवधि को दर्शाते हैं। SSC परीक्षा में tense identification, error spotting और sentence improvement से रोज़ाना 2-4 प्रश्न पूछे जाते हैं, इसलिए प्रत्येक tense की formation और usage की स्पष्ट समझ अनिवार्य है।

Present Simple Tense: यह नियमित/साधारण कार्य या सत्य बताता है। Structure: Subject + V1 (third person singular में +s/es)। उदाहरण: 'She reads a book daily.' या 'Water boils at 100°C.'। Signal words: always, usually, often, every day। Present Continuous: चल रहे कार्य के लिए, Structure: Subject + am/is/are + V1+ing। उदाहरण: 'They are playing football now.' Signal: now, at present, currently। Present Perfect: बीते कार्य का वर्तमान से संबंध, Structure: Subject + has/have + V3। उदाहरण: 'He has finished his work.' Signal: already, just, yet, ever, never, recently। Present Perfect Continuous: कार्य की शुरुआत अतीत में हुई और अभी भी चल रहा है, Structure: Subject + has/have + been + V1+ing। उदाहरण: 'I have been waiting for two hours.' Signal: for, since, lately।

Past Simple Tense: पूर्ण अतीत का कार्य, Structure: Subject + V2। उदाहरण: 'Ravi went to Delhi yesterday.' Signal: yesterday, last week, in 1995, ago। Past Continuous: अतीत में किसी क्षण पर चल रहा कार्य, Structure: Subject + was/were + V1+ing। उदाहरण: 'She was studying when I called.' Signal: while, at 8 PM yesterday, when। Past Perfect: 'अतीत का अतीत', दो बीते कार्यों में पहले हुए कार्य के लिए, Structure: Subject + had + V3। उदाहरण: 'They had left before I arrived.' Signal: by the time, already, before। Past Perfect Continuous: कार्य की शुरुआत अतीत में हुई थी और कुछ समय तक लगातार चला, Structure: Subject + had + been + V1+ing। उदाहरण: 'He had been working there for five years before he resigned.' Signal: for, since, before।

Future Simple Tense: भविष्य में होने वाला कार्य, Structure: Subject + will/shall + V1। उदाहरण: 'I will come tomorrow.' Signal: tomorrow, next year, soon। Future Continuous: भविष्य में किसी विशिष्ट समय पर चल रहा कार्य, Structure: Subject + will be + V1+ing। उदाहरण: 'At this time tomorrow, I will be flying to Mumbai।' Signal: at this time tomorrow, all day tomorrow। Future Perfect: भविष्य की एक निर्দিष्ट तिथि से पहले पूर्ण हो जाने वाला कार्य, Structure: Subject + will have + V3। उदाहरण: 'By next year, she will have graduated।' Signal: by the time, by next year, before। Future Perfect Continuous: कार्य की शुरुआत भविष्य में किसी बिंदु से पूर्व हो चुकी होगी और लगातार चलेगा, Structure: Subject + will have been + V1+ing। उदाहरण: 'By December, I will have been teaching here for ten years।' Signal: by...for/since। SSC में tense change का प्रश्न प्रायः Narration (Direct-Indirect) के साथ जुड़कर आता है; वहां present simple → past simple, present continuous → past continuous, present perfect → past perfect इत्यादि का नियम याद रखें।

Voice (वाच्य) – Active एवं Passive

Voice से तात्पर्य उस संरचना से है जिसमें主语 (subject) क्रिया के प्रति active या passive भूमिका निभाता है। Active Voice में主语 स्वयं क्रिया करता है; Passive Voice में主语 क्रिया का कर्म होता है और क्रिया के साथ be के रूप + V3 आते हैं। SSC में direct transformation और error spotting दोनों प्रकार के प्रश्न आते हैं।

Transformation के सामान्य नियम:
1. Object को Subject बनाएं।
2. Main verb के सही tense के अनुसार be का रूप चुनें (is/am/are → was/were/have been/had been आदि) और उसके बाद V3 लगाएं।
3. Original subject को by के साथ object बना दें (optional जब agent अज्ञात या irrelevant हो)।

Tense-wise उदाहरण:
- Present Simple: 'Ram writes a letter.' → 'A letter is written by Ram.'
- Present Continuous: 'She is cooking food.' → 'Food is being cooked by her.'
- Present Perfect: 'They have completed the task.' → 'The task has been completed by them.'
- Past Simple: 'Rahul broke the window.' → 'The window was broken by Rahul.'
- Past Continuous: 'We were watching TV.' → 'TV was being watched by us.'
- Past Perfect: 'She had read the book.' → 'The book had been read by her.'
- Future Simple: 'I will help you.' → 'You will be helped by me.'
- Modal (can, could, may, might, shall, should, will, would, must): 'You must follow the rule.' → 'The rule must be followed by you.'

विशेष मामले:
1. Intransitive verbs (जैसे sleep, die, go, happen) का passive नहीं बनता, जबकि कुछ transitive इन्हें भी passive ले सकते हैं जैसे 'He died a natural death' → 'A natural death was died by him' (गलत); वास्तव में 'He died a natural death' passive नहीं होता, लेकिन 'The man died quickly' → नहीं। 'Some intransitive verbs taking objects' जैसी cases में देखें: 'She laughed at him' → 'He was laughed at by her' (सही)।
2. Imperative sentences: 'Open the door.' → 'Let the door be opened.'
3. Exclamatory/Sentences with 'Let': 'Let me go.' → 'I may be allowed to go.'
4. Questions: 'Do you like tea?' → 'Is tea liked by you?' 'What did he say?' → 'What was said by him?'
5. Passive में be के रूप tense के अनुसार बदलते हैं, परंतु modal के साथ modal ही रहता है।
6. 'Have something done' structure (causative) → उसका passivization अलग होता है, जैसे 'I had my hair cut.' → 'My hair was cut by someone' या 'I had my hair cut' नहीं बदलता सामान्यतः।

SSC में error spotting में passive misuse अक्सर देखने को मिलता है, उदाहरण: 'The book was wrote by him' (गलत, wrote → written)।

Narration (आख्यात) – Direct & Indirect Speech

Direct Speech में वक्ता के शब्दQuote में आते हैं; Indirect (Reported) Speech में उनका अर्थ बतलाया जाता है बिना Quotes के। SSC में transformation और error दोनों आते हैं।

मूल नियम:
1. Reporting verb (say, tell, ask, reply आदि) के बाद that/whether/if जोड़ें। 'Tell' के बाद हमेशा object रहता है (tell someone), 'Say' के बिना object के भी चल सकता है।
2. Tense change: Reporting verb Past में हो तो interior tense past की ओर बढ़ते हैं; Present/Future में हो तो change नहीं होता।
- Present Simple → Past Simple
- Present Continuous → Past Continuous
- Present Perfect → Past Perfect
- Past Simple → Past Perfect
- Past Continuous → Past Perfect Continuous
- Past Perfect → वही रहता है
- Future (will) → would
- Modals: can→could, may→might, shall→should, will→would, must→had to/must (संदर्भ)
3. Pronoun change: First person根据 reporting subject, third person根据 reported person। उदाहरण: Ram says, "I am happy." → Ram says that he is happy. (tense unchanged क्योंकि reporting verb present है)
4. Time/Place expressions बदलते हैं: today→that day, yesterday→the previous day, tomorrow→the next day, here→there, this→that, these→those, ago→before, now→then आदि।

Exceptions (जहां tense change नहीं होता):
- Universal truths: "The sun rises in the east." → He said that the sun rises in the east.
- Historical facts
- Habits expressed with present simple
- When reporting verb is in present/future

Commands/Requests/Advice:
- Command: "Go away." → He ordered me to go away.
- Request: "Please help me." → He requested me to help him.
- Advice: "You should work hard." → He advised me to work hard.
- Prohibition: "Don't smoke." → He forbade me to smoke.

Questions:
- Yes/No questions: whether/if + Subject + Verb। उदाहरण: "Are you coming?" → He asked whether I was coming.
- Wh- questions: wh- word + Subject + Verb। उदाहरण: "Where do you live?" → He asked where I lived.
- Alternative questions: or
- Reported question में inversion नहीं होता; normal statement order रहता है।

Common pitfalls SSC में:
1. Reporting verb tense को ignore करना और फिर भी tense बदल देना।
2. Time expressions को बदलना जब reporting verb present/future हो।
3. 'Said to' को 'told' में बदलते समय object न लगाना: He said to me → He told me (सही)।

Subject-Verb Agreement (कर्ता-क्रिया सर्वसम्ति)

Subject-Verb Agreement का अर्थ है कि sentence का verb subject के साथ number (singular/plural) और person के अनुसरण में हो। SSC में यह rule-प्रधान topic है और error spotting में बारम्बार पूछा जाता है।

मुख्य नियम:
1. Singular subject के साथ singular verb, plural subject के साथ plural verb। उदा. 'He runs', 'They run'。
2. Compound subject joined by 'and' → plural verb: 'Rama and Sita are coming'。
3. Except quando 'and' दो noun जोड़ता है जो single entity माने जाते हैं (bread and butter is..., fish and chips is..., war and peace is...)
4. Each, every, either, neither के साथ singular verb: 'Each of the boys has a book.'
5. 'One of the + plural noun + relative clause' → relative clause का verb singular होता है क्योंकि 'one' subject है: 'One of the students who have qualified is absent' → यहाँ 'who have qualified' plural है? Wait: rule: 'One of the students who are qualified comes' → actually, relative clause refers to students, so plural: 'who are qualified'. लेकिन main verb singular: 'One ... comes'. SSC में confusion हो सकती है; clear rule: One of the + plural noun + who/which/that + plural verb; main verb singular।
6. Collective nouns (team, committee, government, family, jury, audience) → जब group को single unit मानें singular, जब members को separate मानें plural: 'The team is winning' vs 'The team are arguing among themselves'। SSC में सामान्यतः singular prefer किया जाता है unless context明确要求 plural।
7. 'There is/are' और 'Here is/are': verb subsequent noun के साथ agree करता है: 'There is a pen on the table', 'There are pens on the table'。
8. Indefinite pronouns: all, some, none, most → depend on of-phrase: 'All of the water is gone', 'All of the students are here'。
9. Titles, names of books, movies, countries ( singular even if plural-looking): 'The Great Gatsby is a novel', 'India is a vast country'。
10. 'News', 'physics', 'mathematics', 'economics' जैसे subjects singular verb लेते हैं: 'Physics is difficult'。
11. Neither/nor, either/or, not only/but also → verb nearest subject के साथ agree करता है: 'Neither the boys nor the girl is coming'。
12. 'A number of' + plural noun → plural verb ('A number of students are...'), 'The number of' → singular ('The number of students is large')。

SSC में frequently tested traps:
- 'Each of the boys have' (गलत → has)
- 'Ten miles is a long distance' (सही, distance singular)
- 'Ramesh as well as Suresh is coming' (verb with Ramesh, singular)
- 'Brother and sister are' (plural because two persons), 'The brother and sister of my friend is' (एक ही व्यक्ति? confuse; better avoid).

Parts of Speech (व्याकरणिक पद) – प्रकार, लक्षण एवं पहचान

English में आठ भाग होते हैं: Noun, Pronoun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Preposition, Conjunction, Interjection। SSC में identification और usage दोनों प्रकार के प्रश्न आते हैं।

1. Noun (सर्वनाम/विषयपद): Person, place, thing, idea का नाम। Types: Proper (Delhi), Common (city), Collective (team), Material (gold), Abstract (honesty)। Noun का role: subject, object, complement। Plural formation: regular (-s, -es), irregular (child→children, mouse→mice), invariant (sheep, deer), compound nouns (passengers)。 Possessive case: 'the boy's book'। Nouns that look plural but are singular: news, measles, gymnastics। Nouns that are always plural: scissors, trousers, glasses (pair)।

2. Pronoun (सर्वा名词): Noun की जगह आने वाला शब्द। Types: Personal (I, you, he, she, it, we, they), Reflexive (myself, yourself), Reciprocal (each other, one another), Relative (who, which, that), Interrogative (who, whom, which, what), Indefinite (somebody, anyone, everything)। Case: subjective, objective, possessive। Rule: subject case जब noun का काम करे, object case जब object हो। 'Who' vs 'Whom': who subject, whom object। Informal में whom का प्रयोग कम, परंतु SSC formal में पूछता है।

3. Verb (क्रिया): Action या state expression करने वाली। Types: Auxiliary (be, do, have – helps main verb), Modal (can, could, may, might, shall, should, will, would, must – express ability, permission, obligation, possibility), Transitive (object लेती है), Intransitive (object नहीं लेती), Linking verb (be, seem, become – subject को describe करते हैं)। Voice और tense verb से जुड़े हैं। Verb forms: V1, V2, V3, V-ing। Irregular verbs के forms याद रखें। Agreement auxiliary verbs के साथ: 'He has done', 'They have done'।

4. Adjective (विशेषण): Noun या pronoun का गुण/विशेष बताता है। Types: Descriptive (good), Quantitative (many), Demonstrative (this), Possessive (my), Interrogative (which), Comparative/Superlative (big, bigger, biggest)। Comparison rules: one syllable → -er/-est, two syllables ending in y → -ier/-iest, more/most others। Irregular: good-better-best, bad-worse-worst, little-less-least, much-more-most, far-farther/further-farthest/furthest। Double comparison avoid करें: 'more better' गलत।

5. Adverb (क्रिया विशेषण): Verb, adjective, अन्य adverb को modify करता है। Types: Manner (quickly), Place (here), Time (now), Frequency (always), Degree (very), Reason (therefore)। Placement सामान्यतः after verb या end of clause; frequency adverbs before main verb, after auxiliary। Comparison of adverbs: fast-fastest, carefully-more carefully। Some adjectives and adverbs same form: fast, hard, late, early। 'Hard' (.adverb) vs 'Hardly' (means barely)।

6. Preposition (संबंधवाचक): Noun/pronoun के साथ relation दिखाता है। Common: in, on, at, by, with, from, to, for, about, between, among, during, through, across। Fixed phrases: good at, interested in, afraid of, dependent on, responsible for。 Often confused pairs: affect/effect, lie/lay, rise/raise, sit/set। SSC में preposition + gerund (after preposition, verb ing形式), जैसे 'by doing', 'without saying'。

7. Conjunction (संयोजक): Words, phrases, clauses को जोड़ता है। Types: Coordinating (FANBOYS: for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so), Subordinating (because, although, if, when, while, since, unless, till, as), Correlative (both...and, either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also)। Punctuation rules: comma before coordinating conjunction joining two independent clauses; no comma before subordinating conjunction除非在前半部分之後。

8. Interjection (अनुरणन): अचानक भावना व्यक्त करता है: Oh!, Alas!, Hurrah!, Wow!। ये punctuation में exclamation mark लेते हैं।

Prepositions & Conjunctions – Fixed Usage एवं Confusion Points

Prepositions (सम्बन्धवाचक) और Conjunctions (संयोजक) SSC English grammar के वे हिस्से हैं जहां mechanical memorization से अधिक contextual understanding चाहिए।

Prepositions – Important Fixed Phrases:
• Abide by, Accused of, Affected with, Agree to/with, Apply for, Ask for, Belong to, Blame on, Break into, Call on, Care for, Come across, Come to, Concentrate on, Consist of, Depend on, Die of/from, Dream of, Engaged in, Exist in, Face up to, Feel like, Fond of, Good at, Hear about, Hope for, Insist on, Interfere in/with, Join in,Laugh at, Look after, Look for, Make up, Obey the rule, Participate in, Pick up, Play with, Prevent from, Proud of, Quaranine from, Reduce to, Rely on, Result in, Search for, Suffer from, Take after, Take care of, Think of/about, Trust in, Wait for, Worry about。

Confusing Pairs:
– In vs On vs At: in (larger area, enclosed space, months/years), on (surface, days/dates, public transport), at (specific point, address, institution)。
– Between (two items), Among (more than two)。
– Beside (next to), Besides (in addition to)。
– Economic (related to economy), Economical (saving money)。
– Historic (famous in history), Historical (about history)。
– Lie (recline) vs Lay (put) vs Leigh (past of lie)。
– Rise (intransitive) vs Raise (transitive)。

Preposition + Gerund Rule: जब preposition के बाद verb आए तो उसके ing रूप में आएँगे, क्योंकि preposition का object noun/pronoun होता है, और verb को noun बनाने के लिए gerund उपयोग होता है: 'He is good at swimming', 'She left without saying anything', 'I am looking forward to meeting you' (to यहाँ preposition है, infinitive नहीं)。

Conjunctions – Subordinating Conjunctions and Clause Types:
• Reason/Cause: because, since, as, owing to the fact that。
• Time: when, while, as, before, after, until, till, since, as soon as。
• Condition: if, unless, provided that, as long as, on condition that。
• Contrast: although, though, even though, whereas, while。
• Purpose: so that, in order that, lest。

Correlative Conjunctions – Parallelism अनिवार्य:
'Both Rama and Sita are coming' (correct parallelism)。 'Not only he is intelligent but also hardworking' (गलत: Not only is he intelligent but he is also hardworking)。
'Either you or I am wrong' (verb agrees with nearer subject)。
'She neither sings nor dances' (parallel verb forms)。

Error spotting में conjunction misuse के उदाहरण:
• 'Despite of his illness...' → Despite his illness / In spite of his illness。
• 'He worked hard so that he could pass' → correct; 'He worked hard so he could pass' → colloquial but acceptable in informal, SSC may prefer 'so that' in formal writing।
• 'Although he is poor but he is honest' → Although he is poor, he is honest (double conjunction गलत)。

SSC में कभी-कभी 'conjunction + preposition' combination जैसे 'except for', 'aside from', 'instead of' पूछे जाते हैं।

Articles (निर्धारक) – a, An, The एवं शून्यArticle

Articles निर्दिष्टता (definiteness) और generalization दर्शाते हैं। English में तीन articles हैं: a/an (indefinite) और the (definite)। कुछ contexts में article नहीं लगाया जाता (zero article)। SSC में article usage और omission दोनों test होते हैं।

Indefinite Article 'a' vs 'an':
• 'a' consonant sound के पहले: a book, a university (you sound), a one-way street (w sound)。
• 'an' vowel sound के पहले: an apple, an hour (h silent), an MBA degree (em sound)。
• 'a' before abbreviations जिनका पहला sound consonant हो: a NASA scientist (en sound); 'an' before vowel sound: an FBI agent (ef sound)。
• 'a' vs 'an' का आधार spelling नहीं, sound है।

Definite Article 'the':
• Unique things: the sun, the moon, the earth, the internet, the world。
• Superlatives and ordinals: the best, the first, the highest mountain。
• Previously mentioned noun: I saw a dog. The dog was black。
• Specific group: the Indian team, the people of India。
• Geographical features: the Ganges, the Himalayas, the Pacific Ocean, the Sahara Desert (singular lake: Lake Victoria, no the)。
• Newspapers, hotels, restaurants: the Times of India, the Taj Mahal Hotel。
• Musical instruments: play the piano, the guitar。
• Plural nouns泛指 특정 집단을 나타낼 때: the rich, the poor, the unemployed (abstract concept)。

Zero Article (No Article):
• General plural nouns and uncountable nouns: Dogs are loyal. I like music. (General sense)।
• Meals: breakfast, lunch, dinner (unless specified: the breakfast we had yesterday)。
• Languages, academic subjects: English, Mathematics (unless referring to specific course: the English I studied)。
• Names of continents, countries (most), cities, streets, parks: Asia, India, Delhi, Rajpath, Central Park。
• Cards, sports, meals: play chess, have dinner, call someone by phone。
• Abstract nouns in general sense: Love is blind. Honesty is the best policy।
• With titles before names: President Ram, Doctor Sharma, Professor Gupta (unless followed by surname only in some contexts: Professor Gupta vs Prof. Gupta)。
• Fixed phrases: go to school/college/prison/church (purpose), at home, in bed, by car, on foot, day by day, side by side。

Errors commonly spotted:
• 'He is the honest man' → He is an honest man (first mention, general description)。
• 'The Mahatma Gandhi was born...' → Mahatma Gandhi was born... (proper name前面不加the，除非是特定结构如 'the Mahatma Gandhi of history' but通常不加)。
• 'I will return after the two days' → after two days (no article with time expressions)。
• 'The information are wrong' → Information is wrong (uncountable)。
• 'He is in the hospital' (Indian English often omits the for purpose) → He is in hospital (UK) / He is in the hospital (US meaning as patient) contextdependent；SSC通常接受无冠词表目的。

Sentence Transformation – Improvements, Reordering, Substitution

SSC में Sentence Improvement, Replacement, और Para Jumbles जैसे प्रश्न पूछे जाते हैं जिनके लिए structural accuracy और logical flow का ज्ञान आवश्यक है।

Sentence Improvement:
• Focus on grammar errors (tense, agreement, article, preposition, voice, narration)।
• Idioms and phrasal verbs के सही रूप। उदाहरण: 'He is addicted to drink' → 'He is addicted to drinking' (addicted to + gerund)。
• Redundancy remove करें: 'free gift' → gift (gift inherently free)。
• Misplaced modifiers: 'I saw a man with a telescope' → ambiguous; clarify।
• Comparisons: 'more better' (गलत) → better; 'most unique' (गलत) → unique (absolute adjective)。
• Diction: 'discuss about' (गलत) → discuss; 'paste paper' (गलत) → stick paper; 'enter into' (when enter is transitive) → enter the room。
• Formal vs informal: 'gonna', 'wanna' writing में नहीं आएँगे。

Para Jumbles (Sentence Rearrangement):
• Techniques:
1. Identify the opening sentence – usually introduces topic, no pronoun reference।
2. Find pairings based on pronouns (he, she, it, they, this, that) – they refer to earlier nouns。
3. Chronological/logical sequence – cause→effect, problem→solution, general→specific。
4. Consecutive markers – however, therefore, meanwhile, subsequently, first, second。
5. Repetition of keywords – linked ideas。
6. Eliminate options using fixed pairs。
• Example: A. He returned home. B. After finishing his work. C. It was late at night. D. He felt exhausted. Logical order: B-C-A-D (After finishing his work, it was late at night. He returned home. He felt exhausted.)
• Practice identifying the 'anchor' sentence。

Sentence Substitution (one word replaces phrase):
• 'A person who loves books' → Bibliophile
• 'One who cannot see' → Blind
• 'Without fear' → Fearless
• 'Difficult to pronounce' → Laboured (not exactly) → Prestibular? No: 'Hard to pronounce' → Ineffable? Better:难读的字词有专门术语但SSC不常考，重点在常见词汇替换。
• Common substitutions tested: 'Very hungry' → Famished, 'Speak softly' → Murmur, 'Feel disgust' → Loathe, 'Give birth to' → Bear, 'Make a profit' → Gain, 'Die violently' → Perish。
• These are vocabulary-heavy; still under grammar umbrella because they test precise usage。

Error Detection Strategy:
1. Read sentence aloud mentally, listen for awkwardness。
2. Check subject-verb agreement first。
3. Verify tense consistency。
4. Look for article/preposition misuse。
5. Confirm pronoun reference clarity。
6. Check comparative/superlative form。
7. Identify any fragment or run-on sentence。

Repeated patterns in SSC: 'Each of the boys have' (has), 'The number of students are' (is), 'She said to me that...' (told me), 'He is older than me' (I – formal), 'He is taller than I' (correct formal)但口语可接受。SSC有时接受than me在非正式但考试中prefer than I。

Error Spotting –高频考点与解题技巧

Error Spotting (निर्देश त्रुटि पहचान) में दिए गए sentence के किसी भाग में व्याकरणिक त्रुटि होती है या 'No Error' होता है। SSC में यह सबसे महत्वपूर्ण खंड है और रोज़ 3-5 प्रश्न आते हैं।

सामान्यतः出现的错误类型：
1. Subject-verb disagreement（见前节）。
2. Tense inconsistency – sentence में multiple clauses हों तो tense match होना चाहिए：'He said that he is ill'（गलत）→ 'He said that he was ill'（क्योंकि reporting verb past है）。
3. Wrong preposition – fix phrases या contextually inappropriate preposition。
4. Article misuse – missing या extra article, especially with unique nouns, geographical names, meals, institutions。
5. Pronoun case错误 – 'between you and I'（गलत）→ 'between you and me'（object of preposition）。
6. Double negative – 'I don't need no help'（informal）→ formal में avoided；SSC में error माना जाता है。
7. Incorrect comparative/superlative – 'more better', 'most best', 'more unique'（unique absolutes）。
8. Misplaced/dangling modifier – 'Walking down the street, the trees looked beautiful'（trees不能walk）→ 'As I walked down the street, the trees looked beautiful'。
9. Unnecessary words/redundancy – 'advance notice'（notice本身就是advance），'end result'（result inherently final），'future plans'（plans inherently future）。
10. Countable/uncountable confusion – 'informations', 'advices', 'furnitures'（all wrong）；correct: information, advice, furniture。
11. Word form错误 – adjective vs adverb：'He runs quick'（quickly），'She sang beautiful'（beautifully）；noun vs verb：'decision' vs 'decide'，'success' vs 'succeed'。
12. Conjunction错误 – double conjunction：'although...but'，'because...so'；missing conjunction導致sentence fragment。
13. Modal misuse – 'must to'（गलत）→ must + base verb；'should has'（गलत）→ should have。
14. Passive voice error – incorrect past participle：'was wrote'（written），'has been saw'（seen）。
15. Reported speech错误 – tense shift omission या pronoun/time error（见Narration）。

解题策略：
•先看整体句意，再逐一检查上述类别。
•注意划线部分（如果题目给出）或不划线全部检查。
•消除明显错误的选项后，比较剩余选项细微差别。
•熟记常见不规则动词过去分词形式和固定搭配。
•每天练习20-30题以提升准确率。

典型例子：
- 'The police are searching for the missing child.'（police集体名词，复数动词正确）
- 'Neither the teacher nor the students were present.'（verb agree with nearer subject students → were正确）
- 'He has been living here since ten years.'（since + point in time；for + duration → for ten years）
- 'She is accustomed to wake up early.'（accustomed to + gerund → waking）
- 'The news are shocking.'（news不可数，单数 → is）
- 'Each of the girls have a doll.'（each单数 → has）

常用易混淆词對與詞性轉換

SSC English grammar में frequently confused pairs और word formation (prefix/suffix) के प्रश्न आते हैं। इन्हें याद रखना जरूरी है।

易混淆词对：
• Affect (v.) vs Effect (n.) – 'The medicine affected him' vs 'The effect was immediate'。Effect也可以作动词('to effect change')。
• Altar (祭坛) vs Alter (改变)。
• Beside (旁边) vs Besides (此外)。
• Complement (补充) vs Compliment (赞美)。
• Council (委员会) vs Counsel (法律顾问) vs Consel(拼写错误)。
• Discrete (分离的) vs Discreet (谨慎的)。
• Emigrate (出国) vs Immigrate (入境) – em- out, im- in。
• Ensure (确保) vs Assure (使确信) vs Insure (保险)。
• Historic (历史性的) vs Historical (历史的)。
• Implying (暗示) vs Implies (意味着) – implication n.。
• Lay (放置) vs Lie (躺) vs Leigh (躺的过去式) – lay(laid,laid)是及物，lie(lay,lain)是不及物。
• Lose (丢失) vs Loose (宽松的)。
• Principal (校长/主要的) vs Principle (原则)。
• Respectful (尊重的) vs Respectable (值得尊敬的)。
• Settle (解决/定居) vs Settle vs Settle vs Situate (位于) – 'The house is situated on the hill'。
• Their (他们的) vs There (那里) vs They're (they are)。
• Than (比较) vs Then (然后)。
• Versus (对抗) vs Virtue (美德)。

词性转换（常见后缀）：
• -tion/-sion: act→action, decide→decision, persuade→persuasion。
• -ment: develop→development, agree→agreement。
• -ance/-ence: appear→appearance, differ→difference。
• -ness: kind→kindness, happy→happiness。
• -ity: able→ability, pure→purity。
• -ful: hope→hopeful, care→careful。
• -less: hope→hopeless, care→careless。
• -ous: danger→dangerous, poison→poisonous。
• -ic: electricity→electric, photo→photographic。
• -able/-ible: read→readable, visible。
• -ly: quick→quickly, gentle→gently (但beautiful→beautifully不规则)。
注意：有些形容词加-ly变成副词，但有些以-ly结尾的是形容词如friendly, lovely, silly, lonely, cowardly等。

Prefix常用：un-(unhappy), dis-(disagree), mis-(mislead), re-(rewrite), pre-(prepare), anti-(antibiotic), sub-(submarine), over-(overcome), under-(underestimate)。

SSC में word formation के प्रश्न अक्सर vocabulary section में आते हैं， पर grammar notes में भी include किया जाता है क्योंकि correctness of word class matters for sentence structure。

Important facts

  • 12 tenses: 3 (past/present/future) × 4 aspects (simple/continuous/perfect/perfect continuous)。
  • Voice transformation requires be + V3; tense of be matches original tense。
  • Direct→Indirect: reporting verb past → interior tense shifts back one step; universal truths exempt。
  • Subject-verb agreement: compound subjects with 'and' take plural verb; each/every/either/neither take singular。
  • Collective nouns take singular when group acts as unit, plural when members act individually。
  • Preposition + gerund rule: after preposition, verb takes -ing form。
  • Articles: 'a/an' before consonant/vowel SOUND, not letter; 'the' for specific/universal/superlative/unique。
  • Zero article with general plurals, uncountables, meals, languages, institutions (purpose), geographical names (most)。
  • Error types in SSC: agreement, tense, preposition, article, pronoun case, double negative, comparatives, modifiers, word form, conjunction。
  • Fixed phrases (be good at, interested in, afraid of, dependent on, responsible for, look forward to, consist of, agree to/with) frequently tested。

Formulas and relationships

Present Simple: S + V1(s/es) + O
Present Continuous: S + am/is/are + V1+ing + O
Present Perfect: S + has/have + V3 + O
Present Perfect Continuous: S + has/have + been + V1+ing + O
Past Simple: S + V2 + O
Past Continuous: S + was/were + V1+ing + O
Past Perfect: S + had + V3 + O
Past Perfect Continuous: S + had + been + V1+ing + O
Future Simple: S + will/shall + V1 + O
Future Continuous: S + will be + V1+ing + O
Future Perfect: S + will have + V3 + O
Future Perfect Continuous: S + will have been + V1+ing + O
Passive transformation pattern: Object + be(match tense) + V3 + by + Subject
Reported speech tense shift: Present → Past, Past → Past Perfect, Future → Conditional (will→would)
Subject-verb agreement with 'each/every/either/neither': singular verb
Comparative: adj+er/more adj; Superlative: adj+est/most adj
Preposition + gerund: prep + V1+ing
Article rule: a/an before consonant/vowel SOUND; the before specific/unique/superlative
Zero article rule: general plural/uncountable, meals, languages, most geographical names

Common mistakes

  • Using plural verb with 'each of the boys' → should be singular verb (each has) 。
  • Changing tense in reported speech when reporting verb is present/future 。
  • Adding 'of' after 'despite' → despite of is wrong; use 'in spite of' 。
  • Using 'a' before vowel sound words like 'hour' (silent h) → should be 'an hour' 。
  • Double comparison: 'more better', 'most best', 'more unique' → unique is absolute, no degrees 。
  • Misusing 'affect' as noun and 'effect' as verb (common but not always) 。
  • Writing 'I and my friend' instead of 'My friend and I' as subject 。
  • Forgetting gerund after preposition: 'good in swim' → 'good at swimming' 。
  • Using 'fewer' with uncountable nouns → 'fewer' for countable, 'less' for uncountable 。
  • Confusing 'their/there/they’re' and 'your/you’re' and 'its/it’s' 。
  • Incorrect past participle of irregular verbs: write→write, buy→buyed, go→goed 。
  • Adding article before unique nouns incorrectly: 'the Sun' (correct) but 'the Mumbai' (wrong) 。
  • Mismatching pronoun case after preposition: 'between you and I' → 'between you and me' 。
  • Using 'who' as object: 'Who did you meet?' (colloquial) → 'Whom did you meet?' (formal) 。
  • Repeating conjunction: 'Although he is poor, but he is honest' → remove 'but' 。
