SSC English Language — Spotting Errors (भूक त्रुटि पहचान): Complete Notes

SSCHindiNotes

1. Spotting Errors — परिचय और परीक्षा दृष्टिकोण

SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO आदि) की Tier-1 परीक्षा में English Language के भाग में Spotting Errors एक निश्चित और उच्च-आवृत्ति प्रश्न प्रकार है। प्रत्येक प्रश्न में एक वाक्य दिया जाता है जिसके तीन भाग होते हैं — A, B, C — और कभी-कभी D (no error) भी होता है। छात्र को यह पहचानना होता है कि वाक्य का कौन-सा भाग व्याकरण या शब्द उपयोग की दृष्टि से त्रुटिपूर्ण है।

SSC के स्तर पर त्रुटियाँ मुख्य रूप से इन्हीं क्षेत्रों से आती हैं: Subject-Verb Agreement, Tense, Preposition, Article, Conjunction, Comparative Degree, Relative Pronoun, Verb Form/Voice, Redundancy, Word Usage (like/as, since/for), और Idiom/Phrase के गलत प्रयोग। ध्यान दें — अमेरिकी/ब्रिटिश स्पेलिंग भेद SSC में पूछे नहीं जाते। त्रुटि हमेशा व्याकरण नियम या अंग्रेजी के स्थापित उपयोग से टकराती है।

निराकरण की मूल रणनीति: पहले वाक्य का मूल ढाँचा पहचानो (कर्ता, क्रिया, पुरक)। फिर Tense/Agreement की जाँच करो, उसके बाद Preposition/Article/Conjunction, अंत में शब्द चयन। 'No Error' के विकल्प को तभी चुने जब सब भाग नियम-सहमति से ज्ञात हों — अक्सर पहली नज़र में सही लगने वाले वाक्यों में ही सूक्ष्म त्रुटि होती है।

2. Subject–Verb Agreement (कर्ता–क्रिया सुमेल) — नियम और त्रुटियाँ

SSC में इसी से सबसे अधिक प्रश्न आते हैं। मूल नियम: एकवचन कर्ता + एकवचन क्रिया; बहुवचन कर्ता + बहुवचन क्रिया। परीक्षा-स्तरीय बारीकियाँ:

• Compound Subject with 'and': सामान्यतः बहुवचन क्रिया चाहिए (Rama and Shyam are coming), पर यदि दोनों नाम एक ही व्यक्ति/वस्तु दर्शाते हों तो एकवचन (The poet and philosopher is here = एक ही व्यक्ति), Every man and woman has... एकवचन。
• 'Each/Either/Neither/Every + singular noun': सदैव एकवचन क्रिया (Each of the boys has..., Neither of the two cars is...)।
• 'One of the + plural noun + relative clause': relative pronoun का referent main noun नहीं, 'one' होता है → एकवचन क्रिया (One of the students who has won...). यहाँ छात्र अक्सर 'students' देख कर are लगा देते हैं — यह भूल है。
• Collective Nouns: group, team, committee, jury आदि संख्या-सूचक न हों तो एकवचन; सदस्यों के व्यक्तिगत कार्य दर्शाएँ तो बहुवचन (The committee was formed... / The committee were arguing among themselves.)。
• 'There is/are', 'Here is/are': वास्तविक कर्ता predicate में आता है — उसके वचन के अनुसार (There is a book on the table / There are many books.)。
• Title/Subject/Distance/Money/Time as singular: 'The 'Ramayana' is a great epic.', Ten kilometers is a long walk., Five hundred rupees is not enough.
• 'More than one + singular noun': एकवचन क्रिया (More than one student has failed.) — 'More than + plural' अलग है (More than two students have...).
• Inversion (Question/After 'here/there'): 'Here comes the bus.' न कि 'come'。

त्रुटि पहचानने का तरीका: कर्ता ढूँढो → उसका वचन पहचानो → क्रिया से मिलाओ। बीच में आया modifier तुम्हें भ्रमित न करें।

3. Tense Consistency (काल सन्निबद्धता)

SSC में Tense की त्रुटियाँ दो रूपों में आती हैं: (a) समय सूचक शब्दों के विरोधाभास और (b) कथन के संदर्भ के अनुसार काल का असंगत प्रयोग。

• Time signal शब्द: 'yesterday, ago, last year, in 1990' → Simple Past; 'now, at present, currently' → Present Continuous; 'since + point in time' या 'for + duration' + Present Perfect/Perfect Continuous;
गलत: I have met him yesterday. (met = past, yet = present)। सही: I met him yesterday.

• 'Since' बनाम 'For': since = starting point (since 2010, since morning); for = duration (for ten years, for hours)। Since जब clause के साथ आए तो preceding verb आमतौर पर Perfect form में (I have known her since we were children.)

• Present Perfect vs Simple Past: निश्चित अतीत समय (yesterday, last week) के साथ Present Perfect कभी न आयें। Present Perfect उन स्थितियों में जहाँ परिणाम वर्तमान से जुड़ा हो या समय निर्दिष्ट न हो (I have lost my keys = अभी भी खोई हैं)。

• Sequence of Tenses: Reporting clause past में हो तो reported clause में भी past-side shift हो (He said that he was ill.), पर Universal truth/Permanent fact के लिए present बना रहता है (He said that the earth revolves around the sun.)。

• 'Had + V3' (Past Perfect): जब दो अतीत क्रियाएँ हों तो जो पहले हुई Past Perfect, जो बाद में हुई Simple Past (When I reached the station, the train had left.) → पहले कर्म 'leave', इसलिए had left。

त्रुटि जाँच प्रक्रिया: संदर्भ का समय निर्धारित करो → time signal पहचानो → क्रिया रूप का मिलान करो।

4. Preposition Errors (संबोधनवाचक त्रुटियाँ)

Preposition SSC Spotting Errors का सबसे बार-बार आने वाला हिस्सा है। इनकी त्रुटियाँ अधिकांशतः fixed collocation (स्थिर संयोजन) से टकराती हैं।

मुख्य_fixed_collocation_ पैटर्न:
• Adjective + prep: afraid OF, good AT, interested IN, proud OF, responsible FOR, married TO (not WITH), famous FOR (reason), famous AS (role), different FROM/THAN (दोनों SSC में स्वीकार्य, पर FROM अधिक मानक),相似 TO, similar TO, superior/inferior TO (न कि THAN), capable OF, acquainted WITH, pleased WITH/AT。
• Noun + prep: reason FOR, fond OF, interest IN, hope FOR, praise FOR, fear OF, dependence/dependency ON, effect OF, cause OF。
• Verb + prep: wait FOR, look AT/FOR/AFTER/INTO, listen TO, agree TO (proposal)/WITH (person), depend ON, belong TO, consist OF, succeed IN, apologize TO (person)/FOR (thing), complain ABOUT/OF, insist ON, aim AT, rely/trust/confide IN, participate IN。
• Verb + prep + gerund (V-ing): avoid DOING, miss DONE, give UP DOING, be busy DOING, succeed IN DOING, apologize FOR DOING, look forward TO DOING (यहाँ TO preposition है, इसलिए后面 gerund)。
• Fixed phrases: by accident, on purpose, in trouble, at risk, under pressure, on fire, in hospital (Indian English), go to school/court/prison/library/church (活动时不加the), at night (not in night), by bus/car/train (mode), on foot。

त्रुटि पहचान: वाक्य में प्रत्येक preposition को अकेले न पढ़ो — उसे अपने base word (verb/adjective/noun) के साथ जोड़कर fixed pairing की जाँच करो।

5. Article Errors (निश्चयक त्रुटियाँ: a/an/the)

SSC में article की त्रुटियाँ विशेषकर जहाँ zero-article अनुच्छेद लागू होता है, वहीं घटती हैं।

• Zero article (कोई article न लगे): plural/general sense में nouns, abstract nouns general sense में, meals, languages, subjects, proper nouns (most), planets/stars/countries/places नाम, 'by + transport', institutions when used for primary purpose (go to school/prison/church/court/hospital)。
गलत: I am studying the English. / He is in the prison. / She goes to the school to teach. (अगर teaching ही उद्देश्य है)。

• 'A/An' का चुनाव vowel SOUND पर निर्भर करता है, letter पर नहीं: an hour (h mute), a university (/j/ sound), an MBA, an X-ray。

• 'The' का प्रयोग: unique things (the sun, the moon), superlatives (the best), ordinals (the first), previously mentioned noun, specific group (the poor, the rich), rivers/mountains/ranges/oceans (the Ganga, the Himalayas, the Pacific)।

• Paired nouns: father and son, heart and soul, body and mind — पहली बार आने पर the दोनों के साथ (The father and son were arrested = दो व्यक्ति; Father and son are fighting = साधारण संबंध)。

त्रुटि जाँच: क्या noun countable है? specific/general? पहले से चर्चित? institutional use? plural generic? इन प्रश्नों के उत्तर से article तय होता है।

6. Relative Pronoun और ClauseErrors

SSC में who/whom/whose/which/that का भ्रमित करने वाला प्रयोग अक्सर पूछा जाता है。

• Who (subject), Whom (object), Whose (possession), Which (things/animals), That (restrictive clauses for both persons/things — non-restrictive में प्रयोग नहीं)。
गलत: The man which I met... / The book that I borrowed it... (relative clause में redundant object)。

• Redundant object in relative clause: यदि relative pronoun ही object है तो secondary object मत लगाओ (The boy whom I met __ his brother is a doctor → The boy whose brother...). 'That I bought it' गलत, 'That I bought' सही。

• Restrictive vs Non-restrictive: Non-restrictive (commas वाली) में that不能使用, जो person है उसका object form 'whom' prefer किया जाता है (My uncle, who lives in Delhi, ...)

• 'Such...that' vs 'So...that': such + noun (such a nice day that...), so + adj/adverb (so nice a day that...)। SSC में दोनों पैटर्न पूछे जा सकते हैं。

• As vs Which: 'As' non-restrictive clause में whole sentence के reference के लिए चलता है (As expected, ...), पर position flexibile होती है; 'Which' केवल non-restrictive में comes after comma。

त्रुटि जाँच: relative pronoun clause के अंदर subject या object में से क्या कर रहा है? redundant element तो नहीं? clause restrictive है या non-restrictive?

7. Comparative and Superlative Errors (अतुलनात्मक त्रुटियाँ)

Comparative/Superlative में SSC दो प्रकार की त्रुटियाँ पूछता है — form की और usage की。

• Double comparison 금지: more better, most best, more smarter, less smaller — सभी गलत। better/best, worse/worst, smarter/smallest ये inherently comparative/superlative हैं, इनके साथ again 비교급 접미사 안Attachment。

• Than vs When: comparative के बाद always THAN आता है (better than, more than, no sooner ... THAN), WHEN comparative context में नहीं आता। No sooner did he arrive THAN it started raining.

• Either/Both/NEITHER +_singular_ noun: neither of the two, either of the two — comparative में both/neither/any के संदर्भ सटीक होना चाहिए。

• Comparatives with of the two: 'the' compulsory (He is the taller of the two brothers.) Two-item comparison में superlative also possible with 'the' अगर context whole set की ओर इशारा करे。

• Irregular forms की याद: good-better-best, bad-worse-worst, far-farther/further-farthest/furthest, little-less-least, many/much-more-most, late-later/latest/last。

• 'More of a + noun': meaningful idiom (He is more of a poet than a politician.)

त्रुटि जाँच: क्या adjective inherently comparative है? 'than' है या 'when'? दो items की तुलना है तो 'the' लगा? irregular form सही? Double marking नहीं?

8. Word Choice Errors: Like/As, Since/For, Who/Whom, Few/Little आदि

SSC में frequently confused pairs पर आधारित त्रुटियाँ भी पूछी जाती हैं:

• Like vs As: like = preposition (后面 noun/pronoun); as = conjunction (后面 clause)। He looks like his father. / As I said, ... / Do as I say。As noun के साथ भी 'in the capacity of' अर्थ में चलता है (He works as a teacher.)

• Since vs For: since = point in time (since Monday, since 1990); for = duration (for two hours, for years)。Since कारण अर्थ में भी चलता है (Since you don't know, I'll explain。) Context देखकर चुनाव करो。

• Who vs Whom: who = subject case; whom = object case। Whom का प्रयोग object position में (Whom did you meet?), formal writing में encore; colloquial में who भी चलता है लेकिन SSC में whom required हो सकता है。

• Few/Little vs A few/A little: few/a Few (countable), little/a little (uncountable); 'a' positive sense देता है, बिना 'a' negative। I have few friends (almost none) vs I have a few friends (some)。

• Say/Tell/Speak/Talk: tell = + person object (Tell me...); say = quoted/content (He said that...); speak = language/formal address (speak English, speak to the minister); talk = informal conversation (talk to someone about something)。

• Advice (noun)/Advise (verb): confusion इस pair में होतो है — 'Give me an advice' गलत (advice uncountable); सही 'Give me some advice' या 'Offer an suggestion'。

त्रुटि जाँच: option में दिया गया शब्द किस role में काम कर रहा है — preposition, conjunction, verb type, countability, fixed collocation — और क्या वह इस role के नियमों पर खरा उतरता है?

9. Verb Form Errors: Infinitive, Gerund, Participle, Modals

Verb forms SSC की favorite error categories में शामिल हैं。

•Gerund vs Infinitive: कुछ verbs के बाद V-ing (enjoy, avoid, finish, miss, suggest, admit, consider, practice, risk, keep + Ving), कुछ के बाद to-infinitive (decide, hope, plan, want, promise, refuse, offer, tend, appear, seem), कुछ दोनों (stop, remember, forget, try, begin, start — अर्थ बदलता है)。
गलत: I enjoy to read. / I decided going. / He suggested to go.

• Bare infinitiveafter modal verbs: can, could, may, might, must, shall, should, will, would, ought to के बाद base form (can go, must do)। Has/have/had + V3perfect; did + base form (Did you go? न कि Did you went?)。

• causative: have/get something done (व्यक्तिगत service); make/let/have + object + base form (make him work, let her go, have him repair)। Get + obj + V3 भी चलता है (get it repaired)。

• Participles: present participle (-ing) active meaning; past participle (V3/-ed) passive/completed meaning (The broken vase... / The rising sun...)। Independent participle clause में subject का मिलान जरूरी (Walking down the street, the trees looked beautiful → गलत; Walking down the street, I saw the trees → सही)。

• 'Used to' vs 'Be used to' vs 'Get used to': used to + base (past habit); be used to + Ving (accustomed); get used to + Ving (becoming accustomed)。

त्रुटि जाँच: verb के बाद कौन-सा completer आया? modal के बाद base form है? participle clause का subject सही है? Causative structure सही order में है?

10. Conjunctions, Subordinators and Connector Errors

Conjunction-related errors में SSC निम्नलिखित pattern regularly पूछता है:

• Although/Though vs But: English में这两个不能同现于一个主从复合句中 (Although he is poor, but he is honest → गलत); सही: Although he is poor, he is honest. / He is poor, but he is honest।

• Because vs So: Hindi के अनुवाद में 'क्योंकि...इसलिए' दोनों लगते हैं, English में ऐसा नहीं (Because he was ill, so he stayed home → गलत); सही: Because he was ill, he stayed home. / He was ill, so he stayed home।

• Unless vs If not: unless = if...not, पर negative在主句不允许双重否定意味 (Unless you don't study, you will fail → गलत); सही: Unless you study, you will fail. / If you do not study, you will fail。

• Whether...or vs If...or: indirect question में whether...or preferred (I don't know whether he will come or not)।

• Relative adverbs: when (time), where (place), why (reason)。 Misuse example: The reason is because... → गलत (redundant); सही: The reason is that... / The reason why he came is...

• Conjunction parallelism: either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also के दोनों sides structure match करना आवश्यक (Not only he came but also he danced → गलत); सही: Not only did he come but he also danced./ He not only came but also danced।

त्रुटि जाँच: conjunction pair match है? subordinate + main clause में double connector तो नहीं? Parallelism सही?

11. Redundancy, Double Negative and Fixed Expression Errors

Redundancy (अनावश्यक पुनरावृत्ति) और double negative SSC में अक्सर trick question बनकर आते हैं。

• Redundancy examples:
– Return back (return itself means go back) → सही: Return。
– End result (result implies end) → सही: Final outcome/Result。
– Free gift (gift is inherently free) → गलत usage; सही: Complimentary item / Gift。SSC sometimes tests this as 'gift'本身就意味着免费，因此free是多余的。
– Repeat again (repeat already implies doing again) → सही: Repeat。
– Close proximity (proximity = nearness) → सही: Nearness/Close。
– Unexpected surprise → सही: Surprise。

• Double negative prohibited in standard English: I don't need no help. / I can't hardly move. / He never says nothing. → सभी गलत; सही: I don't need any help. / I can hardly move. / He never says anything。

• Fixed expressions commonly misused:
– In my opinion (not On my opinion)
– With regard to / Regarding (not Regarding to)
– Instead of (not Instead of to)
– Apart from (not Except from)
– As well as (not As good as — 不同含义)
– In spite of / Despite (+ noun/Gerund, NOT of)
– Regardless of (not Regardless to)

त्रुटि जाँच: क्या शब्द/व्याख्यान fixed expression का natural part है या unnecessary augmentation? Negative word के साथ double negative तो नहीं?

12. High-Yield Revision Points (SSC के लिए तेज पुनरावृत्ति बिंदु)

निम्नलिखित quick-reference bullets याद रखो — ये SSC Spotting Errors के高频考点 हैं:

✓ Each/either/neither/every + singular → single verb
✓ One of the + plural + who/that + singular verb
✓ More than one + singular noun + singular verb
✓ Collective noun → context-dependent number
✓ No sooner...than / Hardly...when / Scarcely...when (inversion required)
✓ Since + point; For + duration
✓ Because ↔ So एक साथ नहीं; Although ↔ But एक साथ नहीं
✓ Unless = If not; double negative अवैध
✓ Neither/nor, Either/or, Not only/but also → parallel structure अनिवार्य
✓ Used to + base (past habit); Be used to + Ving (accustomed)
✓ Gerund after: enjoy, avoid, finish, miss, suggest, admit, consider, practice
✓ Infinitive after: decide, hope, plan, want, promise, refuse, offer, seem, appear
✓ Relative clause में redundant object नहीं ('the book that I bought it' → गलत)
✓ Such + noun; So + adj (such a nice day / so nice a day)
✓ Reason is that (not because); The reason why... is that
✓ Who (subject) vs Whom (object) — object position में whom prefer
✓ Superior/inferior/precede/follow + to (न कि than)
✓ Different from/than (दोनों स्वीकार्य)
✓ Married to (not with); Similar to (not like in comparison context)
✓ Return, repeat, already, sufficient शब्दों के साथ redundant modifier न लगें
✓ Double comparatives (more better, most best) हमेशा गलत
✓ Preposition-verb collocations fix याद रखो — ये highest yield category

Important facts

  • SSC Spotting Errors में प्रश्न 3 भागों (A/B/C) + D (no error) के format में आते हैं; प्रत्येक सही प्रयास में केवल एक त्रुटि होती है।
  • Subject-Verb Agreement और Preposition collocation सबसे अधिक weighted topics हैं — इन्हें प्राथमिकता दो।
  • Because+So और Although+But एक ही clause-pair में कभी साथ न आयें — यह SSC का classic trap है।
  • One of the + plural nouns pattern में relative clause का verb SINGULAR होता है — 'one' referent होता है, न कि plural noun।
  • More than one + singular noun भी singular verb लेता है — भ्रम से बचो।
  • No sooner...than और Hardly/Scarcely...when inversion मांगते हैं; when/then गलत pair है।
  • Since = starting point; For = duration — time-signal mismatch त्रुटि का common source है।
  • Gerund vs Infinitive verbs की सूची (enjoy/avoid/suggest vs decide/hope/plan) याद रखो — यह direct recognition question बन सकता है।
  • Relative clause में redundant object (that I bought it) grammar violation है — relative pronoun already object role में है।
  • Double comparatives (more better, most best) हमेशा गलत; irregular forms (good-better-best, bad-worse-worst) की शुद्ध याद रखो।
  • Redundant expressions (return back, repeat again, free gift, end result) SSC में frequently tested होते हैं।
  • Neither/nor, Either/or, Not only/but also में parallel structure अनिवार्य है।
  • Superior/inferior/precede/follow + to (not than) — यह fixed preposition pattern अक्सर पूछा जाता है।

Formulas and relationships

Subject-Verb Agreement Rule: Singular subject → singular verb; Plural subject → plural verb. Exceptions apply for each/either/neither/every + singular (always singular verb), collective nouns (context-dependent), and 'one of the + plural + relative clause' (singular verb).
No sooner + auxiliary(inverted) + subject + V1 + THAN + subject + simple past. Example: No sooner did he arrive THAN it started raining.
Hardly/Scarcely + auxiliary(inverted) + subject + V3 + WHEN + subject + simple past. Example: Hardly had she entered WHEN the phone rang.
Since + point_in_time/perfect_clause → main clause uses Present Perfect. For + duration → main clause uses Present Perfect/Present Perfect Continuous.
Relative clause redundancy rule: If relative pronoun (who/whom/which/that) is already the object, do NOT add a second object pronoun. Correct: the book that I bought. Incorrect: the book that I bought it.
Parallelism rule for correlative conjunctions: Neither/Nor, Either/Or, Not only/But also require identical grammatical structure on both sides. Example: Not only did he study, but he also taught.
Gerund-taking verbs pattern: V + (enjoy, avoid, finish, miss, suggest, admit, consider, practice, risk, keep) + Ving. Infinitive-taking verbs pattern: V + (decide, hope, plan, want, promise, refuse, offer, tend, appear, seem) + to V.
Double comparison prohibition formula: Never combine degree modifier (more/most/less/least) with already-inflected comparative/superlative forms. Illegal: more better, most best, less smarter. Legal: better, best, smarter.

Common mistakes

  • Each/either/neither taking plural verb — These are always singular regardless of the noun following 'of'. (Each of the boys HAVE → गलत; HAS → सही)
  • One of the + plural + plural verb in relative clause — The relative verb agrees with 'one', not the plural noun. (One of the students who HAVE won → गलत; HAS won → सही)
  • Using 'because' and 'so' in the same sentence — English does not allow paired causal conjunctions. (Because he was sick, so he stayed → गलत)
  • Using 'although' and 'but' together — Same rule as above for concessive pairs. (Although he tried, but he failed → गलत)
  • Using 'when' after 'no sooner' — The correct pair is 'no sooner...than', not 'when'. (No sooner did he arrive when... → गलत; THAN → सही)
  • Using present perfect with specific past time markers — 'Yesterday, ago, last year' demand simple past, not present perfect. (I have seen him yesterday → गलत; saw → सही)
  • Using 'like' where 'as' is required (conjunction function) — Like is a preposition; as introduces a clause. (Like I said → colloquial; As I said → formal/correct in exam context)
  • Adding redundant objects in relative clauses — The relative pronoun already fills the object slot. (The man whom I met him → गलत; The man whom I met → सही)
  • Using double comparatives — More/most with already-comparative forms is always wrong. (more better, most best → both wrong)
  • Confusing 'few' and 'little' with their 'a' counterparts — Few/a few (countable); little/a little (uncountable); absence of 'a' gives negative sense. (I have little time = almost none; I have a little time = some)
  • Using 'different than' in formal writing when 'different from' is preferred — Both occur in modern usage but SSC typically expects 'different from'.
  • Misusing 'married with' instead of 'married to' — This is a fixed prepositional collocation error tested repeatedly.
  • Using 'regarding to' — The correct form is 'regarding' (no 'to') or 'with regard to'.
  • Writing 'in spite of' as 'despite of' — Despite NEVER takes 'of'. In spite OF is correct; Despite (alone) is correct.
  • Assuming that compound subjects with 'and' always take plural verbs — Exceptions exist when the compound refers to a single entity or with 'every/each' modifiers.
