Sentence Improvement — Quick Revision (SSC, हिंदी माध्यम)

SSCHindiQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Sentence Improvement (वाक्य सुधार) एक वस्तुनिष्ठ प्रश्न प्रकार है जिसमें वाक्य के एक भाग को bold/अंडरलाइन किया जाता है और चार विकल्प दिए जाते हैं। उम्मीदवार को यह चुनना होता है कि किस विकल्प से वाक्य व्याकरणदृष्टि सबसे श्रेष्ठ बन जाता है। यदि वर्तमान भाग ही सर्वोत्तम है, तो 'No Improvement' चुना जाता है।
  • यह कौशल मूलतः व्याकरण की शुद्धता — त्रुटि-मुक्त संरचना, उपयुक्त शब्द चयन, औपचारिक लेखन शैली और अंग्रेजी भाषा के नियमों के अनुपालन — को परखता है।
  • SSC (CGL/CHSL/MTS/Tier-I) में इस विषय के लिए आमतौर पर 3–5 प्रश्न पूछे जाते हैं; ये ग्रेמר के मुख्य नियमों से सीधे जुड़े होते हैं।

Key concepts

  • ['Sentence Improvement में वाक्य की त्रुटि एक निश्चित पैटर्न में होती है। मुख्य रूप से ये देखें:', '- Subject-Verb Agreement: एकवचनी कर्ता के साथ बहुवचनी क्रिया या इसके विपरीत न हो। "Each of the boys ___" → है / "are" नहीं बल्कि "is" आता है।', '- Tense Consistency: वाक्य के भीतर काल अचानक न बदले। Past narrative में past ही बना रहता है।', '- Preposition misuse:惯用法ी पूर्वसर्ग (accused OF, pleased WITH, afraid OF, consist OF, capable OF आदि) अक्सर गलत दिए जाते हैं।', '- Conjunction/Sentence structure: although/because/if/whether जैसे संयोजकों का प्रयोग सही संदर्भ में हुआ है या नहीं।', '- Article confusion: a/an/the का अप्रासंगिक प्रयोग।', '- Redundancy/Tautology: "end result", "free gift", "unanimously agreed" जैसे दोहराव वाली अभिव्यक्तियाँ।', '- Parallelism:并列 संरचना में समान व्याकरणिक रूप। "He likes reading, writing and to swim" गलत; "reading, writing and swimming" सही।', '- Voice/Active-Passive mismatch: वाक्य का अर्थ बदले बिना voice सही रखना।', '- Modifiers/Dangling phrases: "Running down the street, the tree was seen..." जैसे लटकते विशेषक।']
  • ['निम्नलिखित नियम SSC Sentence Improvement के अधिकांश प्रश्नों का आधार हैं:', "- Rule — Subject-Verb Agreement: Singular subject + singular verb. Plural subject + plural verb। 'Neither…nor', 'Either…or' के निकटतम कर्ता (proximity rule) के अनुसार क्रिया मिलती है। 'One of the boys who ___' → relativo clause का कर्ता 'who' = boys (plural) → verb plural होता है।", "- Rule — Tenses: Present perfect vs past simple: 'I have lived here since 2010' (still living) vs 'I lived there in 2010' (finished)। Past perfect का प्रयोग past से पहले की घटना दर्शाने के लिए होता है (had + V3)।", '- Rule — Conditionals (If-clauses): Type 0 — general truth (If you heat ice, it melts)। Type 1 — real future (If it rains, I will stay)। Type 2 — unreal present (If I were rich, I would travel)। Type 3 — unreal past (If I had studied, I would have passed)। Exam में Type 2 और 3 अधिक पूछे जाते हैं।', '- Rule — Subjunctive mood: "I demand that he ___ be present"। wish, suggest, demand, insist जैसे verbs के बाद base form verb आती है। Also: "If I were you…"', "- Rule — Comparisons:_equal_, _er_, _est_ में भ्रम नहीं होना चाहिए। 'More better', 'most finest' जैसी गलतियाँ अक्सर विकल्प में दी जाती हैं। 'As + adj + as' vs 'Comparative + than'। Correlative: 'the + comparative … the + comparative' (The more, the better)।", '- Rule — Prepositions with specific words: accustomed TO, agree WITH (person)/ON (topic), angry WITH (person)/AT (situation), consist OF, differ FROM, depend UPON, interested IN, jealous OF, proud OF, responsible FOR, superior IN rank TO, inferior TO, similar TO, equivalent TO, prefer … TO/RATHER THAN।', "- Rule — Articles: Generic plural/uncountable nouns के सामने often NO article ('Dogs are loyal', 'Information is power')। Specific reference में 'the'।首次 mention = a/an, पुनः mention = the।", "- Rule — Quantifiers: 'Much' (uncountable), 'Many' (countable plural), 'A lot of' (both), 'Few/A few' (mostly negative vs positive nuance), 'Little/A little' same distinction।", "- Rule — Voice Transformation: Object → Subject; main verb + appropriate be-form + V3। Do/does/did question → 'Did he see the film?' → 'Was the film seen by him?'। Modal verbs के साथ modal + be + V3।"]
  • ['SSC में बार-बार आने वाले शब्दों के जोड़े जिन्हें अक्सर गलत इस्तेमाल किया जाता है:', "- Affect (verb) vs Effect (noun/n少数情况下 as verb meaning 'to influence')। Affect the outcome; The effect was immediate.", '- Lay (place something) vs Lie (recline/rest)。Laid/Lain; Lied (told untruth)/Lie (recline) → lay/lain।', '- Farther (physical distance) vs Further (figurative/degree)।', '- Among (three or more) vs Between (two).', '- Ensure (make certain) vs Insure (financial protection) vs Assure (tell confidently)。Ensure that he comes; Insure the car; Assure him that...', '- Say (reported speech, focus on content) vs Tell (focus on listener: tell ME)。', '- Bring (come near speaker) vs Take (go away from speaker)。Bring the book here; Take the book there。', "- Lose (misplace) vs Loose (not tight)。Don't lose your keys; The shirt is loose。", '- Principal (head/main) vs Principle (rule/belief)。', "- Its (possessive) vs It's (it is / it has)。", "- Your (possessive) vs You're (you are)।", "- Their (possessive) vs They're (they are) vs There (place)。"]

  • ['SSC कभी-कभी punctuation आधारित विकल्प देता है। ध्यान रखें:', '- Comma before coordinating conjunction (FANBOYS: for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so) जब दो independent clauses जुड़ रहे हों।', '- Semicolon connects two closely related independent clauses; colon introduces explanation/list/quote。', "- Apostrophe: possessive nouns (the boys' books) vs contractions (don't = do not) vs plurals नहीं बनते (the 1990s)।", '- Quotation marks: Direct speech में capital letter after comma if new sentence, else lowercase।', '- Dash/em dash: sudden break या clarification के लिए; hyphen (-): compound words में (well-known, self-confidence)।', '- Oxford comma (optional but commonly accepted): A, B, and C.']

Important facts

  • SSC Tier-I (CGL/CHSL) में English से लगभग 25–30 अंक होते हैं; Sentence Improvement से आमतौर पर 3–5 प्रश्न आते हैं।
  • प्रत्येक सही उत्तर पर 1 अंक; कुल नकारात्मक मार्किंग (-0.50) लागू होती है, इसलिए blind guessing न करें।
  • Time allocation: Sentence Improvement में प्रति प्रश्न औसत 40–50 सेकंड देना उचित है।
  • 最常考 grammar topics का क्रम: Subject-Verb Agreement > Tense Consistency > Preposition > Comparative > Voice/Conditional > Idioms/Collocations > Redundancy।
  • No Improvement का अर्थ है वाक्य में कोई व्याकरणिक त्रुटि नहीं है। इसे केवल तभी चुनें जब पूरी तरह संदेह-मुक्त हों।
  • Redundant phrases जो अक्सर विकल्प में आते हैं: 'joint collaboration' (collaboration本身就 joint है), 'end result' (result本身就是 end), 'past history' (history already past), 'free gift' (gift already free), 'unanimously agreed' (already implies everyone)।
  • Subject-verb agreement में विशेष cases: each/every/no one + singular verb; many a + singular noun + singular verb (Many a boy has won); the number of + plural noun + singular verb (The number of students is high); a number of + plural noun + plural verb (A number of students are absent)।

Memory aids

  • FANBOYS mnemonic: For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So — इनके बीच independent clause होने पर comma आती है।
  • SOFT PREPOSITIONS: Accused OF, Afraid OF, Angry WITH/AT, Consist OF, Capable OF, Depend UPON, Interested IN, Proud OF, Responsible FOR, Similar TO, Inferior/Superior TO, Prefer TO/RATHER THAN, Look forward TO, Count ON, Belong TO, Differs FROM, Agree WITH/ON, Pay attention TO।
  • Conditionals quick map: Real present/future → If + present, will + base; Unreal present → If + past, would + base; Unreal past → If + past perfect, would have + V3; Mixed → If + past perfect, would + base (e.g., If I had studied (past), I would be confident now (present))。
  • Farther/Further: Farther → physical (farther distance); Further → abstract/degree (further discussion)。记住: Further has an extra 'r' → more abstract.
  • Among/Between: 2 items → Between; 3+ items → Among।Exception: Between individual items in a group of more than 2 when they are distinct/individual (e.g., between India, Pakistan and China) — पर exam में सरल नियम याद रखें।
  • Neither/Nor, Either/Or — proximity rule: क्रिया निकटतम subject के साथ match होती है। 'Neither the teacher nor the students ARE...' 'Neither the students nor the teacher IS...'
  • Many a + singular noun + singular verb: Many a student HAS failed; Much + uncountable; Many + plural countable।

Exam tips

  • Read the UNDERLINED portion first, then compare options against it. Don't rewrite the sentence yourself — choose the BEST among given options.
  • Eliminate options that contain glaring errors first (wrong tense, wrong preposition, obvious grammar violation) — elimination speeds up guessing among remainders.
  • Watch for REDUNDANCY in options: words like 'return back', 'repeat again', 'merge together' carry inherent duplication — these options are usually incorrect.
  • For conditional sentences, identify the TIME REFERENCE first: past unreal (Type 3), present unreal (Type 2), real future (Type 1)। Pattern recognition eliminates wrong options quickly.
  • Preposition questions: learn collocations in chunks, not individually. E.g., 'capable of doing', 'accused of theft', 'afraid of heights' — memorize the whole phrase.
  • Subject-Verb Agreement: always locate the TRUE subject — ignore prepositional phrases between subject and verb। e.g., 'The box of chocolates ___' → box is subject → is, not are.
  • Practise reading English editorials and news daily — improves intuitive sense of correct collocations and flow, directly benefiting Sentence Improvement accuracy.
  • During exam: if stuck between two options, re-read the COMPLETE sentence with each option mentally; the one that sounds 'cleaner' and follows a standard rule is likely correct.
  • Do NOT overthink No Improvement — it appears roughly 20–30% of the time in good papers; don't avoid choosing it out of suspicion.
  • Previous year practice is most valuable: solve at least 50–70 sentence improvement questions from SSC CGL/CHSL/MTS PYQs to internalise recurring error patterns.

Common traps

  • Trap 1: 'More better', 'most finest', 'more easiest' जैसे double comparison विकल्प में आते हैं। Comparative में 'more' या '-er' एक ही आता है, never both।
  • Trap 2: 'Each of the boys' के बाद 'are' चुनने की प्रवृत्ति। 'Each' हमेशा singular है → 'is/has'।
  • Trap 3: 'The number of' vs 'A number of' का भ्रम। The number = single figure (singular verb); A number = many (plural verb)。
  • Trap 4: 'Less' vs 'Fewer' — countable nouns के साथ fewer; uncountable के साथ less। 'Fewer people', 'less water'।
  • Trap 5: Preposition के बाद verb आए तो verb -ing form में होना चाहिए (considered as going, object to smoking) — base verb नहीं।
  • Trap 6: 'Since' और 'For' का भ्रम — specific point in time → since (since 2010, since Monday); duration → for (for 10 years, for a long time)。
  • Trap 7: 'Say' vs 'Tell' — direct object (person) के बिना say; person के साथ tell। Tell ME; Say THAT; Tell ME THAT...।
  • Trap 8: 'Hope' vs 'Wish' — realistic expectation → hope; impossible/unreal → wish। I hope you come (possible); I wish you came (unlikely)।
  • Trap 9: 'No improvement' trap — कई बार वाक्य सही दिखता है लेकिन विकल्पों में बेहतर phrasing या precise word होता है। अर्थ बदले बिना best option ढूंढें।
