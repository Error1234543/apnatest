SSC English — Spotting Errors | Quick Revision (Hindi)

SSCHindiQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Spotting Errors (त्रुटि पहचान) — Given sentence mein se ek line (A/B/C/D) ya E (No Error) identify karna jahan grammar ki koi rule violation hai.
  • Error Types: Subject-Verb Agreement, Tense, Preposition, Article, Parallelism, Pronoun, Modifier, Redundancy, Word Order, Idiom/Fixed Phrase.
  • Options format: (A) / (B) / (C) / (D) / (E) No Error — har part check karna mandatory hai.

Key concepts

  • Subject-Verb Agreement: Main rule: Singular subject → singular verb; Plural subject → plural verb.

    Special cases:
    • Each/every/either/neither ke saath singular verb: Each of the boys IS coming.
    • Collective noun (team, committee, government) jab group ke roop mein ho → singular; jab members individually → plural. The team plays well.
    • There is/are: 'there' ke baad aane wala noun decide karta hai: There is a book / There are books.
    • News/news items → singular: The news IS bad.
    • Scissors/pants/glasses/jeans/pluralia tantum → plural: My pants ARE torn.
    • Either...or / Neither...nor: neare wala subject decide karta hai: Neither he nor I AM wrong.
    • And se juda subjects: Two subjects joined by 'and' → plural: Ram and Shyam ARE coming.
  • Tense Consistency: Ek sentence mein tense consistent hona chahiye (general truths/satya ke alawa).

    Common errors:
    • Mixed tenses: He said that he WILL come → came.
    • Perfect continuous ke liye time duration: I have been living here SINCE 2010 / FOR 10 years.
    • Had + V3 (past perfect): pehle past event → had done.
    • If clause type 2: If I HAD money, I WOULD help → unreal present/future.
    • If clause type 3: If I HAD studied, I WOULD HAVE passed → unreal past.
    • Reported speech: Direct ke time se pehle ka past → past perfect.
  • Preposition Usage: Fixed prepositions (ratta lagana zaroori):

    • Accuse OF, Angry AT/WITH, Apologize FOR, Belong TO, Capable OF, Different FROM/THAN, Engaged IN/TO, Envious OF, Familiar WITH/TO, Interested IN, Look AFTER, Prefer TO/THAN, Responsible FOR, Succeed IN, Thank FOR.

    Common mistakes:
    • He is good IN maths → AT.
    • She married TO a doctor → with/no preposition (marry is transitive).
    • Discuss ABOUT → Discuss (no preposition needed).
    • Meet WITH (formal) vs Meet (casual).
    • Prior TO, Previous TO.
  • Article Usage: Definite article THE:
    • Specific nouns ke liye: The Ganges, The Taj Mahal.
    • Unique things: The sun, The moon, The earth.
    • Superlatives: The best, The most beautiful.
    • Musical instruments: Play the piano.

    Indefinite article A/AN:
    • First mention: A boy came.
    • A before consonant sound: A university (/j/ sound).
    • AN before vowel sound: An hour (/h/ silent).
    • An before singular countable nouns.

    No article:
    • Abstract/general: Love is blind. / People say...
    • Languages, subjects: He studies English, Maths.
    • Meals: Breakfast is ready.
    • Proper nouns (mostly): Delhi, Ram.
  • Parallel Structure: Same grammatical form same function mein:

    • Incorrect: She likes reading, to write, and swimming.
    • Correct: She likes reading, writing, and swimming.

    • Incorrect: He is honest, hardworking, and he is kind.
    • Correct: He is honest, hardworking, and kind.

    Fixed pairs mein parallelism:
    • Both...and, Either...or, Not only...but also, Such...as

    Example: Not only IS he intelligent but he IS also hardworking.
  • Pronoun Usage: He/Him differences:
    • Subject case: He went there.
    • Object case: Give it to him.
    • After than/as comparison: He is taller THAN I (am) / me (informal).

    Who vs Whom:
    • Who = subject: Who called?
    • Whom = object: To whom did you speak?

    Possessive: Its (possession) vs It's (= it is); Their (possession) vs They're (= they are); Your (possession) vs You're (= you are).

    Reflexive: myself, yourself, himself, themselves — jab subject aur object same hon.
    • Incorrect: between you and I → between you and me.
  • Comparative and Superlative: Two items: comparative (+er / more)
    Three or more: superlative (+est / most)

    Errors:
    • More better → Better (double comparative)
    • Most unique → Unique (absolute — no degree)
    • Twice as big → twice the size (not double as big)

    Correlative: "than" ke saath comparative: taller THAN, not more than.
    "different from" / "different to" (British).
  • Redundancy and Wordiness: Meaningless repetition avoid karo:

    • Free gift → Gift (gift already free)
    • End result → Result (result is already end)
    • Personal opinion → Opinion (opinion is personal)
    • True fact → Fact (fact is true)
    • Repeat again → Again
    • Consensus of opinion → Consensus
    • Unexpected surprise → Surprise
    • Round in shape → Round (already implies shape)
  • Countable vs Uncountable Nouns: Uncountable nouns (no plural, take singular verb):
    Information, advice, furniture, luggage, baggage, knowledge, money, wealth, equipment, stuff, bread, water, rice, wheat, sugar, salt, electricity, weather, news, progress.

    Common errors:
    • Many informations → Much information
    • Few advices → Little advice
    • A lot of informations → A lot of information
    • Two furnitures → Two pieces of furniture

    Quantifiers:
    • Much/many: much water, many students
    • A lot of: a lot of time/people
    • Little/few: little hope, few options (negative sense)
    • A little/a few: a little patience, a few days (positive sense)
  • Conjunctions and Linkers: Common errors:
    • Although/Because ke saath so/not use: Although he was sick, he came. (NOT 'Although...so')
    • Despite/In spite OF + noun/-ing: Despite the rain, we went out.
    • However (concessive): He worked hard; however, he failed.
    • Whether...or: I don't know whether he will come or not.
    • As/like: Do AS I say (action follow). Do LIKE me (comparison).
    • Since (time) vs Because (reason): Since you came, I left. / Since it rained, match cancelled.
  • Modifiers and Dangling Participle: Modifier sahi noun ko modify kare:

    • Dangling participle error:
    Walking in the park, the flowers were beautiful. ← Flowers can't walk.
    Correct: Walking in the park, I saw beautiful flowers.

    • Misplaced modifier:
    She almost drove her car to the garage every day. (almost is misplaced)
    Correct: She drove her car almost to the garage every day.

    • Comparisons clear hone chahiye:
    He has more money than any other boy in class.

Important facts

  • SSC CGL/CHSL/Tier-1 mein 24-25 English questions hote hain; Spotting Errors se 2-4 direct questions aate hain.
  • Har option ko check karna mandatory hai; kabhi bhi pehla error milte hi stop mat karo — doosre options mein bhi error ho sakta hai.
  • No Error (E) option ko ignore mat karo — kai baar sentence completely correct hota hai.
  • SSC mein fixed preposition aur idiom-based errors sabse zyada aate hain; inhein ratta lagao.
  • Double negative banega positive meaning: 'I don't need no help' → grammatically incorrect in formal English.
  • Quantifier + uncountable noun combo SSC mein frequently test hota hai: many/much, few/little, a few/a little, a lot of.
  • Each/Every/One/Everyone → always singular verb leke aata hai.
  • Either...or, Neither...nor — near subject decide karta hai agreement (proximity rule).

Memory aids

  • SVA mnemonic: Subject-Verb Agreement → 'Singular Subject Singular Verb, Plural Subject Plural Verb'
  • Preposition memory chain: Angry AT, Forgive FOR, Good AT, Interested IN, Responsible FOR, Different FROM — sabko ek story bana kar yaad rakho
  • Parallel structure tip: 'and/or/but' se pehle aur baad mein SAME form rakho
  • Countable/Uncountable trick: Unpackable items → uncountable (furniture, luggage, advice, information — inhe count nahi kiya ja sakta)
  • Redundancy detector: Kya word redundant hai? Free gift? Round in shape? True fact?
  • Who/Whom rule: Who = he/she (subject), Whom = him/her (object)

Exam tips

  • Step-by-step approach: Pehle subject dhoondo → phir verb check karo → phir preposition/article/other elements.
  • Agar first part mein error dikhe toh bhi baaki parts zaroor check karo — multiple errors possible hote hain.
  • Preposition errors ko pehchano: fixed preposition yaad karne ke liye daily 5-10 phrases repeat karo.
  • Redundancy errors fast eliminate kar sakte ho: agar koi word meaning badal raha nahi toh wo extra hai.
  • Tense consistency ke liye time words dhundo: since, for, already, yet, just, ago, yesterday — ye tense batate hain.
  • Parallelism check karo jab 'and', 'or', 'but', 'not only...but also' aaye.
  • Practice PYQs from last 5-10 years — SSC patterns repeat hote hain.
  • Speed build karo: har spotting error question 30-45 seconds se zyada nahi lena chahiye.

Common traps

  • Trap 1: Collective noun singular/plural confusion — context dekh kar decide karo
  • Trap 2: Near-to-near subjects (either...or) ka agreement — near wale subject ko check karo, door wale ko nahi
  • Trap 3: Article ke saath proper nouns — 'He goes to school' vs 'He goes to the school' ka matlab alag hota hai
  • Trap 4: Dangling modifier — sentence ke start mein jo participle phrase hai, uska subject sentence ka subject hona chahiye
  • Trap 5: More better, most unique jaise double degree — ye hamesha wrong hote hain
  • Trap 6: Preposition ke baad gerund (-ing form) — interested IN doing, good AT playing
  • Trap 7: 'Between' exactly do cheezon ke beech, 'among' teen+ ke liye
  • Trap 8: 'Less' vs 'Fewer': fewer + countable (fewer students), less + uncountable (less water)
