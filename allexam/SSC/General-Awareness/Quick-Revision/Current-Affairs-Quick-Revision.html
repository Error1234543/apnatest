Definitions

  • संघ लोक सेवा आयोग (UPSC) — भारत का केंद्रीय भर्ती एजेंसी जो सिविल सर्विसेज सहित अन्य प्रमुख भर्तियों का परीक्षण करती है।
  • राष्ट्रपति भवन (NCP) — नई दिल्ली में स्थित, भारत के राष्ट्रपति का कार्यालय; वास्तुकला: लुइस कोंवो (Lewis Colvin) द्वारा डिजाइन किया गया।
  • भारतीय रिज़र्व बैंक (RBI) — भारत का केंद्रीय बैंक, मुद्रास्फीति-लक्ष्य आधारित मौद्रिक नीति लागू करता है; मुख्य रूप से 'बैंक द रेज़र्व' अधिनियम, 1934 के तहत कार्यरत।
  • GDP vs GSDP — राष्ट्रीय आय का माप: GDP = सभी क्षेत्रों में उत्पादन; GSDP = किसी राज्य की आंतरिक उत्पादकता।
  • MSME — सूक्ष्म, लघु और मध्यम उद्यम; मंत्रालय 2020 में स्थापित, अब उद्योग और व्यापार मंत्रालय के अधीन।
  • PM-जाति विश्वविद्यालय योजना — अनुसूचित जाति/जनजाति समुदायों के लिए शैक्षणिक संस्थाएं बनाने की योजना; 2023-24 से लागू।
  • One Nation One Election — राज्यों और संसद के एक ही साथ चुनाव करने की अवधारणा; सुप्रीम कोर्ट और सरकार द्वारा चर्चा में।

Exam tips

  • SSC GA Current Affairs questions typically come from: (1) National events/appointments (3-4 questions), (2) Sports (2-3), (3) Awards (1-2), (4) International summits (1-2), (5) Defence/Science (1-2)।
  • Focus on last 12-18 months before exam date; SSC asks mostly recent events (2023-2025 period for 2024-25 exams)。
  • Practice converting English CA facts to Hindi terms; many SSC papers now have bilingual or Hindi-only CA sections।
  • Key months to remember: Jan (Republic Day, Budget), Feb-Mar (award seasons, GST council), June (Yoga Day, Olympics), Sept (Hindi Diwas, Constitution Day), Oct (Gandhi Jayanti, Diwali breaks), Nov-Dec (civic events, COP summits)。
  • When asked about 'first in India' — prioritize: first woman President (Pratibha Patil), first woman PM (Indira Gandhi), first female IPS (Kiran Bedi), first female astronaut (not yet flown), first private astronaut (Sara Pandya, 2024 commercial spaceflight)।
  • Memory aids help: use acronyms like 'BRICS' = Brazil-Russia-India-China-South Africa; 'G7' = Canada-France-Germany-Italy-Japan-UK-USA; 'SCO' members list।
  • SSC increasingly asks about schemes and their implementing ministries — always match scheme name with correct ministry (e.g., Ayushman Bharat → Ministry of Health, PM Vishwakarma → Ministry of MSME)。
  • For numerical CA: remember key figures like GDP growth ~7%, inflation ~5%, forex reserves ~$640B, remittances ~$111B, UPI transactions >15B/month, GST collection >₹1.8T/month。

Common traps

  • NEET/JEE aspirants often confuse 'Agneepath Yojana' with 'Agniveer scheme' — they are the SAME; exam questions use both terms.
  • Chandrayaan-3 landed at SHIVA station near lunar south pole; Chandrayaan-2 attempted similar but crashed. Don't mix up dates: CH-2 Sept 2019, CH-3 July 2023.
  • UPI is not owned by RBI—it is operated by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India), a not-for-profit under RBI guidance. Many questions trick students here.
  • G20 presidency rotates annually; 2023 was India's, 2024 Brazil's. Do not confuse host with current president.
  • BRICS expanded on 2024; but the 'BRICS+ ' is not an official name—officially it is 'BRICS with new members' or just 'BRICS'.
  • Nobel Peace Prize 2023 went to Nihon Hidankyo (Japanese organization), not an individual. Remember: 2022 was Ales Bialiatski (Belarus), 2023 was Nihon Hidankyo。
  • GST council meetings are held monthly; 28th meeting (March 2024) was significant for gold tax. Do not memorize wrong meeting numbers.
  • ISRO's first satellite launch via GSLV was GSAT-1 in 2001; first indigenously built GSLV was GSLV-F01 in 2003。
  • Aditya-L1 is at Lagrange Point 1 (L1), about 1.5 million km from Earth—not orbiting the moon。
  • CPI inflation target band is 2-6% with a tolerance of ±2%; mean target is 4%। Questions often ask the 'target' not the 'tolerance band'।
  • Padma awards announced on Republic Day (Jan 26); conferred on Republic Day or nearby date। 2024 announcement was Jan 25, conferment later।
  • India's sex ratio at birth (2024): 942 females per 1000 males (NFHS-5). Do not confuse with overall sex ratio (940 approx)。
