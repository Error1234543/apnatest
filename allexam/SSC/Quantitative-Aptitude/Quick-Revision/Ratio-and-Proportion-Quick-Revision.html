Ratio and Proportion — Quick Revision for SSC

SSCHindiQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Anuapaat (Ratio): Do yaadadharon ka bhagfal, jise a/b ya a:b ke roop mein likha jaata hai. Yahan a anuvad (antecedent) aur b anuvidhey (consequent) hai. Anuapaat ek sankhya nahi, ek tulyata (comparison) hai.
  • Vartupaat (Proportion): Jab do anuapaat barabar hon toh vah vartupaat kahaata hai. Agar a:b = c:d ho, toh kahein ge ki a, b, c, d samantar (proportional) hain. Ise a/b = c/d ya a:b :: c:d ke roop mein bhi likha jaata hai.
  • Chaturthi Anupat (Fourth Proportional): Agar a:b = c:x ho, toh x ko c ka chaturthi anupat kahein ge. Isame x = (b×c)/a hota hai.
  • Tritiya Anupat (Third Proportional): Agar a:b = b:x ho, toh x ko b ka tritiya anupat kahein ge. Isame x = b²/a hota hai.
  • Moolyaanupaat (Mean Proportional): Agar a:x = x:b ho, toh x donon ka moolyaanupaat (geometric mean) hai. Isame x = √(a×b) hota hai.

Key concepts

  • Anuapaat ke vidhi (Basic operations): • Anuapaat ko purnank (integer) mein badalne ke liye donon pakkhon ko LCM lena padta hai.
    • Antekaran (Inverting): a:b → b:a
    • Viparit (Reciprocal): a:b → b:a (yahi antekaran ke saman hai)
    • Anyoanyopet (Componendo): a:b → (a+b):b
    • Vibhajan (Dividendo): a:b → (a−b):b
    • Ekikaran (Compound ratio): a:c aur b:d ka anyoanyopet anuapaat ab:cd hota hai.
  • Samantar (Proportion) ke nidhan gun (Properties): • Agatyagny Gunaka (Cross-multiplication): a/b = c/d ⇒ ad = bc
    • Antyakann Phal = Madhyakann Phal: bahar ke ankron ka gunan = andar ke ankron ka gunan
    • Vichal (Alternendo): a/b = c/d ⇒ a/c = b/d
    • Samahara (Componendo): a/b = c/d ⇒ (a+b)/b = (c+d)/d
    • Vihara (Dividendo): a/b = c/d ⇒ (a−b)/b = (c−d)/d
    • Sam-vihara (Componendo-Dividendo): a/b = c/d ⇒ (a+b)/(a−b) = (c+d)/(c−d)
    • Bhagfal Pratiyogita (Invertendo): a/b = c/d ⇒ b/a = d/c
  • Khaas Anuapaat (Special Ratios): • Anuurup (Duplicate): a:b ka anuurup anuapaat a²:b²
    • Tripurat (Triplicate): a:b ka tripurat anuapaat a³:b³
    • Vimarsh (Sub-duplicate): a:b ka vimarsh anuapaat √a : √b
    • Viparit Anuapaat (Inverse Ratio): a:b ka viparit anuapaat b:a
    • Yukta Anuapaat (Joint Ratio): a:b aur c:d ka yukta anuapaat ac : bd
  • Mishran Vidhi (Method of Alligation / Rule of Mixture): Jab do ya adhik varagon ko milakar ek naya mishran banaya jaata hai:
    • Dheem (Mean price) = (C₁ × Q₁ + C₂ × Q₂) / (Q₁ + Q₂)
    • Alligation ka nidhan roop: (Dheem − Cheeni rat) / (Amoli rat − Dheem) = migraan ki matra ka anuapaat
    • Yeh vidhi sirf tab laguu hoti hai jab do varagon ko milakar naya varg banaya jaata hai aur anuapaat puchha jaata hai.
  • Vishesh Anumaa Tark (Special Tricks for SSC): • Agla anuapaat pata karne ke liye b × c / a formula ka upyog.
    • Trirashi samantar (Three terms proportional): a, x, b samantar hone par x² = ab.
    • Chatur rashi (Four quantities) samantar hone par a×d = b×c.
    • Jod-vigat anuapaat: agar a:b = c:d ho, toh (a+c):(b+d) bhi ek anuapaat dega jo prashn ke anusaar labhh rahega.
    • Sankhya ke ank pratyayogee (digit reversal): agar sankhya 10x+y hai, toh ulatha 10y+x hai.

Important facts

  • SSC (CGL/CHSL/MTS) mein Ratio and Proportion se 3 se 6 prashn pratisarvarshan aaate hain.
  • Is vishay ke prashn aksar sahitik (numerical) ya anumanit (approximate) hotte hain; 60 second ke andar hal karna lakshiyan hai.
  • Alligation prashnon mein dhyan rakhein: yadi koi mishran 2:3 anuapaat mein hai aur usme se kuch nikal kar badlav karein, toh naye anuapaat ke liye purani aur nayi matra dono ka hisaab lagana hota hai.
  • Componendo-Dividendo se milne wale prashnon mein (a+b)/(a−b) ka uttar 3:1 aata hai toh a:b = 2:1 hota hai — yeh nikash yaad rakhein.
  • Ganit prishthan (Arithmetic Progression) aur anuapaat ka sangam: agar teen sankhyaaen a, b, c samantar anuapaat mein hoon, toh b² = ac.
  • Age-related prashnon mein: 'a saal pehle' ya 'a saal baad' ka anuapaat nikalte samay donon taraf sam anka lena na bhoolein.
  • Money distribution prashn: rupeyon ko anuapaat mein baantein, fir har hisse ko prakritik sankhya mein dhundhein. Utar prakritik sankhya hona chahiye.

Memory aids

  • Antyakann × Madhyakann = Phal yaad rakhne ke liye: 'Bahar andar se milo, phal barabar milo' — cross multiply ko yaad dilaata hai.
  • Componendo, Dividendo, Alternendo ke naam yaad rakhne ke liye: 'CAAD' — Compo, Alter, Div, Endo — incharak (alphabetical) kramanujar hai.
  • Duplicate, Triplicate, Sub-duplicate ke liye: 'Dupli=2, Tri=3, Sub=1/2' — ghātan (powers) yaad rahegi.
  • Alligation ke liye mnemonic: 'Tez cheez ko tez, dheemi ko dheemi — madhya ka anuapaat dhundho' — misaal: amoli rat − dheem rat = madhya anka.

Exam tips

  • SSC mein anuapaat prashnon ka uttar 60 se 90 second mein nikalna chahiye. Practice se speed badhti hai.
  • Option elimination ka upyog karein: kuch prashnon mein uttar prakritik sankhya ya bhinn ka hissa aata hai, jo options se hi patla chal jaata hai.
  • Alligation ke prashnon mein diagram bana ke hi kaam karein — dimaag mein solve karne se glti ho sakti hai.
  • Componendo-Dividendo wale prashnon mein direct formula lagaa kar uttar nikal sakte hain — isake liye aapko (a+b)/(a−b) ke uttar se a:b nikalne ka nidhan yaad rakhna chahiye.
  • Prishthan (A.P./G.P.) se judi anek prashnon mein anuapaat ka upyog hota hai — G.P. ke liye b² = ac yaad rakhein.

Common traps

  • Kai baar prashn kevan 'anuapaat' mangta hai par uttar milne ke baad use kam se kam roop (simplified form) mein likhna bhool jaate hain — jaise 48:32 ko 3:2 banakar dena na bhoolein.
  • Prishthan (progression) se judi anek prashnon mein 'samantar anuapaat' ka arth smjhte samay keval b² = ac hi laga dein, par prishthan ka antar (difference) nikaalne bhool jaate hain.
  • Age prashn mein 'pehle' ya 'baad' ka samay alag-alag hota hai — kuch log keval current age ka anuapaat nikaal kar uttar de dete hain jo galat hota hai.
  • Mishran (alligation) mein chetavani: yadi mishran ke bad kuch nikal kar wapas milaya jaata hai, toh purane mishran ki matra ko sahi tareeke se badlena zaroori hai.
  • Jaldbaazi mein anko ko mulankan (fraction) ke roop mein likh dete hain, jisse gunan-bhag mein ghalti hoti hai.
