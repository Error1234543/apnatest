CA Intermediate Group 1 - Taxation (Direct + Indirect) Quick Revision

CAEnglishQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Total Income: The total amount of income computed under the Income-tax Act from all sources after deducting allowances, exemptions, and deductions admissible under the Act.
  • Residential Status: Determines the extent of income chargeable to tax in India. Based on number of days of stay in India during the relevant previous year and earlier years.
  • Assessable Value (for GST): The value of supply as determined under Section 15 of CGST Act, upon which GST is levied. Includes all charges except certain excluded items.
  • Input Tax Credit (ITC): Credit of GST paid on inputs and input services used in making taxable supplies, available to a registered person under GST law.
  • Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM): Mechanism under GST where the recipient of goods/services is liable to pay tax instead of the supplier.
  • Composition Scheme: A scheme under GST allowing small taxpayers to pay tax at a fixed percentage of turnover, with restricted ITC and compliance benefits.
  • Tax Deducted at Source (TDS): Deduction of tax at the source of income as per specified rates and sections of the Income-tax Act, to ensure steady revenue flow.

Key concepts

  • Heads of Income: Salary, House Property, Profits & Gains of Business/Profession, Capital Gains, Other Sources.
  • Set-off and Carry Forward: Inter-head set-off, intra-head set-off, loss carry forward conditions and time limits.
  • Deductions under Chapter VI-A: Sections 80C to 80U; specific limits and eligible investments/expenditures.
  • Computation of Total Income: Sequence of steps from gross total income to total income after deductions.
  • Residential Status Criteria: Indian resident, NR Indian, NNRI based on stay days in India and citizenship.
  • Place of Supply Rules: Key provisions for goods, services, and mixed supplies to determine GST jurisdiction.
  • Input Tax Credit Eligibility: Conditions under Section 16 CGST Act; blocked credits under Section 17(5).
  • GST Valuation: Rules under Section 15 CGST Act including transactions between distinct/related persons.
  • Advance Rulings: Authority for Advance Rulings (AAR) jurisdiction and binding nature.
  • Penalties and Prosecutions: Key provisions under CGST/IGST Acts for defaults like evasion, delay.

Important facts

  • Due date for filing ITR-1 (FY 2025-26): 31st July 2026 (assessed as per current norms). — ICAI Study Material
  • Basic exemption limit for individual below 60 years: ₹2,50,000. — Income-tax Act
  • TDS rate on salary (Section 192): Slab rates applicable with standard deduction of ₹50,000. — Section 192
  • Deduction under Section 80C: Maximum ₹1,50,000 for specified investments/payments. — Section 80C
  • GST threshold for composition scheme: Turnover up to ₹1.5 crore (₹75 lakh for special category states). — Section 10 CGST Act
  • Time limit for claiming ITC: Earlier of 30th November following end of financial year or filing of annual return. — Section 16(4) CGST Act
  • Interest on delayed payment of GST: 18% per annum under Section 50 CGST Act. — Section 50
  • Penalty for tax evasion: 100% of tax evaded plus interest under Section 122 CGST Act. — Section 122
  • Capital gain holding period for listed equity shares: 12 months for long-term capital gain. — Section 2(42A)
  • Audit threshold under Section 44AB: Turnover exceeding ₹1 crore (or ₹10 crore if cash receipts <5%). — Section 44AB

Memory aids

  • Head of Income Mnemonic: 'SHIPS' – Salary, House property, Income from Profits & Business, Capital gains, Other sources.
  • Section 80C Investments: 'LIC EPF PPF NSC TU' – Life Insurance, EPF, PPF, NSC, Tuition fees.
  • Order of Set-off: Intra-head first, then inter-head (except Head-specific losses like Foreign Income).
  • GST Composition Scheme Limits: '1.5 Cr / 75 L' – General ₹1.5 crore, Special Category States ₹75 lakh.
  • Time Limit for ITC: 'November before next FY end or annual return filing' – Use mnemonic 'NAR' (November Annual Return).

Exam tips

  • Focus on numerical problems: Practice computations of taxable income, GST liability, TDS, and capital gains thoroughly.
  • Revise section citations: Memorize key section numbers and their subject matter for quick reference in answer writing.
  • Understand interlinkages: Many topics like residential status affect taxability across heads; link concepts for holistic understanding.
  • Solve previous year questions: ICAI publishes past exam questions; analyse pattern and frequently tested areas.
  • Manage time in exam: Allocate more time to calculation-based questions and ensure all steps are shown for partial marks.
  • Use diagrams for GST flows: Draw flow charts for place of supply, ITC chain, and composition scheme benefits to clarify answers.

Common traps

  • Confusing residential status criteria: Remember that an NRI may still be a resident if stay exceeds 182 days in previous year.
  • Misapplying deduction limits: E.g., Section 80D limit varies by age; verify correct limit for self/family/parents.
  • Incorrect computation of capital gains: Distinguish between short-term and long-term based on asset type and holding period.
  • Overlooking blocked ITC: Certain expenses like motor vehicles, food, construction material have restricted ITC under Section 17(5).
  • Missed due dates: File returns before due date to avoid penalty and interest; note different dates for different ITR forms.
  • Wrong valuation for GST: Exclude discounts, subsidies, and taxes other than GST from assessable value.
