Quick Revision: General Mental Ability and Reasoning (GPSC)

GPSCEnglishQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Syllogism: A form of deductive reasoning where a conclusion is drawn from two or more given statements (premises) assuming them to be true.
  • Coding-Decoding: A type of reasoning where words/letters are transformed according to a specific rule or pattern, and the candidate must identify or apply that pattern.
  • Blood Relation: Questions based on familial relationships expressed through coded or direct statements requiring family-tree construction.
  • Direction Sense: Questions testing understanding of spatial orientation — movement directions (North, South, East, West), turns (left/right), and distance calculation.
  • Seating Arrangement: Ordering of persons around a circle, rectangle, or line based on positional clues provided in the question.
  • Analogy: A pair of words/items showing a relationship; the candidate must find a pair with the same relationship.
  • Puzzle: A set of interrelated constraints among elements (persons, objects, days, etc.) requiring logical placement.
  • Series Completion: Identifying the missing or next term in a sequence based on a discernible numerical/alphabetical/logical pattern.

Key concepts

  • ANALOGY & CLASSIFICATION — Core types: (1) Letter analogy: e.g., ACE : BDF :: GJO : ___ (answer: IKM), shifting by +1 each letter. (2) Number analogy: e.g., 6 : 42 :: 8 : ___ (8×7=56). Pattern types include: product rule (a×b → result), square/cube relation (n², n³), difference-based (n+2, n²-1), digit operations (sum of digits → multiply by something). For 'odd one out' classification: identify the property shared by three options — divisibility, prime/composite, palindromic, digital sum, positional odd-even, etc.
  • CODING-DECODING — Standard patterns: (1) Direct opposite: A↔Z, B↔Y (pair sum = 27; use mnemonic EJOTY: E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25 — mirror at 13). (2) Shift cipher: +N, −N, alternating +1/−1 (AB→AC, CD→CE pattern). (3) Reverse letter order: REMAIN → NIMAER. (4) Pair swap: ABDCEF → BADCFE. (5) Mixed: alternate letters reversed, rest shifted. Quick method: write A B C D E F G H I J K L M above N O P Q R S T U V W X Y Z; any pair summing to 27 are opposites.
  • BLOOD RELATIONS — Two types: (1) Pointing type: 'He is the son of the only daughter of the father of my sister.' Break stepwise from inside out: father of my sister = my father → only daughter = my mother → son of my mother = my brother. (2) Pedigree/chART type: draw symbols — male=□ female=○ marriage=— sibling line=| divorce=÷ death=×. Key phrases: 'only child' means no siblings; 'maternal uncle' = mother's brother; 'paternal grandmother' = father's mother. Remember gender-invisible terms: parent, sibling, cousin, aunt, uncle, spouse — you must determine gender separately.
  • DIRECTION & DISTANCE — Standard directions: North, South, East, West, plus NE, NW, SE, SW. Left turn = anticlockwise, right turn = clockwise. When facing North: left→West, right→East. Displacement = shortest straight-line distance from origin. Use Pythagoras theorem for right-angle movements: If a person walks a km North, then b km East, displacement = √(a²+b²) km. For non-right angles, draw and resolve using components. Common trick: after multiple turns, first establish final facing direction, then trace position relative to start point.
  • RANKING & ORDER — Rank from top/bottom/left/right. If A is rth from top and sth from bottom in a column: total = r + s − 1. If A is kth from left and mth from right in a row: total = k + m − 1. Overlap problems: When two people cross each other moving in opposite directions, new positions can be found using: Total = (Position of A from one end + Position of B from same end) when they interchange. Always verify whether rank includes the person themselves or counts others only.
  • SYLLOGISM — Rule-based approach using Venn diagrams. Standard forms: 'All A are B' → circle A completely inside circle B. 'No A is B' → two separate circles. 'Some A are B' → overlapping circles. 'Some A are not B' → part of A outside B (always leaves room for some overlap). Conclusions that ALWAYS follow: from 'All A are B' → 'Some B are A' (conversion valid), but 'All B are A' does NOT follow. Do not assume real-world facts — treat premises as absolute truth even if contradictory to reality. For multiple conclusions, check each individually against ALL possible Venn configurations.
  • NUMBER & LETTER SERIES — Number series patterns: (1) Simple difference: ±d, ±2d, ±3d... (2) Difference of differences constant → quadratic series: nth term = an²+bn+c. (3) Square/cube based: n²±k, n³±k. (4) Multiplication-addition: ×a+b, ×c+d pattern. (5) Mixed: alternate operations. (6) Prime-based: primes ×k or primes ±k. Letter series: track position values A=1 to Z=26. Look for: fixed gap, alternating gaps, reverse order segments, vowel-consonant patterns. When two series are interleaved, separate odd/even positions. For missing-term series, test each option by substitution and check consistency.
  • PUZZLES & SEATING ARRANGEMENT — Linear puzzle types: single row (facing North/South — note that facing South reverses left/right), two rows (facing each other). Circular puzzle types: all face center vs. all face outside. Key rules: (1) 'A sits second to the left of B' means count two seats anticlockwise from B. (2) In circular arrangement facing center: left = anticlockwise, right = clockwise. (3) Never make assumptions not stated — use direct clues first, then indirect. Drawing method: draw rough diagram, fill definite positions first, handle 'either case' situations separately, eliminate contradictions.
  • CLOCKS & CALENDARS — Odd days concept: 1 normal year = 1 odd day, 1 leap year = 2 odd days. 100 years = 5 odd days, 200 = 3, 300 = 1, 400 = 0 (cycles every 400 years). Leap year rule: divisible by 4, except century years must be divisible by 400 (e.g., 2000 is leap, 1900 is not). Day calculation: add odd days to reference day. Clock angles: minute hand moves 6° per minute, hour hand moves 0.5° per minute. Angle between hands at H:M = |30H − 11M/2| degrees. Straight line = 180°, right angle = 90°.
