Current Affairs (National, International, Gujarat) — Complete Notes for GPSC General Studies

GPSCEnglishNotes

1. Scope and Importance of Current Affairs in GPSC

Current Affairs is a high-weightage topic in both the Prelims and Mains stages of the GPSC examination. In Prelims, questions are typically direct-fact based, statement-based, or combination-style from national and Gujarat-specific events. In Mains, current affairs integrate with Indian Polity, Economy, Geography, and Gujarat-specific papers, demanding analytical answers that connect recent developments to constitutional, administrative, or socio-economic frameworks.

The syllabus divides current affairs into three concentric domains:
- National Current Affairs: Central government policies, parliamentary developments, economic surveys, budget highlights, important appointments, scientific missions, and national-level indices.
- International Current Affairs: Major summits, UN resolutions, India's foreign policy engagements, defense agreements, trade deals, global indices where India ranks, and events affecting the Indian diaspora or security.
- Gujarat Current Affairs: State government flagship schemes, industrial and infrastructure projects, budget highlights, cultural and heritage initiatives, environmental developments specific to Gujarat, and administrative reforms within the state.

Recommended coverage window: the 18–24 months preceding the examination notification. Events older than two years rarely appear unless they launched enduring schemes or caused lasting structural changes.

2. National Current Affairs — Key Domains

National current affairs for GPSC should be systematically reviewed across the following domains:

Government Policy and Governance: Major policy launches by the Union Cabinet, amendments to central legislation, Supreme Court landmark judgments, Law Commission recommendations, and constitutional bench rulings. Focus on policies related to federalism, cooperative governance, and rights-based legislation.

Economy and Finance: Union Budget highlights, RBI monetary policy decisions, GDP and inflation data releases, PLI scheme updates, GST Council decisions, banking sector reforms, and fiscal deficit trajectory. Pay attention to items appearing in the Economic Survey's chapter summaries.

Parliamentary Affairs: New Bills introduced and passed, Parliamentary Committee reports (especially Public Accounts, Estimates, and Business), anti-defection cases, and significant Question Hour debates on national issues.

Appointments and Institutions: Appointments to constitutional bodies (Election Commission, UPSC, CAG, NHRC), apex judicial positions, military chiefs, heads of PSUs and public banks, and chairpersons of key commissions.

National Indices and Reports: India's ranking in global indices such as HDI, Corruption Perception Index, Global Innovation Index, Ease of Doing Business sub-indicators, Human Development indicators, and NITI Aayog composite indices. Note the year, ranking, and the parameter where India performs best or worst.

Scientific and Technological Developments: ISRO mission launches and achievements, DRDO defense tests, indigenous technology breakthroughs, space cooperation agreements, and digital public infrastructure initiatives.

Defence and Internal Security: Strategic partnerships, military exercises, border infrastructure development, counter-terrorism operations, and cyber security initiatives.

Social Development Schemes: Centrally sponsored and fully funded schemes in health, education, agriculture, rural employment, women and child development, and social justice. Understand the implementing ministry, target group, benefit mechanism, and latest budgetary allocation.

3. International Current Affairs — India's Perspective

International current affairs for GPSC must always be viewed through India's strategic, economic, and diplomatic lens:

Major Multilateral Forums: G20 outcomes and India's presidency themes, BRICS expansion and initiatives, Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) decisions, BIMSTEC progress, IPR (India-Pacific Route) developments, QUAD cooperation areas, and Commonwealth summits. Know the host country, key declarations, and India's stated position.

Bilateral Relations: Important state visits by Indian leadership and foreign leaders visiting India, MoUs signed in trade, defence, education, and culture, visa and ease-of-travel agreements, and dispute resolution mechanisms with neighbours (Pakistan, China, Bangladesh, Myanmar).

India's Diaspora Connections: PIO/OCI card updates, Pravasi Bhartiya Divas outcomes, remittance trends, Indian community welfare initiatives abroad, and expatriate recognition awards.

Global Indices with India: World Bank Doing Business legacy indicators, IMF World Economic Outlook projections for India, WHO health statistics, UNESCO World Heritage site additions from India, WIPO patent filings by Indian applicants, and FAO food security data.

Conflict and Security: Ongoing conflicts with implications for Indian energy imports or diaspora safety (e.g., Red Sea crisis, Ukraine war impact on fertilizer and grain supplies), India's peacekeeping contributions to UN missions, and counter-terrorism coordination with neighbouring countries.

Trade and Economy: FTAs and EPA negotiations India is pursuing, WTO dispute settlements involving India, commodity price shocks affecting India's import bill, and supply chain diversification initiatives (China plus one strategy participation).

Climate and Environment: COP summit outcomes, India's Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) progress, international climate finance commitments, and multilateral initiatives on green hydrogen, lithium alliance, and solar energy.

4. Gujarat Current Affairs — Political, Economic and Administrative

Gujarat-specific current affairs form the most differentiated segment of the GPSC exam and require dedicated, sustained preparation:

State Budget and Economy: Annual Gujarat Budget highlights including revenue and capital expenditure allocation, subsidy distribution, tax revenue trends, MSME and industrial investment figures, GSDP growth rate, and sector-wise contribution (agriculture, industry, services). Pay attention to the Chief Minister's budget speech themes and any new industrial policy announcements.

Flagship Schemes of Gujarat Government: Detailed understanding of each major scheme including full name, launching year, implementing department, target beneficiaries, financial outlay, and current coverage/delivery statistics. Examples include Bhagyashree Lakshmi, Kanya Vivah Yojana, Miyani Yojana, Gujarat Green Revolution Yojana, Ambika Kranti Yojana, and various farmer and worker welfare programmes. Recent scheme integrations or replacements must also be noted.

Infrastructure and Industrial Projects: Smart City Gujarat progress, Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) nodes in Gujarat (Dholera SIR, Kheda), port-led development (Pragati Mahalanobis Port, Pipavia Port), renewable energy projects (Ghanta Garhu wind, solar parks), and major highway/railway projects initiated or completed during the review period.

Agriculture and Rural Development: Crop production statistics, horticulture missions, dairy development initiatives, micro-irrigation coverage, and land record digitization (Bhulekh) updates. Gujarat's unique agricultural profile—groundnut, cumin, castor, cotton, and dairy—should be kept in mind.

Environment and Ecology Specific to Gujarat: Gir lion conservation updates, marine national park developments, Rann of Kutch ecological concerns, pollution monitoring in industrial belts (Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Ankleshwar), water resource management (percolation tanks, check dams), and forest cover reporting by FSI.

Administrative and Governance Reforms: District administration innovations, e-governance initiatives by the Gujarat state IT mission, public grievance redressal mechanisms, municipal corporation reforms, and Panchayati Raj modernization efforts in the state.

Gujarat Culture, Art, Literature, and Heritage: Recent UNESCO recognitions, national awards to Gujarati artists and writers, preservation projects for stepwells and historical monuments, and festivals receiving government support or international visibility.

5. Awards, Honours, Sports and Culture

This section covers recognitions and achievements frequently asked in objective and descriptive formats:

National and International Awards: Bharat Ratna, Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri recipients with category and citing work; Jnanpith Award, Sahitya Akademi Award, Kalaimamani, Dadasaheb Phalke Award, Nehru Prize, Gandhi Peace Prize, Indira Gandhi Prize, Ramon Magsaysay Award, and major international literary and scientific prizes won by Indians.

Sports Achievements: Olympic, Asian Games, Commonwealth Games, and World Championship medalists from India with sport discipline, event, and medal colour. Recognitions like Arjuna Award, Khel Ratna, Dhyan Chand Award, and Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna latest recipients. Also track Paralympic achievements and emerging sports disciplines where India is gaining visibility.

Cultural and Literary Recognition: Gujarati language and literature awards both state and national level, folk art and traditional craft recognitions, film awards with Indian relevance, and music and dance academy honours.

Important Days and Themes: UN and India-observed international days with their annual themes during the review period. These commonly include World Environment Day, International Yoga Day, World Health Day, Human Rights Day, and India-specific commemorations like Republic Day, Independence Day, and Gandhi Jayanti themes.

Books and Authors: Recently published influential books by Indian authors, especially those related to polity, governance, history, and Gujarat's development. Note the publisher and central thesis if relevant.

6. Science, Technology and Innovation

Science and technology current affairs intersect heavily with GS Paper I and General Studies in GPSC. The following domains should be monitored:

Space Technology: ISRO missions including Chandrayaan series, Gaganyaan programme milestones, Aditya-L1, small satellite launches, GSLV and PSLV operational updates, and India's collaboration with NASA, Roscosmos, ESA, and JAXA on specific projects.

Defence Technology: Agnipath scheme details and implementation status, indigenous aircraft carrier INS Vikrant commissioning and subsequent vessels, Tejas fighter variants, Arjun tank upgrades, BrahMos missile exports, UAV/drone indigenisation programmes, and next-generation warship developments.

Digital Infrastructure and Governance: ONDC rollout status, Account Aggregator framework expansion, India Stack components, UPI transaction volume and international partnerships, Digital India landmarks, state-level digital service integration, and cybersecurity incidents affecting India.

Biotechnology and Health Innovation: Indigenous vaccine development, pharmaceutical export milestones, genome sequencing initiatives, public health technology deployments, and clinical trial regulatory updates.

Green and Renewable Energy: India's international solar alliance contributions, green hydrogen mission progress, biofuel blending targets, electric vehicle ecosystem development, and battery storage pilot projects.

Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Tech: National AI strategy implementation, Bhashini language AI progress, semiconductor fab developments, quantum computing research by Indian institutions, and regulatory frameworks for AI deployment.

7. Environment, Ecology and Climate Action

Environmental current affairs carry increasing weightage as climate change dominates both national and international policy discourse:

Conservation Milestones: New wildlife sanctuaries and national parks notified, species listed under Schedule I of the Wildlife Protection Act, tiger reserve expansions,Project Elephant and Project Tiger status reports, crocodile conservation project updates, and gharial or vulture breeding centre developments.

Pollution and Air Quality: NGT orders on air pollution in Delhi-NCR and other cities, solid waste management rule enforcement, plastic waste regulation updates, noise pollution control measures, and industrial effluent violations with regulatory action taken.

Climate Commitments and Policy: India's Panchamrita commitments progress report, net-zero emissions pathway announcements, carbon credit trading market framework, coal transition strategies, and state-level climate action plans submitted to the central ministry.

Forest and Land Use: India State of Forest Report (ISFR) findings on forest and tree cover change, green cover in urban areas, afforestation campaigns, eco-sensitive zone notifications, and conflict between development projects and environmental clearance.

Water and Ocean Management: Amrit Bharat Station Scheme water initiatives, river linking progress updates, Interlinking of Rivers status, Ganga and Yamuna cleaning programme expenditures and outcomes, coastal regulation zone notifications, and marine pollution incidents affecting Indian shores.

Disaster Management: IPCC report highlights relevant to India, extreme weather events (cyclones, floods, heatwaves) during the review period, NDMA guidelines issued, and early warning system improvements implemented by IMD.

8. High-Yield Revision Points and Common Question Patterns

To maximize score efficiency, focus revision on these recurring question patterns and high-yield fact categories specific to GPSC:

Assertion-Reason Statements: Many Prelims questions present an assertion about a policy outcome or scheme impact alongside a reason explaining the rationale. Distinguish between schemes that are centrally funded versus state-funded, and between mandatory and voluntary participation programmes.

Match-the-Following: Frequently asked in the format of scheme-launching ministry, award-recipient-field pairing, index-country-rank triads, and initiative-host organization matching. Practice building paired recall tables rather than memorizing long lists.

Statement-Based Questions: Three-statement questions test whether you can separate genuinely reported facts from plausible-sounding but incorrect claims. Read every statement independently before selecting the answer option.

Gujarat-Specific Data Recall: Memorize Gujarat's current population rank among states, GSDP growth figures from recent budgets, number of SMVTIs and SEZs, district count, major port names and locations, and the names of Gujarat's Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha seat counts.

National Data Recall: India's population rank globally, GDP rank, per capita income figures, fiscal deficit percentage, inflation target band, literacy rate, sex ratio, and HDI rank. These figures are regularly refreshed and should be verified against the latest Economic Survey or NSO release.

Scheme Conversion Tracking: When a scheme is renamed, merged, or replaced, questions often ask about the original scheme name or the combining entity. Maintain a conversion log: old name → new name → effective date.

Glossary Anchors: For every major scheme or programme, know its acronym expansion, launching year, parent ministry/department, and beneficiary category. GPSC frequently asks 'which of the following schemes was launched in the year…' or 'which ministry administers X scheme?'

Important facts

  • GPSC current affairs coverage should span approximately 18–24 months before the notification date, with emphasis on events after the preceding exam cycle.
  • National, International, and Gujarat current affairs each form distinct but overlapping question sets in the Prelims General Studies paper.
  • Gujarat's economy is one of the largest in India by GSDP; industrial investment treaties, port infrastructure, and renewable energy capacity are repeatedly tested.
  • The Rann of Kutch, Gir National Park, and Gulf of Kutch Marine National Park are ecologically significant Gujarat-specific entities often referenced in environment questions.
  • Gujarat has the longest coastline among Indian states at approximately 1,600 km, hosting major ports including Kandla, Mundra, Pipavav, and upcoming Pragati Mahalanobis Port.
  • Gujarat currently has 33 districts, 45 Lok Sabha seats, and 20 Rajya Sabha seats.
  • Central schemes relevant to Gujarat aspirants must be understood alongside their Gujarat-state implementation variants or sub-schemes.
  • India's G20 Presidency in 2023 produced outcomes on digital public infrastructure, green development, and multilateral reform that continue to feature in current affairs questions.
  • ISRO's Chandrayaan-3 landing at the lunar south pole in 2023 made India the fourth nation to achieve a soft landing on the Moon.
  • The Agnipath Scheme, introduced in 2022, is a significant defence recruitment reform that has featured in polity and current affairs questions.
  • Gujarat Green Revolution Yojana and Ambika Kranti Yojana are prominent state schemes targeting agricultural productivity and crop diversification respectively.
  • NITI Aayog's SDG India Index publishes annual district and state-level progress rankings that are frequently cited inGPSC questions.
  • India's coal dependency, renewable energy targets of 500 GW by 2030, and international solar alliance leadership are recurring thematic anchors.
