GPSCEnglishChapter Test

This is Part 1 of a 30-question Chapter Test on Vocabulary for the GPSC English Language paper. This part contains 12 questions covering different sub-aspects of the chapter: synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution, idioms & phrases, phrasal verbs, word usage in context, homophones, spelling-based errors, and prefixes/suffixes. Each question carries equal marks. Choose the single best option for each question. Total duration for all 3 parts combined is recommended at 45 minutes.
