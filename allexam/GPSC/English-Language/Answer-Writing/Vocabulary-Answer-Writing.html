Write a paragraph of approximately 120–150 words on 'The Role of Digital Literacy in Rural Empowerment in Gujarat'. Use at least five advanced vocabulary words correctly and ensure precise diction throughout.

Suggested points

  • Introduce digital literacy with a precise definition and its relevance to rural Gujarat
  • Discuss the proliferating reach of smartphones and internet connectivity in villages
  • Explain how digital literacy empowers farmers through e-NAM, weather forecasts, and market prices
  • Highlight women's empowerment via online banking, education portals, and government scheme awareness
  • Acknowledge persistent challenges such as infrastructural deficits and linguistic barriers
  • Conclude with a forward-looking statement on sustained digital inclusion
  • Use vocabulary words such as: ubiquitous, empowering, bridging, marginalised, dissemination, transformative

Write a formal letter to the District Collector requesting improved sanitation infrastructure in your taluka. Employ formal register, elevated vocabulary, and coherent organisation within 200–250 words.

Suggested points

  • Correct formal letter format: sender's address, date, receiver's designation and address, subject line, salutation
  • State the purpose clearly in the opening sentence with respectful yet firm language
  • Describe the current deplorable sanitation conditions with specific vocabulary: dilapidated, insanitary, exacerbating
  • Reference relevant government schemes or past assurances if applicable
  • Propose concrete, feasible suggestions rather than mere complaints
  • Close with an expectant yet courteous tone, using words like: expedite, imperative, conducive
  • Maintain formal register consistently; avoid colloquial expressions

Write a concise precis of approximately 60–80 words on the passage below, preserving the core message with accurate vocabulary substitution.

Suggested points

  • Read the passage thoroughly at least twice before drafting
  • Identify the central theme and eliminate examples, illustrations, and repetitions
  • Rewrite the essence in your own words using precise and economical language
  • Ensure the precis is one-third to one-fourth the length of the original
  • Maintain objective tone; do not insert personal opinion
  • Check grammar, coherence, and logical flow after drafting
  • Replace wordy phrases with single appropriate words: e.g., 'due to the fact that' → 'because', 'in spite of the fact that' → 'although'

Write an essay of approximately 300–350 words on 'Conservation of Wildlife: Balancing Development and Biodiversity in Gujarat'. Demonstrate command over vocabulary, thematic development, and structured argumentation.

Suggested points

  • Begin with an engaging introduction defining the tension between development and conservation
  • Provide factual context: Gujarat's wildlife heritage — Gir Forest, Rann of Kutch, marine biodiversity
  • Discuss the imperatives of conservation: ecological balance, genetic diversity, climate resilience
  • Acknowledge developmental needs: industrialisation, infrastructure, habitat encroachment
  • Present case studies: successful coexistence models, community conservation initiatives
  • Offer policy-level recommendations: sustainable planning, wildlife corridors, green technology
  • Conclude with a balanced synthesis advocating harmonious progress
  • Employ vocabulary strategically: symbiosis, encroachment, biodiversity, unsustainable, preservation, coexistence

Write a paragraph of 100–120 words on 'The Significance of Mother Tongue in Early Childhood Education'. Use at least four vocabulary words from the list provided and avoid repetition of common words.

Suggested points

  • Opening: state the fundamental role of mother tongue in cognitive and emotional development
  • Explain how early education in mother tongue facilitates comprehension and retention
  • Discuss the psychological comfort and identity formation linked to one's native language
  • Address the danger of premature transition to unfamiliar languages causing learning deficits
  • Reference educational policies or research supporting mother-tongue-based instruction

Write an analytical paragraph of 120–150 words on 'The Impact of Social Media on Youth Political Awareness in India'. Use precise vocabulary and maintain logical progression of ideas.

Suggested points

  • Introduce the paradigm shift in political communication driven by social media platforms
  • Analyse how platforms like Twitter, Instagram, and YouTube have democratized political discourse among youth
  • Discuss positive impacts: heightened awareness, grassroots mobilisation, accountability culture
  • Address negative dimensions: misinformation, echo chambers, superficial engagement
  • Mention the role of regional language content in reaching non-urban youth
  • Conclude with a measured assessment calling for digital literacy alongside political awareness
  • Use vocabulary: democratisation, discourse, mobilisation, misinformation, superficial, discernment
