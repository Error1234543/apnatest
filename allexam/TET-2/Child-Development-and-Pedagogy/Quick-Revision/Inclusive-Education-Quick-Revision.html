TET-2 | Bala Vikas ani Shikshana Vidha | Samaveshi Shiksha | Quick Revision

TET-2GujaratiQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Samaveshi Shiksha: Eka pratikriya shiksha paddhati aa che jema sabhi bakra (divyang, janjat, tribal, sthaneeya bhasa, dharm, ling, arthik sthiti aadi antargat) ni samannan avsadhu keval shikshalaya ma pravesu karva naa, parantu pratyek bakra ni avashyakta anusaar upyogita sudharva, shikshak no roop badlavvu, aani shikshana mahatva sudharva. Iya UNESCO te paribhashit thiye chhe.
  • Samaveshi Shikshana Maargdarshan: Aadhunik shikshana vidhana ma jama pade chhe ke shikshalaya eko aise mahatva rakhe jo pratyek sthala ni samman kare, bheda-bhav na hu, aani har ek bakra no vikas theti saral banave.
  • PwD (Viklangta Athey Bakra): Bharatiya Kanoon anusaar PwD ma bhautik, buddhik, sharirik, manovigyanik, sankrami, aani anya prakaro no vikang aapadta hai. 2016 no adhiniyam 21 prakara no vikang nirdhist karse chhe.
  • Mainsreaming: Viklang bakrona samajik evam shikshnak sansthan ma samannan abhivanthan karva na process ne aakhya the chhe, jema iya shikshakalon ma samannan praves u aaviye che. Samaveshi shiksha thyo ek vastupradhan roop ahe.
  • Sahayataathik Shikshana: Jethi viklang bakrono vikas thyo shikshakalon ma madat milti re chhe — jema resource room, special educator, assistive technology, aani accommodation.

Key concepts

  • Nandishwari Committee Report (1993): Ekti sandarbha jema Nandishwari Samiti ne Samaveshi Shiksha na mukhya siddhant no vivaran kariye chhe. I report te Samaveshi Shiksha ni vyakhya aani ushthaapan kashiya gaya chhe jema shiksha pravihar na sudharva no uttaradhikar aayo chhe.
  • UNCRC (1989) Article 23: Bharat sarkar dvara ratifit thiyo international treaty no hisabo, jema shishu adhikar no vivaran aapo chhe. Viklang bakra no adhikar, samman, aani samannan shiksha ne prapti karyakari banavi.
  • UN Convention on Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD, 2006): Bharat ne 2007 ma ratify kariye thiyo. I document ne viklang bakra no samannan shiksha, access, social participation aani dignity ni assurance give kari chhe. TET-2 ma bhut important.
  • RPWD Act 2016 (Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act): 20 Indian Parliament ne pasa kariye thiyo. I act mela 21 categories no viklangta nirdhista thiye. Shiksha ma samannan access, reservation aani negative discrimination no protishodh kariye chhe. 4% reservation educational institutions ma mandate.
  • NEP 2020: National Education Policy ne inclusive education ni bahut mahattva aapo chhe. Mother tongue/local language medium, sanskriti, regional variation no somadhan aapo chhe. Shikshak training, resource availability aani digital inclusion par vishesh jogavad aapo chi.
  • NCERT Guidelines: Samaveshi Shiksha no implementation kashiya thiye. Barrier-free environment, teacher training, curriculum flexibility, adaptive learning material, assessment accommodation — ei sarva NCERT te mention thi chhe.
  • Barriers to Inclusion: Bhautik (staircase, ramp), samajik (bheda-bhav, stereotypes), shikshak (training na huni), curriculum (rigid), methodology (standardized assessment). Exam ma ei barriers ni pehchan khub important.
  • Universal Design for Learning (UDL): Ek concept ji school environment, teaching methods, assessment materials ne har bakra access ability de chhe. Multi-modal presentation, multiple means of expression aani engagement UDL ne center ma rakhse chhe.
  • Aids and Assistive Technology: Prosthetic, hearing aids, communication boards, screen readers, braille, adapted furniture, sign language interpretation, speech therapy equipment — ei sabhi inclusive classroom ma madat karva kaam aaptha re.
  • Gifted Children & Inclusion: Samaveshi shiksha hevin gifted children (advanced ability) ni bhi aapatti re. Special provision, acceleration, enriched curriculum gifted bakrono samavesh karva no ansh ahe.

Important facts

  • 2016 no RPWD Act ma 21 categories no viklangta nirdisht thi chhe. Pichhla 1995 ke act ma sirf 7 categories thi thi.
  • Constitution Amendment 86 (2002): Article 21A ne add karike shishu ni mutuk shiksha (6-14 varsh) ne right to education banavva ma aay vi.
  • Article 21A: Sarvabhavarupen free aani compulsory shiksha right no roop ma nirdhishta che. I law bakra ni shiksha ni sutra ni rakhse.
  • Article 46: State ne minority or disadvantaged group bakrona educational and economic interests no protection karva no dharm di chhe.
  • Article 41: Right to work, education aani public assistance no mention chhe. Disabled bakro ni help ke right vi aapo chhe.
  • Section 16-19 RPWD Act 2016: Shiksha ma negative discrimination, accessibility, accommodation aani special training no mention kare chhe.
  • Shiksha aane ni umar (6-14) free aani compulsory right aapo chhe. Viklang bakra ni bhi same right.
  • Samaveshi shiksha ma har bakra kshetra (ability, religion, caste, gender, economic status, language, location) no abhiman re.
  • NCERT ke 'Inclusive Education Handbook' published thi chhe jo teacher guide kare chhe ke inclusive classroom kasya plan karyu chhe.
  • UDISE+ Data (2021-22): Bharat ma approx 2.5 million shishu samaveshi shikshalaya ma study keche. Pichhla data compare kare to barhtiya dekhyu chhe.
  • Special educator, counselor, assistant teacher — samaveshi shiksha ma ei roles bahut important re.
  • Sign Language (Indian Sign Language) national recognition aapo chhe. Shiksha ma ISL ko use madatgar.
  • Transportation subsidy, hostel facility, scholarship — RPWD Act 2016 ma ei support available che.
  • Positive discrimination (affirmative action) — disadvantaged bakro ni support karva khatir special provisions aapo chu, ei principle inclusive education no bhag re.
  • TET-2 ma exam pattern: MCQ paper. Child Dev + Pedagogy (BPSDP) section ma 20-30 questions aaviye che. Inclusive Education topic ma 3-5 questions regular aaptha re.

Memory aids

  • "IDEA" mnemonic: Inclusive Education means Diversity, Equity, Access — ei charo shabd samaveshi shiksha no core bindu ni yaad karve madat kare.
  • "UDL Triangle": Presentation, Representation, Action/Expression — UDL no triangle yaad rakhva mate ei tin angles samjhau che ki samaveshi method kasya kaam aape.
  • "21 Categories": RPWD Act 2016 ma 21 types no viklangta ne yaad rakhva mate 'Buddhi-Karjan-Lekh-Natak-Swasthya-Aankh-Kaan-Drishti-Paon-Mansik-Samachaar-Sparsh-Bhasha-Chal-Gati-Sanskritik-Yuva-Samudai-Aarthik-Jeevan-Sahayog' (abbreviated mnemonic) use kariiye.
  • "Constitution 86 = 21A" mnemonic: Constitution amendment 86 ne Article 21A with connect karo. 86th amendment = 21A article.
  • "NANDI = Samaveshi" mnemonic: Nandishwari Committee ne yaad rakhva mate 'NANDI' word use karo ji committee name ni pehli charo letter aapo chhe.
  • "PwD = People with Disabilities" mnemonic: acronym yaad rakhva mate PwD ne 'Power with Diversity' ni roop ma yaad rakhu je practice inclusive bakrona power increase kari chhe.
  • "4% Reservation": RPWD Act ma 4% reservation educational institutions ma yaad rakhvu mate '4P' mnemonic use karo: Practice, Placement, Progress, Participation.
  • "3 Barriers Mnemonic": Bhautik, Samajik, Shikshak — ei tin types no barrier yaad rakhvu mate 'BSB' mnemonic use karo.

Exam tips

  • TET-2 ma BPSDP paper ma 20-30 questions aaptha re. Inclusive Education topic se directly 3-5 questions standard aaptha re, sometimes assertion-reason, statement-based, aani case-based formats ma.
  • Jebhgi exam ma 'Which of the following is NOT a principle of inclusive education?' type question aase, then option ne carefully padho. Negative question standard trap.
  • Case-based questions ma specific bakra ni situation aapo chhe aani correct pedagogical response choice karva pade. Method focus na, bakra focus.
  • Assertion-Reason questions ma dono statement separately check karo — Assertion sahi ho, Reason sahi ho, but Reason ka Assertion no correct explanation re che ke nahi.
  • Legal framework questions (Act, Amendment, Article) ne correct article number, year aani section yaad rakhu. Year confusion kam kare.
  • NCERT handbook, UDL, IDEA, Nandishwari committee — ei terms direct yaad rakhu. MCQ ma options ma ye terms appear thay.
  • Differentiation technique, Universal Design for Learning, Positive Discrimination — ei three terms exam ma regularly aaptha re. Unaro proper samjhau.
  • Viklangta categories ne confuse na karvu. 2016 act ma 21 categories che — ei fact important re.
  • Practice karva mate PYQ aani previous question papers dekho. Trend analyze karva madat kari she.
  • Note: TET-2 ma difficulty level moderate aapthi re. Concept understanding exam ma important re, rote memorization nahi.

Common traps

  • "Mainstreaming" aani "Inclusive Education" ne alag karna important re. Mainstreaming sirf shikshakalon ma pravesu kariye che; inclusive education system level ma change kase che. Exam ma ei donon concept ne compare karve question aapiye sakte.
  • "Integration" vs "Inclusion" confuse na hunu mate: Integration ma student ne existing system ma fit kariye chhe; Inclusion ma system ni background student anusaar badalathi re. Inclusive system student-centered re.
  • RPWD Act 2016 ma 7 nahi, 21 categories haju chu — purano 1995 act ni detail galti the exam ma. Pichhla 7 categories only thi: blindness, low vision, leprosy cured, hearing impairment, locomotor disability, mental retardation, muscular dystrophy.
  • Constitution Article 21A free shiksha 6-14 age group ne mention kare che. 14-18 age free nahi chhe — ei condition yaad rakhva jarura re.
  • UDISE+ data 2.5 million shishu ke correct samjhu — approximate figure, exact number change sakte.
  • Reservation 4% educational institutions ma, govt job ma nahi. Job reservation alag kanoon ma mention thi che. Confusion na karvu.
  • Sign Language national recognition aapo chhe, but itihas ma history exam ma aaviye sakte ke ISL kab official recognition padi?
  • NCERT Handbook correct yaad rakhva — handbook title 'Inclusive Education: A Handbook for Teachers' thi chhe. Title exact match karva jarura.
  • Gifted children ne samaveshi education ma shamil kariye kya? Haan, because inclusive education sabhi bakro samil karti che including gifted. Some trick question aavi sakte.
  • Nandishwari Committee 1991 thi nahi, 1993 thi report publish thi. Year confuse na karvu.
