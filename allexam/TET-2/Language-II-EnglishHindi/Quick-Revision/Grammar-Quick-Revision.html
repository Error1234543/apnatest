TET-2: બીજી ભાષા (ઈંગ્રેજી/હિન્દી) - યાકરણી (Grammar) - ઝડપી રિવિઝન

Definitions

  • Part of Speech (બ્રહ્મવાચક/અક્ષરપ્રકાર): દરેક શબ્દ તેના અર્થ અને વાક્યમાં કાર્ય પ્રમાણે એક અ ચોક્કસ ભાગમાં ગણાય છે. ઈંગ્રેજીમાં ૮ બ્રહ્મવાચકો છે: Noun (નામ), Pronoun (પ્રત્યય), Adjective (વિશેષણ), Verb (ક્રિયાપદ), Adverb (વિશેષણ - ક્રિયા/વિશેષણ), Preposition (સામીપ્ય/અધ્યયન), Conjunction (સંયોજક), Interjection (વિસ્મયાદિ).
  • Tense (કાળ): ક્રિયાપદની કાળજન્ય ક્રિયા જે સમયમાં થાય છે તે દર્શાવે છે. ઈંગ્રેજીમાં ૩ મુખ્ય કાળ છે: Present, Past, Future. દરેક કાળની ૪ અવસ્થાઓ (Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous) હોય છે, તેથી કુલ ૧૨ કાળો.
  • Voice (કારક): વાક્યનો ખરડો દર્શાવે છે કે કર્તા પ્રધાન છે (Active Voice - સકર્મક) કે કર્મ પ્રધાન (Passive Voice - નિષ્કર્મક). Active: કર્તા → ક્રિયા → કર્મ. Passive: કર્મ + be + V3 + by + કર્તા.
  • Direct & Indirect Speech (પરોક્ષ અનુષંગી વાણી): સીધી વાણી (Direct) માં વક્તાના શબ્દો જણાય છે. પરોક્ષ વાણી (Indirect) માં શબ્દોનું રૂપાંતર કરીને સાંભળનાર દ્વારા જણાવવામાં આવે છે. સૂત્ર: Reporting verb + that/if/wh-word + કાળમાં ફેરફાર + time-reference changes.
  • Subject-Verb Agreement (વિધેય-કર્તૃ સંમતિ): વાક્યનો કર્તા અને કર્મ (subject) અને ક્રિયાપદ (verb) એકબીજાની સાથે સંમત હોવા જોઈએ. Singular subject → singular verb; Plural subject → plural verb.
  • Clause (વાક્યભાગ): વાક્યનો એક ભાગ જે પોતાનો કર્તા અને ક્રિયાપદ ધરાવે છે. Main clause (મુખ્ય વાક્ય) સ્વતંત્ર રહે છે. Subordinate clause (અવતંત્ર વાક્ય) main clause સાથે જોડાયેલો હોય છે. Relative clause, Noun clause, Adverbial clause હોય છે.
  • Modal Verbs (મદદગાર ક્રિયાપદો): Can, could, may, might, will, would, shall, should, must, ought to. આ મદદગાર ક્રિયાપદો વાક્યમાં શક્યતા, ફરજ, સલાહ, ભવિષ્ય, અનુમતિ વગેરે દર્શાવે છે.
  • Preposition (સામીપ્ય): બે શબ્દો વચ્ચેનો સંબંધ દર્શાવતો શબ્દ. To, from, at, in, on, by, with, for, about, between, among વગેરે.
  • Conjunction (સંયોજક): બે શબ્દો, વાક્યભાગો કે વાક્યોને જોડતો શબ્દ. Coordinating (FANBOYS: for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so), Subordinating (because, although, if, when, since, unless), Correlative (either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also).

Key concepts

  • ક્રિયાપદના ૧૨ કાળો (કુલ ૧૨ tense forms):
    • Present Simple: S + V1(s/es) + O — daily habits, facts.
    • Present Continuous: S + am/is/are + V1+ing — happening now.
    • Present Perfect: S + has/have + V3 — completed action with present result.
    • Present Perfect Continuous: S + has/have + been + V1+ing — started in past, continuing.
    • Past Simple: S + V2 — completed past action.
    • Past Continuous: S + was/were + V1+ing — ongoing past action.
    • Past Perfect: S + had + V3 — action before another past action.
    • Past Perfect Continuous: S + had + been + V1+ing.
    • Future Simple: S + will/shall + V1.
    • Future Continuous: S + will be + V1+ing.
    • Future Perfect: S + will have + V3.
    • Future Perfect Continuous: S + will have been + V1+ing.
  • ક્રિયાપદ સંમતિના મુખ્ય નિયમો:
    • Singular subject → singular verb (He goes, She sings).
    • Plural subject → plural verb (They go, The boys sing).
    • 'Each/every/no one/anyone' → singular verb (Each student has...).
    • 'And' જોડાયેલ બે વિશેષણો → plural verb (Rama and Shyam are...).
    • Collective noun (team, committee, jury) → singular or plural based on meaning.
    • 'Either/or, Neither/nor' → verb agrees with nearest subject.
    • Infinitive/gerund as subject → singular verb (To err is human. Swimming is good.)
    • 'The number of' → singular; 'A number of' → plural.
  • Active-Passive Voice ના નિયમો:
    • Object of active → Subject of passive.
    • 'be' verb adds tense match: is/am/are → being → been.
    • Present Simple: does → is done. Past Simple: did → was done.
    • Modal verbs: can be done, must be done.
    • Intransitive verbs generally cannot become passive.
    • Impersonal passive: 'It is said that...' / 'He is said to...'
    • Double object: Give him a book → He is given a book / A book is given to him.
  • Direct to Indirect Speech ના નિયમો:
    • Tense shifts: Present → Past, Past → Past Perfect, Future → Conditional.
    • Exceptions: Universal truth, habitual action, reporting verb in present tense.
    • Interrogative sentences: 'if/whether' for yes/no; question word retained for WH questions.
    • Imperative: Tell/ask/order + to-infinitive.
    • Exclamatory: exclaim with joy/sorrow that.
    • Time changes: today→that day, here→there, now→then, yesterday→the day before.
    • Pronoun changes: I→he/she, my→his/her, you→he/she.
  • કુલ્સ (Clauses) ની પ્રકારો:
    • Noun Clause: વાક્યમાં નામ તરીકે કાર્ય કરે. e.g., What he said is true.
    • Adjective (Relative) Clause: નામ/પ્રત્યયનું વિશેષણ. e.g., The boy who is playing is my brother.
    • Adverb Clause: ક્રિયા/વિશેષણનું વિશેષણ. e.g., When he came, I left.
    • Relative pronouns: who, whom, whose, which, that.
    • Subordinating conjunctions: because, although, if, when, while, since, unless.
  • Modals ના ઉપયોગ:
    • Can/Could: ability, possibility, request.
    • May/Might: permission, possibility.
    • Will/Would: future, willingness, conditional.
    • Shall/Should: suggestion, obligation.
    • Must: strong obligation, necessity.
    • Ought to: moral duty.
    • Need: necessity.
    • Dare: challenge (rare).
  • Prepositions ના મુખ્ય ઉપયોગો:
    • Time: at (specific time), on (days/dates), in (months/years/seasons).
    • Place: at (point), in (enclosed space), on (surface).
    • Direction/Motion: to, towards, into, onto, through, across.
    • Manner: by (method), with (instrument), in (manner).
    • Reason/Purpose: for, because of, due to, owing to.
    • Common pairs: agree with, afraid of, angry at/with, belong to, depend on, interested in, proud of.

Important facts

  • TET-2 Grammar માંથી સામાન્ય રીતે ૫-૮ પ્રશ્નો આવે છે જે Mainly Active-Passive, Direct-Indirect Speech, Tense, Subject-Verb Agreement, Prepositions અને Conjunctions પર આધારિત હોય છે.
  • સૌથી વધુ માંગ શકતો વિષય: Direct-Indirect Speech અને Active-Passive Transformation (પરિવર્તન). આ બંનેના transformation rules આપણે યાદ રાખવા જોઈએ.
  • ત્રુટિ શોધવાના પ્રશ્નોમાં સૌથી વધુ ગડબડ થતી જગ્યા: Subject-Verb Agreement, Wrong preposition, Wrong tense usage, Wrong article (a/an/the).
  • Fill-in-the-blanks માં સૌથી વધુ આવતા વિષયો: Prepositions (at/on/in), Articles (a/an/the), Modals, Conjunctions.
  • Sentence improvement માં પ્રસંગે પ્રસંગે: Wrong verb form, Wrong preposition after verb, Double comparison, Redundancy.
  • Transformation questions examples:
    • Active→Passive: The cat catches the mouse. → The mouse is caught by the cat.
    • Direct→Indirect: He said, 'I am tired.' → He said that he was tired.
    • Simple→Complex: He worked hard. He passed. → Because he worked hard, he passed.
  • Reported speech માં exception: If reporting verb is in present/future tense (says/say/said), tense does not change. e.g., He says, 'I am happy.' → He says that he is happy.
  • Relative clause માં 'that' vs 'which': 'That' definite/restrictive, 'which' non-restrictive (with commas). e.g., The book that I bought is good. / The book, which I bought, is good.
  • Parallel structure: યાદ રાખો — coordinating conjunction (and, but, or) જોડતી items સમાન grammatical formની હોવી જોઈએ. e.g., She likes reading, writing and to swim. (Wrong) → She likes reading, writing and swimming. (Right)
  • Comparisons માં ખાસ ધ્યાન: Positive → Comparative → Superlative. Irregular: good/better/best, bad/worse/worst, far/farther/farthest. Use 'than' with comparative. Use 'most/mostest' with superlative.

Memory aids

  • FANBOYS — Coordinating conjunctions યાદ રાખવા: For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So. (ફોર્મ્યુલા: F-A-N-B-O-Y-S)
  • Tense transformation mnemonic: DO → DID → DONE — Simple present 'do', simple past 'did', past participle 'done'. દરેક tense ના transformation માં આ સ્પષ્ટતા મદદરૂપ થાય.
  • Direct-Indirect tense shift chart: Present Simple → Past Simple; Present Continuous → Past Continuous; Present Perfect → Past Perfect; Past Simple → Past Perfect; Future → Would + V1.
  • Voice transformation formula: Object + appropriate form of 'be' + V3 + by + Subject. 'be' નું સ્વરૂપ tense પર આધારિત: is/am/are → was/were → being → been.
  • Preposition time table: at → clock time, on → day/date, in → month/year/season/hour (afternoon, evening).
  • Subject-Verb Agreement quick check: કર્તાનું સ્વરૂપ જોવું. Singular → single verb form; Plural → plural verb form. 'Each/every/any/no' + singular noun → singular verb.
  • Reported speech pronoun chart: I→he/she, we→they, my→his/her, our→their, me→him/her, us→them, mine→his/hers, ours→theirs.
  • Relative pronoun rule: who → person (subject), whom → person (object), whose → possession, which → thing/animal, that → person/thing (restrictive), where → place, when → time, why → reason.

Exam tips

  • TET-2 grammar પ્રશ્નોમાં transformation (voice/speech) પ્રથમ કરો — આમાં નિયમો સ્પષ્ટ છે અને ઝડપથી ઉકેલી શકાય છે.
  • Error detection પ્રશ્નમાં સૌથી પહેલા verb જુઓ (tense/agreement), phir preposition/article, phir pronoun/reference.
  • Fill in the blanks માં option test કરો — જો બે options ઠીક લાગતા હોય તો sentence structure જુઓ.
  • Direct-Indirect માં reporting verb શોધો. If 'said' → that/if. If command/request → to-infinitive. If question → wh-word/if.
  • Subject-Verb Agreement માં કર્તા શોધવા માટે intermediate words bypass કરો. e.g., The box of toys IS/ARE on the table. → IS (box singular).
  • Preposition fill-ups માં fixed pair યાદ રાખો — fixed collocations છે (depend ON, afraid OF, proud OF, etc.).
  • Question tag: positive statement → negative tag; negative statement → positive tag. e.g., He is coming, isn't he? / She doesn't know, does she?
  • Comparison માં 'than' જરૂરી છે જ્યારે comparative degree હોય. e.g., She is taller THAN her sister. (not 'from' અથવા 'of').
  • Paragraph jumble પ્રશ્નોમાં: first sentence માં general statement અથવા time reference હોય. last sentence માં conclusion. connecting words (however, therefore, because) ધ્યાનથી જુઓ.
  • Time management: grammar section માં ૧ પ્રશ્નને ૧ મિનિટથી વધુ ન લગાડવો. Transformations અને error detection પ્રશ્નો પહેલાં કરો, પછી comprehension/part jumble.

Common traps

  • Trap 1: 'One of the + plural noun + relative clause' માં verb_singular હોય. e.g., One of the students who IS/ARE late. → ARE (who refers to students, plural). ઘણા વિદ્યાર્થીઓ 'One of' સાથે singular લખે છે, પણ વાસ્તવિક કર્તા plural છે.
  • Trap 2: 'Both ... and' સદૈવ plural verb લાવે. e.g., Both Rama and Shyam IS/ARE coming. → ARE.
  • Trap 3: 'There is/are' માં nearest noun જોવો. e.g., There IS/ARE many books on the table. → ARE (books plural).
  • Trap 4: Passive voice માં intransitive verb નું ઉપયોગ. e.g., 'He died.' → 'He was died.' (Wrong). Intransitive verbs have no passive form.
  • Trap 5: Direct speech માં universal truth નું tense ન બદલાય. e.g., He said, 'The sun rises in the east.' → He said that the sun rises in the east. (Not 'rose').
  • Trap 6: 'Enough' placement. Adjective+enough: beautiful enough. Noun+enough: enough money. Not 'enough beautiful' અથવા 'money enough' (અપવાદ સિવાય).
  • Trap 7: Article usage with unique things — the sun, the moon, the earth, the sky, the universe. e.g., We see THE sun every day.
  • Trap 8: Preposition after adjective. e.g., afraid OF, good AT, proud OF, interested IN, familiar WITH, different FROM. ઘણીવાર ભૂલ થાય છે.
  • Trap 9: 'Let's' માં પ્રશ્નાત્મક રૂપ: Let's go, shall we? / Let us go, will you? (different meanings: suggestion vs request).
  • Trap 10: 'Used to' vs 'be used to' vs 'get used to'. Used to + V1 (past habit). Be used to + V-ing (accustomed). Get used to + V-ing (becoming accustomed).
