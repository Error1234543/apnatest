Important facts

  • Chandrayaan-3 successfully landed at lunar south pole region (Shiv Shakti Point) on September 23, 2023 — India became the first country to land near the lunar south pole.
  • India's GDP growth estimate for FY 2024-25: ~7.0%; fiscal deficit target: 4.9% of GDP.
  • G20 Presidency 2023: First developing country to hold G20 presidency; resulted in African Union being made a full member.
  • Aditya-L1 positioned at Lagrange point L1 (~1.5 million km from Earth) for continuous solar observation.
  • INS Vikrant (IAC-1) commissioned December 2, 2022 — India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier.
  • National Digital Education Architecture (NDEAR) launched to standardize digital education across India.
  • UPI transactions in 2023-24 exceeded 120 billion, with monthly turnover surpassing ₹20 lakh crore.
  • KAVACH indigenous train collision avoidance system fully developed by RDSO and DRDO; operational on multiple routes.
  • India's renewable energy capacity crossed 180 GW by end of 2023; target of 500 GW non-fossil by 2030 remains committed.
  • PM Vishwakarma Yojana launched September 2023; provides tools, training, and credit support to traditional artisans.
  • Dhanwantri Health Centers under Ayushman Bharat: over 1.5 lakh operational as of 2024.
  • RBI digital currency (e-Rupee) pilot expanded to multiple cities; covers wholesale and retail segments.

Common mistakes

  • Confusing ISRO's Chandrayaan-2 (2019) landing attempt with Chandrayaan-3 (2023) success — remember: Chandrayaan-3 was the FIRST successful Indian lunar landing; Chandrayaan-2's Vikram lander crashed.
  • Mixing up NITI Aayog Chairman (Amitabh Kant) with former Planning Commission members — NITI Aayog replaced Planning Commission in 2015; current chairman is not the same as former PM Dr. Manmohan Singh who headed it initially.
  • Forgetting that INS Vikrant is IAC-1 (first aircraft carrier) vs INS Vikramaditya (second, ex-Soviet); both are active but different origins.
  • Not distinguishing between G20 2023 Presidency (India hosted summit in New Delhi, Sept 2023) and G20 current rotation — Brazil held 2024 presidency.
  • Wrongly attributing UPI to RBI alone — UPI is a joint initiative of NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) under RBI's regulatory framework.
  • Confusing COP28 (Dubai, 2023) with COP27 (Sharm el-Sheikh, 2022) or COP26 (Glasgow, 2021) — India's Net Zero pledge was at COP26.
  • Incorrectly stating the fiscal deficit target — 2024-25 budget targets 4.9% (not 4.5%); remember the exact percentage for current year.
  • Mixing up Padma award levels: Padma Vibhushan (2nd), Padma Bhushan (3rd), Padma Shri (4th) — not in order of seniority!
  • Forgetting that World Environment Day 2023 theme was 'Beat Plastic Pollution' — not 'Climate Action' (that was 2024).
  • Not updating recent Supreme Court judges — CJI Sanjay Kishan Kaul (since January 2024) replaces former CJI D.Y. Chandrachud.
