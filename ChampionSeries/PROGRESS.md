# Champion Series — Production Tracker

**Working branch:** `backend-security`  
**Scope:** Standard 11 & 12 · Physics, Chemistry, Biology  
**Rule:** A chapter is marked complete only after its question bank, answer key, explanations, scoring, timer, navigation and mobile layout are checked.

## Quality standard for every chapter
- 100 distinct Gujarati MCQs, aligned to the applicable NCERT/NEET syllabus
- Four options and one verified correct answer per question
- Short, concept-correct Gujarati explanation
- NEET scoring: +4 correct, −1 incorrect, 0 unanswered
- Timer, question palette, mark-for-review, clear answer, submit and auto-submit
- Result summary and question-wise review
- Mobile-first layout and functional checks
