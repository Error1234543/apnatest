🎯 પરીક્ષા ટ્રિક્સ

બોરેક્સ મણકું પ્રયોગનાં રંગ યાદ કરવાની કોષ્ટક પદ્ધતિ: Co→ नीला, Cu→ ऑxidizing માં हरा / reducing માં लाल, Cr→ हरा, Fe→ गरम माં पिट्टा / ठंडा माં नीला, Ni→ कपडी/ब्लैक, Mn→ हल्का पिट्टा / रंगहीन. Co અને Cr બંને वातावरणમાં સમાન રંગ આપે છે.
When a question asks about borax bead test colors for transition metals or asks to identify a metal by bead color.
દા.ત.: CuCl સાથે oxidative火焰માં બોરેક્સ મણકું હરોળું દેખાય છે; reducing火焰માં લાલ (Cu₂O) દેખાય છે → उत्तर: Cu.
ડાયગોનલ સંબંધ (Diagonal Relationship): Li–Mg, Be–Al, B–Si. જે તત્વનો પ્રશ્ન થાય અને તેના ગુણધર્મો Ag ના સમાન હોય (જેમ કે carbonates thermal decomposition → oxide + CO₂, nitrate → oxide + NO₂ + O₂), તો Be અથવા Al લખો.
When asked about anomalous properties of first member of a group or similarity with second member of next group.
દા.ત.: BeCO₃ નું thermal decomposition: BeCO₃ → BeO + CO₂. MgCO₃ સાથે સમાનતા. उत्तर: Be અથવા Mg બંને સાથે સમાન વર્તણૂક.
Inert Pair Effect નું quick rule: Group 13 માં Tl > In > Ga > Al (Tl⁺ stabilized), Group 14 માં Pb > Sn > Ge > Si (Pb²⁺ stabilized). ઓક્સિડેશન અંકમાં 2 નો ઘટાડો = inert pair નો પ્રભાવ વધે છે. Pb⁴⁺ oxidizing, Tl³⁺ oxidizing.
Oxidation state stability, redox behavior, and 'which ion is more stable?' type questions.
દા.ત.: PbO₂ અને MnO₂ બંને oxidizing agents છે because Pb⁴⁺ → Pb²⁺ થાય છે (inert pair stabilization). Correct elimination.
BH₃ હંમેશા dimer B₂H₆ (diborane) રૂપમાં જ મળે છે. Diborane માં 4 terminal B−H bonds (normal 2c−2e) + 2 bridging B−H−B bonds (3c−2e). Bridge angle ≈ 97°. This structure is asked almost every year.
Structure questions about diborane, number of bonds, bridge type, hybridization of B.
દા.ત.: B₂H₆ માં કુલ 6 B−H bonds (4 normal + 2 bridge). B નું hybridization sp³ (4 regions: 3 B−H + 1 B−B bridge interaction). 3c−2e bonds = 2.
Boron trihalide Lewis acid strength reverse order: BCl₃ > BBr₃ > BF₃. Reason: back-bonding F(2p)−B(2p) strongest → BF₃ least eager to accept electrons. Size mismatch 2p−3p/4p weakens back-bonding → more Lewis acidic.
Comparing Lewis acidity of BX₃ or asking why BF₃ is weakest among BX₃ despite most electronegative halogen.
દા.ત.: BF₃ vs BCl₃: F ના 2p electrons B ના 2p સહ性强 back-bonding કરે છે, thus B પર lone pair acceptance capability ઘટે છે. Answer: BCl₃ > BF₃.
Silicate structural unit SiO₄⁴⁻. Single tetrahedron = orthosilicate (nesosilicate), sharing 1 O = pyrosilicate Si₂O₇⁶⁻, chain = inosilicate, sheet = phyllosilicate, 3D network = tectosilicate (silica/quartz). 'પૂરો shared' = pure SiO₂.
Identifying silicate class from structure description or formula.
દા.ત.: Be₃Al₂Si₆O₁₈ (beryl): Si₆O₁₈¹²⁻ chain silicate (each SiO₄ shares 2 corners) → inosilicate. Recognize Si₆O₁₈ as ring/chain type.
C₆₀ (Buckminsterfullerene): 20 hexagons + 12 pentagons, 60 vertices (carbon atoms), sp² hybridized but slightly curved. Each C bonded to 3 others. Euler: V − E + F = 2. Number of double bonds = 30.
Questions on fullerene structure, C₆₀ geometry, bond counts, or 'how many pentagons/hexagons'.
દા.ત.: C₆₀ માં કુલ 90 bonds: 60 single + 30 double. 12 pentagons (required by topology) + 20 hexagons. Any sphere-like fullerene needs exactly 12 pentagons.
CO vs CO₂ identification shortcut: CO = paramagnetic? NO → diamagnetic. CO reduces metal oxides (Fe₂O₃ → Fe), forms metal carbonyls (Ni(CO)₄), binds to hemoglobin (more than O₂). CO₂ does none of these. CO is neutral oxide; CO₂ is acidic oxide.
Distinguishing CO from CO₂ properties, gas identification, or 'which gas forms carbonyl?'
દા.ત.: Which gas reduces Fe₂O₃ to Fe and forms volatile Ni(CO)₄? Answer: CO. CO₂ cannot do either.
Borate ring/chain test: Borax Na₂B₄O₇·10H₂O + H₂SO₄ + ethanol → ethyl borate B(OEt)₃, burns with green-edged flame. This is confirmatory test for borate. Quick mnemonic: 'Ethanol + borate = green flame'.
Analytical chemistry questions on borate detection or qualitative analysis of borax.
દા.ત.: Borax solution + conc. H₂SO₄ + ethanol, heated → ester forms → green flame at burner. This identifies boron presence.
Boron vs Aluminium reactivity with acids: B is protected by oxide layer, does not react readily with non-oxidizing acids. Al dissolves in HCl but passivates in HNO₃ (oxide layer). Both are amphoteric. Tl does not dissolve in ammonia.
Questions on chemical reactivity, solubility in acids, or passivation behavior of Group 13 metals.
દા.ત.: Which Group 13 metal is PASSIVATED by HNO₃? Answer: Al (forms protective oxide). B is unreactive due to oxide coating.
Allotropes quick facts: Diamond — each C sp³, 4 C−C bonds, hardest natural substance, insulator. Graphite — sp², layers held by van der Waals, conductor, soft. Fullerenes — sp² curved, soluble in organic solvents (unlike diamond/graphite). Graphene — single layer graphite.
Comparing carbon allotropes, conductivity, hardness, hybridization, or interlayer forces.
દા.ત.: Which carbon allotrope is soluble in toluene? Answer: Fullerene (C₆₀). Diamond and graphite are insoluble in all common solvents.
Group 14 hydride thermal stability: CH₄ > SiH₄ > GeH₄ > SnH₄ > PbH₄ (decreases down group). Reducing character increases down the group. PbH₄ is least stable. Bond strength decreases as M−H bond length increases.
Stability, reducing nature, or decomposition temperature comparisons of EHahydrides.
દા.ત.: Most unstable hydride of Group 14? Answer: PbH₄. Strongest reducing agent among them? Also PbH₄.
