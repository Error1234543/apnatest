Card 1

Alcohol क्या है? इसकी सामान्य क्रिया सूत्र और वर्गीकरण लिखें।

Alcohol वह यौगिक है जिसमें एक या अधिक –OH समूह स्प3 संकरित कार्बन से जुड़े हों। सामान्य क्रिया सूत्र: CnH2n+1OH (वृद्धि) या R–OH। वर्गीकरण: • प्राथमिक (1°): –OH वाला कार्बन केवल एक अन्य कार्बन से बंधा हो, उदा. ethanol • द्वितीयक (2°): दो कार्बनों से बंधा हो, उदा. isopropanol • तृतीयक (3°): तीन कार्बनों से बंधा हो, उदा. tert-butanol साधारण अल्कोहल में 1° अल्कोहल सबसे अधिक प्रतिक्रियाशील होता है।

Card 2

Phenol की संरचना और JEE Main के लिए महत्वपूर्ण विशेषताएँ लिखें।

Phenol में –OH समूह प्रत्यक्ष रूप से benzene वलय के sp² कार्बन से जुड़ा होता है (C6H5OH)। मुख्य बिंदु: • O–H बंध में ऋणात्मक अनुनाद (-R) के कारण H+ आसानी से निकलता है → phenol अम्लीय है • +M प्रभाव से ऑक्सीजन पर अयुग्मित इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्म वलय की ओर खिंचता है → O–H द्रुतजनीयता बढ़ती है • Phenoxy anion (C6H5O–) resonance से स्थिर होता है, 6 अनुनादी संरचनाएँ • Phenol का संतृप्त अल्कोहल (जैसे ethanol) से अधिक अम्ल性情 होता है

Card 3

Phenol के अम्लीय स्वरूप का कारण स्पष्ट करें। Ethanol से तुलना सहित लिखें।

Phenol अधिक अम्लीय होने का कारण: 1. Phenoxy anion (C6H5O–) अनुनाद से स्थिर होता है — ऋणावेश ऑर्थो एवं पैरा कार्बनों तक वितरित होता है (कुल 6 अनुनादी संरचनाएँ) 2. Ethoxide anion (C2H5O–) में ऐसा कोई अनुनाद नहीं, इसके स्थिति की अपेक्षा phenoxide अधिक स्थिर है 3. Benzene वलय का −I प्रभाव ऑक्सीजन पर आवेश को और स्थिर करता है pKa मान: • Phenol ≈ 10 • Ethanol ≈ 16 कम pKa = मजबूत अम्ल → Phenol, ethanol से लगभग 10⁶ गुना अधिक अम्लीय। NaOH में phenol घोल जाता है पर NaHCO3 में नहीं — यह भेद करने वाली क्लासिक परीक्षा प्रश्न है।

Card 4

Phenol के दो महत्वपूर्ण नामांकित अभिक्रियाएँ लिखें: Kolbe-Schmitt और Reimer-Tiemann।

1) Kolbe-Schmitt अभिक्रिया: • Phenol + NaOH → Sodium phenoxide • Na-phenoxide + CO2 (压力下 120°C, 4 atm) → Salicylate (o-hydroxybenzoate) • H3O+ उपचार → salicylic acid (2-hydroxybenzoic acid) • महत्व: ortho-उत्पाद प्रमुख है क्योंकि Na+ ion-pairing O– को CO2 के करीब रखता है 2) Reimer-Tiemann अभिक्रिया: • Phenol + CHCl3 + 4NaOH (60°C) → Salicylaldehyde (o-hydroxybenzaldehyde) + कुछ मात्रा में para-उत्पाद • मध्यवर्ती: dichlorocarbene (:CCl2) बनता है • :CCl2 phenoxy anion पर ortho आक्रमण करता है → hydroxycyclohexadienone intermediate → तुरंत rearrangement → salicylaldehyde • JEE में अक्सर :CCl2 के formation mechanism पर प्रश्न आते हैं

Card 5

Phenol की अन्य महत्वपूर्ण अभिक्रियाएँ — Electrophilic substitution और Lugol's/Iodine test।

Electrophilic aromatic substitution (phenol): • –OH strong activating, o,p-directing group है • Bromination: phenol + Br2/H2O → 2,4,6-tribromophenol (white precipitate) — qualitative test के लिए प्रयोग • Nitration: dilute HNO3 → o-nitrophenol + p-nitrophenol • Friedel-Crafts: phenol की अम्लीयता के कारण AlCl3 के साथ कम प्रभावी, पर क्षारीय माध्यम में संभव Iodoform test: • Phenol स्वयं iodoform test नहीं देता • पर ethyl phenyl ether (C6H5OCH2CH3) या o/p-cresol भी नहीं — केवल –CH3CH(OH)– या –COCH3 समूह वाले यौगिक देते हैं • अक्सर विपरीत प्रश्न पढ़ें: phenol का iodoform test? उत्तर: नहीं

Card 6

Ethers की बनावट, नामांकन (IUPAC) नियम और Williamson synthesis लिखें।

संरचना: R–O–R′ (सामान्य सूत्र CnH2n+2O) IUPAC नामांकन: alkyl alkoxide — छोटे alkyl group को alkoxide के रूप में, बड़े को alkane root के रूप में; उदाहरण: CH3OC2H5 = methoxyethane Williamson synthesis (मुख्य उपचार): • R–X + NaOR′ → R–O–R′ + NaX • SN2 mechanism (primary alkyl halide सर्वोत्तम) • SECONDARY/TERertiary alkyl halide उपयोग करें तो elimination (E2) प्रमुख होगा → alkene बनेगा नकि ether • उदा. (CH3)3C–Br + NaOMe → isobutylene (E2) नहीं बल्कि t-butyl methyl ether नहीं बनेगा • सही उदाहरण: sodium ethoxide + bromoethane → diethyl ether (SN2, 80% yield) • Symmetrical ethers: C2H5OH + H2SO4 (140°C) → (C2H5)2O

Card 7

Ethers के रासायनिक गुण — कौन सी अभिक्रियाएँ होती हैं? JEE重點।

1) cleavage by HI/HBr (strong acid): • R–O–R + HI → R–OH + R–I • यदि असममित ether हो तो small alkyl group iodide बनाता है (SN2 pathway) • उदा. CH3OC2H5 + HI → CH3OH + C2H5I (CH3-O bond टूटता है क्योंकि CH3 छोटा है) • गर्मी पर अतिरिक्त HI → दूसरा alcohol भी HI से reacts 2) Peroxide formation (सुरक्षा संबंधी महत्वपूर्ण): • Hवाला ether (α-H) वायु के सम्पर्क में peroxide बनाता है • उदा. diethyl ether + O2 → C2H5O–O–CH(CH3)OC2H5 • peroxides highly explosive — ether को लंबे समय तक न रखें 3) Ethers generally unreactive toward bases, oxidizing agents (KMnO4, K2Cr2O7 नहीं करते) 4) Complexation with Lewis acids (BF3, AlCl3) — ether solvent के रूप में प्रयोग के कारण

Card 8

Primary/Secondary/Tertiary alcohols के oxidation products और PCC vs KMnO4 का अंतर स्पष्ट करें।

1° Alcohol → Aldehyde → Carboxylic acid • Mild oxidant (PCC = pyridinium chlorochromate, CH2Cl2 में) → aldehyde रोक देता है • Strong oxidant (KMnO4/H+ या K2Cr2O7/H2SO4) → carboxylic acid तक oxydize करता है • उदा. CH3CH2OH + PCC → CH3CHO (acetaldehyde) • 1° → RCOOH via strong oxidant 2° Alcohol → Ketone (कोई further oxidation नहीं unless extreme conditions) • उदा. (CH3)2CHOH + PCC/KMnO4 → (CH3)2C=O (acetone) 3° Alcohol → oxidation नहीं (C–H absent on carbon bearing OH) • Exception: strong heating + strong oxidant → dehydration 먼저 और फिर cleavage • JEE tip: PCC = selective for aldehyde from 1° alcohol; CrO3/H2SO4 (Jones reagent) = carboxylic acid

Card 9

Alcohol की Dehydration mechanism (E1/E2) और saytzeff rule — ज़्यादा substituted alkene कैसे मिलती है?

Dehydration: Alcohol → Alkene + H2O (acid catalyst: conc. H2SO4 या H3PO4, heat) Mechanism 1° vs 2°/3°: • 1° alcohol: E2 mechanism (concerted removal of β-H and –OH as water) • 2°/3° alcohol: E1 mechanism — first protonation of –OH, loss of H2O gives carbocation, then β-H removal Saytzeff (Zaitsev) rule: • dehydration में more substituted alkene major product (more stable) • उदा. 2-butanol + H2SO4 → 2-butene (80%) + 1-butene (20%) • 2-methylcyclohexanol dehydration → 1-methylcyclohexene major (tri-substituted over di-substituted) Exception: bulky base या specific condition में Hofmann product (less substituted) भी बने

Card 10

Lucas test, Victor Meyer test, and Iodoform test — इनसे 1°, 2°, 3° alcohol कैसे पहचानें?

1) Lucas test (ZnCl2 + conc. HCl): • 3° alcohol → instant turbidity (fastest, SN1 द्वारा carbocation बनता है) • 2° alcohol → turbidity in 5 min • 1° alcohol → no turbidity at room temperature (सबसे धीमा) • सीधे tertiary distinction के लिए best 2) Victor Meyer test (sequential: HI/red P → CH3I → AgNO2 → NaNO2/HgCl2 → HNO2 → NaOH/Δ): • 1° → red colour (nitrous acid test में) • 2° → blue colour • 3° → no colour (colourless solution) 3) Iodoform test (I2 + NaOH): • Positive: ethanol और सभी methyl carbinols (R–CH(OH)–CH3 या R–CO–CH3) • Negative: other primary alcohols, 2°/3° non-methyl alcohols • Methanol negative; 2-propanol positive

Card 11

Aldehydes और ketones के प्रमुख nucleophilic addition mechanism (CN⁻, ROH, NH3 derivative) लिखें।

Nucleophilic addition to C=O: 1) Addition of HCN: • Cyanohydrin बनता है: RCHO + HCN → RCH(OH)CN • Mechanism: CN⁻ carbonyl carbon पर attack करता है → tetrahedral alkoxide → protonation by HCN • Ketones slower than aldehydes (steric + electronic reasons) • उपयोग: hydroxy acid synthesis, amino acid synthesis 2) Addition of alcohols (Acetals/Ketals): • RCHO + 2ROH (dry HCl gas) → acetal + H2O • 1st equivalent → hemiacetal; 2nd → acetal • Acetals protect C=O as protecting group • Ketone से बनता है ketal (same mechanism) 3) Addition of NH3 derivatives: • Hydroxylamine (NH2OH) → oxime (C=N–OH) • Hydrazine (NH2NH2) → hydrazone • 2,4-DNP → 2,4-dinitrophenylhydrazone (orange/yellow ppt — structural identification) • Phenylhydrazine → phenylhydrazone • सभी derivatives के melting points standard reference data में available

Card 12

α-Hydrogen की अभिक्रियाशीलता, Aldol condensation और Cannizzaro reaction — दोनों का mechanism और कब कौन सा?

Aldol condensation: • Conditions: dilute base (NaOH/KOH), α-H present • Mechanism: base α-H निकालती है → enolate ion बनता है → enolate दूसरे molecule के carbonyl carbon पर attack → aldol (β-hydroxy aldehyde/ketone) → heating पर dehydration → α,β-unsaturated carbonyl • Self aldol: same aldehyde का दो अणु • Crossed aldol: two different aldehydes (mix unpredictable unless one has no α-H) Cannizzaro reaction: • Conditions: concentrated base (50% NaOH), α-H NO aldehyde only • Mechanism: one molecule oxidized to carboxylate, another reduced to alcohol (disproportionation) • उदा. 2HCHO + 50% NaOH → CH3OH + HCOONa • Formaldehyde और benzaldehyde (no α-H) canonical examples हैं • JEE distinction: अल्डिहाइड में α-H है → aldol; नहीं है → cannizzaro

Card 13

Tollen's test और Fehling's test — दोनों में क्या अंतर है और कौन सा aldehyde कौन सा देता है?

Tollen's test (Ammoniacal silver nitrate): • Reagent: [Ag(NH3)2]+ (silver mirror test) • Aliphatic + aromatic aldehydes both positive • Ketones negative • उत्पाद: metallic silver deposit (mirror) + carboxylate • उदा. C6H5CHO → C6H5COO– + Ag(s) • Formaldehyde also gives positive Fehling's test: • Reagent: Cu2+ (tartrate complex in alkaline medium) • ONLY aliphatic aldehydes positive; aromatic aldehydes NEGATIVE • उत्पाद: red precipitate of Cu2O (cuprous oxide) • उदा. CH3CHO → CH3COO– + Cu2O (brick-red ppt) • benzaldehyde, formaldehyde, acetophenone — all negative JEE quick recall: • Aromatic aldehyde: Tollens(+), Fehling(–) • Aliphatic aldehyde: Tollens(+), Fehling(+) • Ketone: both (–)

Card 14

Carboxylic acid की तैयारी (preparation) — oxidation, nitrile hydrolysis, Grignard + CO2, haloform method लिखें।

Preparation methods: 1) Oxidation of primary alcohol/aldehyde: • RCH2OH or RCHO → KMnO4/H+ or K2Cr2O7/H+ → RCOOH • उदा. ethanol → acetic acid 2) Nitrile hydrolysis: • R–CN + H2O/H+ or OH– → RCOOH + NH3 • One carbon MORE than starting halide (via Sn2 with KCN) • उदा. CH3Br + KCN → CH3CN → CH3COOH 3) Grignard reagent + CO2: • R–MgX + CO2 (dry ice) → RCOO–MgX+ → H3O+ → RCOOH • Useful for preparing acids without losing carbon 4) Haloform reaction (methyl ketones): • R–CO–CH3 + X2 + NaOH → RCOO– + CHX3↓ • Example: acetone + I2/NaOH → acetate + CHI3 (yellow ppt) • Also gives carboxylic acid with 1 less carbon

Card 15

Carboxylic acid की acidity — effect of substituents (+I, –I, resonance) और Hammett equation का संकेत।

Acidity影响因素: 1) –I groups (electron-withdrawing) → acid性强तर → benzoic acid के ortho/para position पर –NO2, –Cl, –CN जैसे substituents acidity बढ़ाते हैं 2) +I groups (electron-donating: –CH3, –OCH3) → acidity कम करता है 3) Resonance effect: –OH, –NH2 ortho/para पर positioning से resonance donation → acidity कम 4) Ortho effect: ortho-substituted benzoic acid (जहाँ substituent किसी भी nature का हो) हमेशा stronger acid होता है — steric inhibition of resonance और hydrogen bonding दोनों contribute pKa values (approx): • Benzoic acid: 4.20 • p-Nitrobenzoic: 3.41 (–NO2 –I +R withdrawal) • p-Methoxybenzoic: 4.47 (+OCH3 +M donating) • o-Nitrobenzoic: 3.49 (ortho effect dominant) • JEE tip: order of acidity questions frequently appear with o-, m-, p- isomers comparison

Card 16

Decarboxylation reaction — soda lime और electrophilic aromatic substitution context में महत्वपूर्ण उदाहरण लिखें।

Decarboxylation: RCOOH → RH + CO2 (removal of –COOH as CO2) Soda lime decarboxylation: • Sodium salt of carboxylic acid + NaOH/CaO (soda lime) on heating → alkane • उदा. CH3COONa + NaOH/CaO → CH4 + Na2CO3 • Electrolytic or thermal methods also work but soda lime classic lab method Kolbe's electrolysis (अलग concept): • Electrolysis of sodium/potassium salt of carboxylic acid • 2RCOONa → R–R + 2CO2 + H2 (at electrodes) • उदा. 2CH3COONa → C2H6 + 2CO2 + H2 Electrophilic aromatic substitution context: • –COOH strong deactivating, meta-directing group है • Бензойной кислотой में substitution meta position पर होता है • Nitration of benzoic acid: HNO3/H2SO4 → m-nitrobenzoic acid major

Card 17

Esters और amides की hydrolysis (acidic + basic) — saponification और its application लिखें।

Ester hydrolysis: • Acidic: RCOOR' + H2O/H+ ⇌ RCOOH + R'OH (reversible, equilibrium) • Basic (saponification): RCOOR' + NaOH → RCOO–Na+ + R'OH (irreversible, complete) • Saponification = base-mediated ester hydrolysis — practical use: soap making • Triglycerides + 3NaOH → glycerol + 3 soap molecules (fatty acid salts) Amide hydrolysis: • Acidic: RCONH2 + H2O/H+ → RCOOH + NH4+ • Basic: RCONH2 + NaOH → RCOO–Na+ + NH3 • Amides much less reactive than esters (resonance stabilization makes C=O less electrophilic) • Required harsher conditions (higher temp, longer time) JEE distinction: ester hydrolysis in base is irreversible (carboxylate salt formed); acid hydrolysis reversible

Card 18

Acid chlorides (acyl chlorides) — preparation, reactivity comparison, और nucleophilic acyl substitution mechanism।

Preparation: • RCOOH + SOCl2 → RCOCl + SO2 + HCl • RCOOH + PCl5 → RCOCl + POCl3 + HCl • सबसे शुद्ध方法: SOCl2 (gaseous byproducts easily removed) Reactivity order (nucleophilic acyl substitution): RCOCl > (RCO)2O > RCOOR' > RCONH2 (Acid chloride most reactive, amide least) Reason: Cl– best leaving group (weakest base among Cl–, RCOO–, RO–, NH2–); –NH2 poorest leaving group Mechanism — Nucleophilic acyl substitution (addition-elimination): 1) Nu: attacks carbonyl carbon → tetrahedral intermediate 2) Leaving group departs → C=O reforms • উদাহরণ: RCOCl + R'OH → RCOOR' + HCl (ester formation) • RCOCl + NH3 → RCONH2 (amide formation, excess NH3 required to neutralize HCl)

Card 19

JEE Main के लिए "high-yield" named reactions जो Organic Compounds with Oxygen chapter में आते हैं — short list with key points।

1) Williamson ether synthesis: SN2 on primary halide + alkoxide → ether 2) Kolbe-Schmitt reaction: phenol + CO2/NaOH → salicylic acid (ortho-directed) 3) Reimer-Tiemann reaction: phenol + CHCl3/NaOH → salicylaldehyde (:CCl2 intermediate) 4) Aldol condensation: α-H containing aldehyde/ketone + dilute base → β-hydroxy carbonyl → α,β-unsaturated 5) Cannizzaro reaction: no α-H aldehyde + conc. base → alcohol + carboxylate (disproportionation) 6) Fittig/Wurtz-Fittig: not directly oxygen but related芳烃 synthesis note 7)酯化 (Fischer esterification): RCOOH + R'OH/H+ ⇌ RCOOR' + H2O 8) Saponification: ester + NaOH → soap (irreversible) 9) Iodoform test: CH3CO– or CH3CH(OH)– group → yellow CHI3 ppt 10) Perkin reaction: aromatic aldehyde + anhydride +酸钠 → α,β-unsaturated acid (salicylic acid precursor)

Card 21

Ether aur alcohol दोनों की preparation methods ka difference — कौन सा reagent किसके लिए बेहतर है?

Alcohol preparation: • Hydration of alkenes: RCH=CH2 + H2O/H+ → Markovnikov addition → RCH(OH)CH3 • Anti-Markovnikov: RCH=CH2 + BH3 then H2O2/OH– → RCH2CH2OH • Reduction of carbonyls: RCHO + NaBH4 → 1° alcohol; R2C=O + NaBH4 → 2° alcohol • Grignard + carbonyl: RMgX + R'CHO → 2° alcohol (R'CHOH-R) Ether preparation: • Williamson synthesis (best for mixed ethers): R–X + R'O–Na → R–O–R' • Intramolecular dehydration: HO–(CH2)n–OH + H2SO4 → cyclic ether (dioxane) • Intermolecular: 2ROH + H2SO4 (140°C) → R–O–R (symmetric only) Key distinction: • Alcohol बनाने के लिए hydration/reduction/addition use होता है • Ether बनाने के लिए Williamson (SN2) सबसे useful — asymmetric ethers के लिए ideal • Diethyl ether industrial preparation: ethanol + H2SO4 (140°C), 2C2H5OH → (C2H5)2O + H2O

Card 22

Aldehyde/Ketone vs Carboxylic acid — functional group distinction tests जिनका JEE में अक्सर पूछता है।

Distinguishing tests: 1) Tollens' test: • Aldehyde → silver mirror (positive) • Ketone → no reaction • Carboxylic acid → no reaction 2) Fehling's test: • Aliphatic aldehyde → brick-red Cu2O ppt • Aromatic aldehyde → no reaction • Ketone → no reaction • Carboxylic acid → no reaction 3) NaHCO3 test: • Carboxylic acid → brisk effervescence (CO2 evolution) • Phenol → no effervescence (too weak acid) • Aldehyde/ketone → no reaction 4) 2,4-DNP test: • Aldehyde/Ketone → orange/yellow precipitate • Carboxylic acid/ester → no precipitate Quick decision tree for JEE: NaHCO3 bubbles? → Carboxylic acid ↓No Tollens positive? → Aldehyde (any type) ↓No Fehling positive? → Aliphatic aldehyde ↓No 2,4-DNP positive? → Ketone ↓No Phenol (FeCl3 violet)? → Phenol

Card 23

α-Hydrogen के replacement reactions — Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky (HVZ) reaction और उसका उपयोग।

Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky (HVZ) Reaction: • Reaction: RCH2COOH + X2/P (red phosphorus) → RCHXCOOH + HX • Specific for α-halogenation of carboxylic acids • Mechanism: P + X2 → PX3; PX3 converts RCOOH to acyl bromide/chloride → enolization at α-carbon → halogenation → hydrolysis back to α-halo acid • Product: α-halo carboxylic acid (useful for further substitution to make amino acids, hydroxy acids) • उदा. CH3CH2COOH + Br2/P → CH3CHBrCOOH (2-bromopropanoic acid) • JEE application: α-bromo acid + NH3 → α-amino acid (e.g., alanine from propanoic acid) • Important: HVZ only works with carboxylic acids having α-H; does not work with aromatic acids (benzoic acid has no α-H)

Card 24

Phenol की Electrophilic substitution में –OH का directing effect और ortho/para ratio पर प्रभाव — JEE numerical type question की conceptual basis।

–OH group phenol में strong activating, o,p-directing group है क्योंकि: • Lone pair on oxygen delocalizes into ring via +M effect • Increases electron density at o,p positions specifically • Stabilizes σ-complex (arenium ion) intermediate when electrophile attacks o/p positions Typical product ratios (approximate, for teaching purposes): • Nitration: o-nitrophenol ~30–40%, p-nitrophenol ~60–70% (para major due to steric) • Bromination (in CS2/non-polar solvent): o-bromophenol + p-bromophenol mixture (both significant) • Bromination (in water): 2,4,6-tribromophenol (all activated positions occupied simultaneously) • Sulfonation (conc. H2SO4, 20°C): o-phenolsulfonic acid kinetic product; 100°C → p-phenolsulfonic acid thermodynamic product JEE conceptual point: higher temperature favors para product (thermodynamic control, less steric strain); lower temperature favors ortho (kinetic control, closer proximity effect)

Card 25

Carbonyl compounds की physical properties — boiling point trends, solubility, aur intermolecular forces comparison।

Boiling point trends (similar molecular weight): • Carboxylic acid > Alcohol > Aldehyde ≈ Ketone > Ether > Alkane • Reason: carboxylic acids form strong dimeric H-bonds (two H-bonds per dimer); alcohols form intermolecular H-bonds; aldehydes/ketones have dipole-dipole interactions but no H-bonding between themselves Solubility in water: • Lower aldehydes/ketones (up to 4 carbons) soluble — can H-bond with water via O atom • Solubility decreases as hydrocarbon chain increases (hydrophobic effect dominates) • Phenol moderately soluble (~8 g/100 mL) — aromatic ring limits but –OH provides H-bonding • Ethers slightly soluble (weak H-bond acceptors only, no H-bond donor) JEE pattern insight: • Compare BP of CH3CHO (20°C), CH3CH2OH (78°C), CH3COOH (118°C) — all ~same MW range • This sequence proves: acid > alcohol > aldehyde for BP

Phenol के commercial/industrial importance और everyday life applications — JEE Chemistry in Everyday Life linkage।

1) Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid): • Prepared from salicylic acid + acetic anhydride • Salicylic acid from Kolbe-Schmitt reaction of phenol • Analgesic, antipyretic, anti-inflammatory 2) Phenol itself: • Antiseptic (dilute solution — Lysol contains cresols) • Precursor for plastics (Bakelite: phenol + formaldehyde) • Precursor for explosives (picric acid: 2,4,6-trinitrophenol) 3) BHA/BHT (butylated hydroxyanisole/toluene): • Phenolic antioxidants used in food packaging • Prevent rancidity by scavenging free radicals 4) Paracetamol (acetaminophen): • Synthesized from phenol via acetylation • Analgesic and antipyretic 5) Picric acid: • 2,4,6-trinitrophenol — powerful explosive, also antiseptic (old medical use) JEE link: Phenol → salicylic acid → aspirin cascade frequently asked as multi-step reaction sequence

Card 27

Ethers के solvent uses — क्यों ethers (विशेषतः diethyl ether और THF) organic reactions में prefer किए जाते हैं?

Ethers as solvents — reasons: 1) Inertness: ethers do not react with most reagents (Grignard reagents, strong bases, nucleophiles) 2) Good solvating ability: oxygen lone pairs coordinate with cations (Mg2+, Li+) → stabilize organometallic intermediates 3) Low boiling point (diethyl ether: 35°C) → easy removal by evaporation after reaction 4) Non-polar enough to dissolve organic compounds, polar enough to solvate ionic species THF (tetrahydrofuran): • Cyclic ether, higher boiling point (66°C) than diethyl ether • Better solvating power for Grignard reagents • Commonly used for Grignard, organolithium reactions Diethyl ether in Grignard: • Mg inserts into R–X forming RMgX • Ether oxygen coordinates Mg → stabilizes the otherwise very reactive Grignard reagent • Without ether solvent, Grignard formation would be very difficult

Card 28

Aldehydes aur ketones के spectroscopic properties (IR, NMR) के fundamentals जो JEE Main में आ सकते हैं।

IR Spectroscopy (Key absorption bands): • C=O stretch: 1700–1750 cm⁻¹ (strong, sharp) • Aldehyde C–H stretch: 2720 cm⁻¹ + 2820 cm⁻¹ (two weak bands — diagnostic for aldehyde) • O–H stretch (phenol): 3200–3600 cm⁻¹ (broad) • C–O stretch (ether/alcohol): 1000–1300 cm⁻¹ • Carboxylic acid O–H: very broad 2500–3300 cm⁻¹ 1H NMR Key features: • Aldehyde proton (CHO): δ 9–10 ppm (singlet, distinctive) • Phenolic OH: δ 4–7 ppm (variable, broad, D2O exchangeable) • α-H to carbonyl: δ 2–2.5 ppm • Ether α-H: δ 3.3–4.0 ppm • Alcohol α-H: δ 3.4–4.0 ppm JEE application: distinguish aldehyde from ketone using IR (the two C–H bands at 2720/2820 cm⁻¹) or 1H NMR (CHO at 9–10 ppm)

Multi-step synthesis question pattern — Phenol से aspirin तक और Phenol से phenolphthalein तक के pathways लिखें।

Pathway 1: Phenol → Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid) Step 1: Phenol + NaOH → Sodium phenoxide Step 2: Sodium phenoxide + CO2 (4 atm, 120°C) → Sodium salicylate (Kolbe-Schmitt) Step 3: Sodium salicylate + HCl → Salicylic acid Step 4: Salicylic acid + (CH3CO)2O (acetic anhydride) → Aspirin + CH3COOH • Acetylation of phenolic –OH (not carboxylic –OH) selectively Pathway 2: Phenol → Phenolphthalein Step 1: Phenol + phthalic anhydride (conc. H2SO4, heat) → Phenolphthalein • Condensation: 2 phenol molecules + 1 phthalic anhydride • Acid catalyst required • Product: pH indicator (colourless in acid, pink in base, colourless again in strong base >13) JEE note: These multi-step sequences test knowledge of named reactions + connectivity
