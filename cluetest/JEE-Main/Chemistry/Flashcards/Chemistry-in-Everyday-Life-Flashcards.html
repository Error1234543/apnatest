JEE Main Chemistry (Hindi) | Chemistry in Everyday Life | Flashcards

JEE MainHindiFlashcards

Card 1

Rasayanik vishva mein 'Chemistry in Everyday Life' ka arth kya hai?

Yeh vishay hamare prativarshik jeevan ke rasayanon par kendrit hai — dwndh (drugs), svabhbhoshak (soaps/detergents), annav adhithe (food additives), aur anya aavashyak rasayan jo pratyek vyakti ke jeevan se jude hote hain.

Card 2

Dwndh (Drugs) ko kis prakaar se vibhajit kiya ja sakta hai? JEE Main ke drishtikon se.

Dwndh ko mukhya roop se do vargon mein baata ja sakta hai: 1. Rashtriy karyawahi (Pharmacological action) ke adhaar par — jaise: Nidranilayak (sedatives), Kshudhrdhak (stimulants), Pridaah Nashak (analgesics), jwaramushak (antipyretics) etc. 2. Karm-sidhanti (Mechanism of action) ke adhaar par — receptor binding, enzyme inhibition waghera.

Card 3

'Praddaah Nashak' (Analgesics) kya hote hain? Inke do udaharan dijiye.

Praddaah Nashak ve dwndh hain jo dukh (pain) ko kam karti hain. Inke do udaharan hain: - Aspirin (Acetylsalicylic acid) — halka praddaah aur bukhar ke liye. - Paracetamol (Ace aminophenol) — bukhar aur mridu praddaah ke liye; adhik matra mein vishtarak hota hai.

Card 4

'Bukhar Nashak' (Antipyretics) ka karyakshetra kya hai? Do udaharan dein.

Antipyretic dwndh sharirik tapman ko samanya sthar par laane mein madad karti hain, khaaskar bukhar ke dauran. Unhi do udaharan aspirin aur paracetamol hain — dono hi praddaah nashak hain lekin inka mukhya upyog bukhar ghataane ka hai.

Card 5

'Antiseptics' aur 'Disinfectants' mein antar batayein.

- Antiseptics: chhote pramnaan (skin wounds) par lagai jaane wali dravya jo jinujanon ko maartiy hain ya unki vrddhi rok ti hain; udaharan: bhaiphal, hydrogen peroxide. - Disinfectants: bade pramkaan (floors, instruments) ke liye; ye injuriyaaparak ho sakti hain; udaharan: phenol ka 3% vah, carbolic acid. Dono hydrocarbon-yukt rakshak dravya hain par upyog sthar alag hota hai.

Card 6

'Ketona' (Chloramphenicol) kaisi dawa hai? JEE level par iska mahatwa kya hai?

Ketona ek broad‑spectrum antibiotic hai jo bakteriyon ko inhibit karti hai. JEE Main ke liye yeh yaad rakhein — Chloramphenicol ne 'Typhoid' ke ilaaj mein bahut yogdaan diya, lekin ise adhik matra mein istemal karne se 'bone marrow suppression' ho sakta hai.

Card 7

'Sulpha drugs' kin bakteriyan viruddh prabhavi hain? Unka kary‑kruti (mechanism) likhiye.

Sulpha drugs (jaise sulfanilamide) bacteriostatic hain; ye bacterial enzymes (dihydropteroate synthase) ko block karte hain, jisse folic acid banane ki kshamata kam ho jaati hai. Ye mainly urinary tract infections aur kuch pneumococcal infections ke liye upyogi hain.

Card 8

'Antihistamines' ka prayog kahan hota hai? Dwoodh (allergy) ke karan kya hote hain?

Antihistamines allergy ke lakshan (jhatakna, chhalaki, namna nikaas) ko kam karti hain. Inka kary‑kruti hai – H1 receptors ko block karna, jisse histamine ka proinflamation prabhav ruk jaata hai. Urinary tract infection, hay fever, allergik rhinitis mein inka istemal hota hai.

Card 9

'Tranquilizers' (Nidranilayak) kin bimariyon ke ilaaj mein diye jaate hain?

Tranquilizers mental tension, chinta, insomnia aadi ke liye diye jaate hain. Inka prayog depression, anxiety disorders aur kabhi-kabhi schizophrenia ke ilaaj mein bhi hota hai. Halanki, yeh dwndh sharik prakriya ko dhimaa karti hain, isliye inka istemal doctor ke parामर्श mein hi karein.

Card 10

'Food Preservatives' ke do udaharan dein aur unka sthirta vargh batayein.

Food preservatives food ko kharab hone se bachaate hain. - Sodium benzoate: acidic food (juices, pickles) mein. Ye antimicrobial hai. - Potassium metabisulphite: fruits, wine etc. mein — antioxidant hai. Ye donon sthir (stable) prakrit ke hote hain, adhiksam taapman se kam kamjor hote hain.

Card 11

Svanbhushak (Soap) ka rasayanik banavat kya hoti hai? Iska shodhan (cleansing) karyakshetra kaisa hota hai?

Soap sodium ya potassium ka long‑chain fatty acid salt hota hai (RCOONa/K). Shodhan ka karyakshetra hai: 1. Hydrophobic peshi (tail) charbu/ gandagi ko jod leta hai. 2. Hydrophilic head (COO⁻) paani se judkar micelle banta hai. 3. Micelle ke andar charbu phansi rehti hai aur paani se dhul jaati hai.

Card 12

'Anionic', 'Cationic' aur 'Non‑ionic' detergents ke beech antar batayein.

- Anionic detergent: SO₄⁻/SO₃⁻ anion vala (jaise sodium lauryl sulphate). Paani ki kathorata se prabhavit hota hai. - Cationic detergent: NH₄⁺ cation vala (jaise cetrimonium bromide). Antimicrobial gune bhi rakhta hai. - Non‑ionic detergent: koi charge nahin hota; kateer kathor paani mein bhi achchha kaam karta hai; jaise polyoxyethylene sorbitan monooleate (Tween).

Card 13

'Biodegradable' detergent kya hota hai? Iska mukhya aadhar kya hai?

Biodegradable detergent aisa hota hai jiska shikhar (hydrocarbon chain) seedha (straight) hota hai, jisse bacteria aasani se usko tod sakte hain. Non‑biodegradable detergent mein branched chain hota hai, jo kshaya nahin hoti aur paani ka pradbhushan karti hai.

Card 14

Kathor paani (Hard water) soap ke saath kyon pratikriya kar paata hai? Reaction likhiye.

Kathor paani mein Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺ ions hote hain. Ye ions soap ke carboxylate anion ke saath pratikriya karke insoluble precipitation bana dete hain, jisse soap ki shodhan shakti kam ho jaati hai. Reaction: 2 C₁₇H₃₅COONa + Ca²⁺ → (C₁₇H₃₅COO)₂Ca↓ + 2Na⁺

Card 15

'Synthetic detergents' (barfs) ko kyun kathor paani mein bhi upyog kiya ja sakta hai?

Synthetic detergents mein sulfonate ya sulfate jaise anion hote hain, jinke calcium/magnesium salts paani mein dravny hoti hain. Isliye ye kathor paani ke saath reaction nahi dete aur unki shauchkriya shakti bana rahti hai.

Card 16

'Enzymatic detergents' kya hote hain? Inka kya upyog hai?

Enzymatic detergents mein protease, lipase, amylase jaise enzymes milaye jaate hain. Ye jhamma, rakt, aalu ki daag jaise organic daagon ko todkar safai mein madad karte hain. Ye thande paani mein bhi kaam karte hain aur kapdon ke liye surakshit hote hain.

Card 17

'Perfumes' (sugandh dravya) ke mukhya varg (classes) kaun‑se hain?

Perfumes ko mukhya roop se teen vargon mein baata gaya hai: 1. Essential oils – phoolon/patton se nikali jaane wali (rose, jasmine). 2. Synthetic fragrances – aromatic compounds jaise benzyl acetate, vanillin. 3. Fixatives – ethanol, musk ketone jo sugandh ko drudh banaye rakhte hain.

Card 18

'Antibiotics' ko kin prakaar se vibhajit kiya ja sakta hai? Do udaharan dein.

Antibiotics ko kin prakaar se vibhajit kiya ja sakta hai? Do udaharan dein.

Card 19

'Penicillin' ka kary‑kruti kaisa hota hai? JEE level par uska mukhya bindu kya hai?

Penicillin bacteria ki cell wall synthesis ko rokti hai — peptidoglycan cross‑linking mein interference karti hai. JEE ke liye yeh yaad rakhein: Penicillin G (benzylpenicillin) oral nahi hai; injection se di jaati hai; streptococcal infections ke liye effective hai.

Card 20

'Paracetamol' aur 'Aspirin' ke kary‑kruti mein antar batayein.

- Paracetamol: cyclooxygenase (COX) ko inhibit karta hai, khaaskar CNS mein; bukhar aur halka praddaah ke liye upyogi. - Aspirin: COX‑1 aur COX‑2 dono ko block karti hai; antiplatelet gan bhi rakhti hai, isliye heart attack ke risk kam karne mein madad karti hai.
