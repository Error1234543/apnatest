Redox aur Electrochemistry — Quick Revision (JEE Main)

JEE MainHindiQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Oxidation: Woh prakriya jismein electron ka tyag hota hai ya oxidation sankhya badhti hai. Example: Na → Na⁺ + e⁻
  • Reduction: Woh prakriya jismein electron ka graham hota hai ya oxidation sankhya ghatti hai. Example: Cl₂ + 2e⁻ → 2Cl⁻
  • Redox Reaction: Aisi raasaayanik kriya jisme ek hi samay mein oxidisation aur reduction hoti hai. Dono aadhe-kriyaayein saath mein hote hain.
  • Oxidizing Agent (Oxidant): Woh pdrarth jo khud reduce hota hai aur doosre ko oxidize karta hai. Yeh electron prapta karta hai. Example: KMnO₄, K₂Cr₂O₇, HNO₃
  • Reducing Agent (Reductant): Woh pdrarth jo khud oxidize hota hai aur doosre ko reduce karta hai. Yeh electron dān detā hai. Example: Zn, H₂, KI
  • Oxidation Sankhya (O.N.): Har parmanu par arpit ki gayi kalpit valency jise yeh dān/graahn kartā hai jab elektron yugal uske adhik electronegative parmanu ki or chala jaye.
  • Galvanic Cell (Voltaic Cell): Raasāyanik ushshakta ko vidyut ushshakta me badalne wali kosh. Isme spontaneous redox kriya se bijli banti hai. Example: Daniel cell
  • EMF (Electromotive Force): Do electrodes ke beech ka potential antar. Ise cell potential E_cell bhi kehte hain. Unit: volt
  • Salt Bridge: U-tube jisme KCl ya KNO₃ ka agar gel bhara hota hai. Yeh do ardh-koshon ke beech ion sandharan banaye rakhta hai aur bijli savitritaa ko santulit karta hai. Iska mukhy karya: charge neutral rakhna aur junction potential kam karna.
  • Standard Hydrogen Electrode (SHE): Reference electrode jiska standard electrode potential zero manaya gaya hai. Isme Pt electrode H₂ gas (1 atm) aur H⁺ (1 M) ke samarpan me hota hai. Anode ya cathode dono bana saktā hai.
  • Nernst Equation: Kisi bhi aadh-kosh ya poorā kosh ke liye non-standard haalat me electrode potential ya EMF nikalne wala samikaran: E = E° − (RT/nF) ln Q. 298 K par: E = E° − (0.0591/n) log Q
  • Molar Conductivity (Λm): Vilitran ek mol electrolyte ke liye yojak kshetra phalantar ka aavishkar. Λm = κ × V (jaha κ yojakta hai aur V mol ka aayatan). Unit: S cm² mol⁻¹
  • Kohlrausch Ka Nyay: Anant talan par molar savitrikta ko cation aur anion ke aayunik savitriktaon ke yog se vyakt kiya ja sakta hai. Λ°m = ν₊λ°₊ + ν₋λ°₋. Example: Λ°(NaCl) = λ°(Na⁺) + λ°(Cl⁻)
  • Faraday Ka Prathm Nyay: Kisi electrolyte ke elektrodo pr avakshepit/vilit hui dravya rashi (w) calit bijli ki rashi (Q = I×t) ke anusmani hoti hai: w = Z×Q, jaha Z samawartan dravyamitra hai.
  • Faraday Ka Dvitīya Nyay: Same quantity of electricity se alag-alag electrolytes se avakshepit dravyaon ke mol bhagnusmani unke samawartan dravyamitron ke anusmani hote hain. w₁/E₁ = w₂/E₂ = ...
  • Faraday Nirayan (F): Ek mol electron ka aayush. F = 96485 C mol⁻¹ ≈ 96500 C mol⁻¹
  • Conductance (G): Savitritaa ka ultrata. G = 1/R. Unit: siemens (S) = Ω⁻¹
  • Limiting Molar Conductivity (Λ°m): Anant talan par molar savitrikta. Es me ions aapas me shuny spandan krte hain.

Key concepts

  • Oxidation Number niyam: (1) Mull padarthon me O.N. shunya. (2) Ek atomik anjan me sabhi parmanvon ka kul O.N. shunya. (3) Ek atomik anjan me kul O.N. anjan ke aayush ke barabar. (4) H ka O.N. adhiksarhak sandhon me +1, metal hydrides me −1. (5) O ka O.N. −2, peroxides me −1, OF₂ me +2. (6) Halogens ka O.N. −2, halides me −1; par AgF₂/IF₇ jaise vishesh sandhon me poshativ. (7) Sabse adhik electronegative parmanu pehle O.N. assign karo.
  • Jeevo me O.N. nikalne ke udahrnan: KMnO₄ me Mn = +7; K₂Cr₂O₇ me Cr = +6; H₂SO₄ me S = +6; HNO₃ me N = +5; Na₂S₂O₃ me S ka average O.N. +2 (par pratyek S alag hota hai: +6 aur −2).
  • Redox me samarthan tarika: IONS-ELECTRON TARIIKA. Dono aardh-kriyaayein likho, phir electrons ko barabar karo, dono ko jodo. Jali me H⁺/OH⁻ aavishkarak anujasik shamat se samantar karo.
  • Laghu sanikaran (LCM) method me bhi same nitya — electrons barabar karo, phir atoms aur charge check karo.
  • Galvanic Cell ka aadhar: Daniell Cell — Zn|Zn²⁺(1M)||Cu²⁺(1M)|Cu. Left electrode (anode) par oxidation: Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻. Right electrode (cathode) par reduction: Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu. Electron bahar circuit me anode se cathode ki or pravahit hote hain. Ions salt bridge dwara charge santulan banate hain.
  • E°_cell = E°_cathode − E°_anode = E°_right − E°_left. Agar E°_cell > 0 ho to kriya spontenious hoti hai.
  • Nernst Equation ka prayog: (a) Kisi bhi haalat me E_cell nikalna, (b)平衡 sthirank K ka sambandh E° ke saath: E° = (0.0591/n) log K (298 K par). Jisase badi K, utna hi adhik spontaneous.
  • Savitrikta aur talan ka sambandh: Λm = Λ°m − b√c (Kohlrausch ka prakritik nyay, only for strong electrolytes). Weak electrolytes me Λ°m ka anuman Kohlrausch ke nyay se lagato. Dilute hone par Λm badhta hai kyunki dissociation badh jata hai.
  • Faraday ke niyam ka prayog: (1) w = (M×I×t)/(n×F), (2) same Q ke liye w∝Equivalent mass, (3) electrolysis ke samay product ka mol = I×t/(n×F).
  • Strong electrolytes: Λm vs √c graph nearly straight line. Weak electrolytes: Λm tezi se badhta hai dilute hone par. Λ°m weak electrolyte ke liye direct measurement nahi milta — Kohlrausch law se nikalte hain. Example: acetic acid ka Λ°m = Λ°(CH₃COONa) + Λ°(HCl) − Λ°(NaCl).
  • Fuel cell: H₂−O₂ fuel cell me anode par H₂ oxidation, cathode par O₂ reduction. Overall: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. Shuddh jal utpadit hota hai, isliye yeh environment-friendly hai. Efficiency ~60–70%.
  • Corrosion: Loha ka rust banana — anodic area me Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻, cathodic area me O₂ + 2H₂O + 4e⁻ → 4OH⁻. Result: Fe₂O₃·xH₂O (rust). Protection: galvanization, sacrificial anode (Mg/Zn), alloying.
  • JEE Main ke liye important standard potentials yaad rakho: E°(Zn²⁺/Zn) = −0.76V, E°(Cu²⁺/Cu) = +0.34V, E°(Ag⁺/Ag) = +0.80V, E°(Fe²⁺/Fe) = −0.44V, E°(H⁺/H₂) = 0.00V, E°(MnO₄⁻/Mn²⁺) = +1.51V (acidic), E°(Cr₂O₇²⁻/Cr³⁺) = +1.33V (acidic).
  • KMnO₄ ka titrating agent ke roop me prayog: Acidic medium me MnO₄⁻ → Mn²⁺ (5e⁻ grahan, n-factor = 5). Neutral/basic me MnO₄⁻ → MnO₂ (3e⁻, n-factor = 3). Basic me MnO₄⁻ → MnO₄²⁻ (1e⁻, n-factor = 1). Isliye acidic medium me KMnO₄ sabse balishth oxidant hai.
  • Titration me KMnO₄ auto indicator hai — khud ka rang visual endpoint deta hai. K₂Cr₂O₇ ke liye diphenylamine ya potassium dichromate indicator (orange to green) use hota hai.

Important facts

  • ΔG° = −nFE°_cell. Agar E°_cell positive ho to ΔG° negative aur kriya spontaneous.
  • ε°_cell ke liye positive value = galvanic cell constructible. Negative = electrolytic cell (external voltage chahiye).
  • Conductivity κ ka one-unit meaning: 1 cm distance par rakhne valon do parallel electrodes ke beech ka solution ka conductance. κ = G × (l/A).
  • Molar conductivity Λm = κ/C, jaha C mol/cm³ ya mol/m³ me ho. SI unit: S m² mol⁻¹; practical unit: S cm² mol⁻¹.
  • Λm = κ × (1000/C) when C in mol/L. Ye formula yaad rakho — often used directly in JEE problems.
  • Kohlrausch law ka ek important application: Λ°(CH₃COOH) nikalna. Λ°(CH₃COOH) = Λ°(CH₃COONa) + Λ°(HCl) − Λ°(NaCl).
  • Faraday constant F = 96500 C/mol ya 96485 C/mol. Problem me kabhi 96500 diya jata hai, kabhi exact value.

Memory aids

  • RIGER (Reduction Is Gain Electron Rate)
  • Anode = Oxidation (A mein O) — Cathode = Reduction (C mein R). Yaad rakhne ke liye: An OX, Red CAT.

Exam tips

  • JEE Main mein Redox se 1-2 questions direct aate hain, Electrochemistry se 1-2 questions. Total 2-4 questions is chapter se expected.
  • KMnO₄/K₂Cr₂O₇ ke n-factor har medium ke liye yaad raho — acidic mein 5, neutral/basic mein 3 (MnO₂).
  • Daniel cell ka diagram aur working yaad rakho — anode-left, cathode-right, electrons left se right. JEE mein diagram-based questions aate hain.
  • Nernst equation ki numericals solve karte waqt temperature 298 K maano, 0.0591/n form use karo. Simple calculations ke liye 0.06/n bhi accept hota hai.
  • Faraday ke laws ki numericals mein w = MIt/nF formula direct apply karo. n = valency factor, M = molar mass.
  • Conductivity ki questions mein κ se Λm nikalne ke liye Λm = κ × 1000/C formula yaad rakho, C mol/L mein honi chahiye.
  • Standard electrode potential ki values compare karke spontaneity bata sakte ho — jiska E° adhik positive hai cathode banega.
  • PYQ practice karo — NTA ne pichle varshon mein KMnO₄ titration, Nernst equation numericals, Faraday's law numericals frequently pucha hai.

Common traps

  • Oxidation number me O.N. nikalte samay peroxide (-1) aur superoxide (-½) ko samajhna zaroori hai. H₂O₂ me O ka O.N. −1 hota hai, na ki −2.
  • KMnO₄ ke reaction me pH ka dhyan rakho — acidic/neutral/basic medium alag-alag products dete hain aur n-factor alag hota hai. Yeh JEE ka favorite trap hai.
  • Salt bridge ke electrolyte KCl ya KNO₃ hote hain kyunki unke cation aur anion ka transport number lagbhag barabar hota hai. AgNO₃ salt bridge mein use nahi hota kyunki Ag⁺ precipitate de sakta hai.
  • Nernst equation me log base 10 hota hai (natural log nahi). 2.303 RT/F = 0.0591 at 298K. Calculation me 0.0591/n log Q use karo, ln nahi.
  • ΔG = −nFE hota hai, ΔG° = −nFE° — donon ko galat se judha mat lena.
  • Weak electrolyte ka Λ°m experimental tarike se directly nahi maapa ja sakta — sirf Kohlrausch law se nikalta hai. Direct plot se bahar anant talan tak nahi pohanch pate.
  • Galvanic cell me anode negative hota hai (electron deta hai), electrolytic cell me anode positive hota hai (external source se electron kheencha jata hai). Yeh confuse nahi hona chahiye.
  • E° values subtract karte samay sign ka dhyan rakho — E°_cell = E°_cathode − E°_anode. Anode ka sign khud change mat karo.
