Properties of Solids and Liquids — Quick Revision (JEE Main Physics)

JEE MainHindiQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Stress (प्रत्याबल): प्रति इकाई क्षेत्रफल पर लगने वाला आंतरिक पुनर्स्थापना बल | σ = F/A | SI unit: N/m² या Pa
  • Strain (प्रत्याहन): विरूपण के कारण होनी वाली सापेक्ष ज्यामितीय परिवर्तन | ε = ΔL/L, ε = ΔV/V, या tan(θ) | dimensionless
  • Young's Modulus (यंग मापांक): E = longitudinal stress / longitudinal strain = (F/A)/(ΔL/L) | N/m²
  • Bulk Modulus (संकुचन मापांक): B = volumetric stress / volumetric strain = -P/(ΔV/V) | आयताकार चिह्न दर्शाता है कि दबाव बढ़ने पर आयतन घटता है | N/m²
  • Shear/Modulus of Rigidity (कतिपेश मापांक): G = tangential stress / shear strain = (F/A)/tan(θ) | N/m²
  • Compressibility (आकुंच्यता): k = 1/B = -ΔV/(V·P) | बल्क मॉडुलस का व्युत्क्रम | m²/N
  • Poisson's Ratio (पॉइसन अनुपात): σ = lateral strain / longitudinal strain | dimensionless; सामान्य पदार्थों के लिए 0 ≤ σ ≤ 0.5
  • Elastic Potential Energy (प्रत्यास्थ स्थितिज ऊर्जा): U = ½ × stress × strain × volume = ½·σ·ε·V = ½·(F·ΔL)·L/A
  • Elastic Energy Density: u = U/V = ½ × stress × strain
  • Pressure (दाब): P = F/A | normal force per unit area | Pa = N/m²; 1 atm = 1.013×10⁵ Pa
  • Gauge Pressure (तरल दाब): P_gauge = ρgh (gहरात्मक गहराई पर) | P_absolute = P_atm + ρgh
  • Viscosity (श्यानता): तरल की वह गुण जिसके कारण यह प्रवाह का विरोध करता है | आंतरिक घर्षण | N·s/m² या Pa·s (SI); Poise (CGS), 1 Pa·s = 10 Poise
  • Stokes' Law (स्टोक्स नियम): F = 6πηrv | एक गोल कण पर श्यान बल, η = श्यानता गुणांक, r = त्रिज्या, v = वेग | केवल laminar flow के लिए मान्य
  • Terminal Velocity (परिणामी वेग): v_t = (2r²g/9η)(ρ_s - ρ_f) | जब श्यान बल + उत्प्लावन = भार | जहाँ ρ_s = कण का घनत्व, ρ_f = तरल का घनत्व
  • Reynolds Number (रेनॉल्ड्स संख्या): Re = ρvd/η | dimensionless; Re < 2000 → laminar, 2000 < Re < 4000 → transitional, Re > 4000 → turbulent
  • Surface Tension (पृष्ठतनाव): T = F/L या T = Energy/Area | N/m | तरल की सतह को कम से कम क्षेत्रफल में रखने की प्रवृत्ति
  • Surface Energy (पृष्ठ ऊर्जा): U_s = T × ΔA | नई सतह बनाते समय आवश्यक ऊर्जा
  • Excess Pressure inside a Liquid Drop: ΔP = 2T/R | दो सतहें नहीं होतीं (एक मुक्त सतह)
  • Excess Pressure inside a Soap Bubble: ΔP = 4T/R | दो सतहें होतीं हैं (अंदर और बाहर)
  • Excess Pressure inside an Air Bubble in Liquid: ΔP = 2T/R | एक सतह (तरल-वायु अंतरापृष्ठीय)
  • Capillary Rise/Fall (कैपिलरी उन्नयन): h = (2T cosθ)/(ρgr) | θ = संपर्क कोण, r = नली की त्रिज्या; यदि θ < 90° → उन्नयन, θ > 90° → अवम्लान
  • Bernoulli's Theorem (बरनौली प्रमेय): P + ½ρv² + ρgh = constant (along a streamline) | ऊर्जा संरक्षण से व्युत्पन्न, ideal fluid (incompressible, non-viscous, steady flow) के लिए
  • Equation of Continuity (संतति समीकरण): A₁v₁ = A₂v₂ | अघूर्णी द्रव के लिए द्रव्यमान संरक्षण
  • Torricelli's Law (टोरिसेली नियम): v = √(2gh) | पात्र की तली के छेद से निकलने वाले द्रव का वेग, जहाँ h = मुक्त सतह से छेद तक गहराई

Key concepts

  • Hooke's Law: stress ∝ strain, within elastic limit. E = Y = σ/ε (within proportional limit). Three elastic moduli interrelated: E = 3B(1 - 2σ) = 2G(1 + σ). When σ = 0.5 (incompressible, e.g. rubber near limit), B → ∞. For most metals σ ≈ 0.25-0.35. These relations are essential for numerical problems involving two given moduli.
  • O→A: Proportional limit (linear, Hooke's law valid). A→B: Elastic limit (material returns to original shape if unloaded before B). B: Yield point (permanent deformation begins). B→C: Plastic region. C: Ultimate tensile strength (maximum stress). D: Fracture/breaking point. Brittle materials (cast iron) fracture near elastic limit; ductile materials (copper) have large plastic range.
  • With increase in temperature, elastic modulus generally decreases (springs become softer). For most metals, Y decreases approximately linearly over moderate ranges. This is because interatomic forces weaken as atoms vibrate more. Exception: Invar steel has nearly constant elasticity over a wide range — used in precision instruments.
  • For liquids: viscosity decreases with temperature (molecules move faster, cohesive forces weaken) → η ∝ e^(E_a/RT). For gases: viscosity increases with temperature (increased molecular collisions transfer momentum more effectively). This opposite behavior is frequently tested. Dynamic viscosity η has units Pa·s; kinematic viscosity ν = η/ρ has units m²/s.
  • At terminal velocity, net force = 0: Weight = Buoyant force + Viscous drag. mg = ρ_f V g + 6πηrv_t. Substituting m = ρ_s·(4/3)πr³: ρ_s·(4/3)πr³g = ρ_f·(4/3)πr³g + 6πηrv_t. Solving: v_t = (2r²g/9η)(ρ_s - ρ_f). Key: v_t ∝ r², inversely ∝ η, directly ∝ density difference. Heavier/denser particles settle faster; thicker fluids slow them down.
  • Molecules at surface experience net inward cohesive force (no molecules above them), so surface behaves like a stretched membrane. Surface tension T = F/L. For a liquid film (e.g. soap film), there are TWO surfaces, so effective force = 2TL. Energy perspective: creating new surface area dA requires work dW = T·dA. Molecules deeper inside have zero net cohesive force; surface molecules have potential energy excess = surface energy.
  • Curved surface creates excess pressure because surface tension pulls the surface taut. Force balance on a hemispherical section: excess pressure × πR² = T × (circumference) = T × 2πR for drop → ΔP = 2T/R. For soap bubble (two surfaces): ΔP × πR² = T × 2πR × 2 → ΔP = 4T/R. Always check whether 2T/R or 4T/R applies based on number of interfaces.
  • h = 2T cosθ/(ρgr). θ depends on liquid-tube material pair. Water-glass: θ ≈ 0°, cosθ ≈ 1 → capillary rise. Mercury-glass: θ ≈ 138°, cosθ < 0 → capillary depression. Pure water gives higher rise than tap water (impurities降低 T). Cleaning tube surface increases wettability (decreases θ). For very fine tubes (r → 0), h can be large. The meniscus shape directly reflects θ.
  • Applications: (1) Venturimeter: Q = A₁A₂√[2(P₁-P₂)/ρ(A₁²-A₂²)] for horizontal flow. (2) Torricelli: v = √(2gh). (3) Atomizer/spray gun: high-speed air creates low pressure drawing liquid up. (4) Lift on aeroplane wing: v_top > v_bottom → P_bottom > P_top. Note: Bernoulli assumes ideal fluid — no viscosity, no turbulence, steady incompressible flow along a streamline.
  • Laminar flow: smooth parallel layers (Re < 2000). Each layer slides past adjacent layers. Viscosity dominates. Turbulent flow: chaotic eddies and mixing (Re > 4000). Inertial forces dominate. Reynolds number Re = ρvd/η determines transition. d = characteristic length (pipe diameter for pipes). Blood flow in capillaries is laminar; river flow is often turbulent.

Important facts

  • SI unit of stress and pressure: Pascal (Pa) = N/m²; 1 atm = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa = 76 cmHg = 10.3 m water column
  • Strain is dimensionless — it is a ratio of similar quantities
  • Elastic moduli (Y, B, G) have same dimensions as stress: [ML⁻¹T⁻²]
  • Poisson's ratio σ ranges 0 to 0.5 for real materials; steel ≈ 0.29, rubber ≈ 0.49, cork ≈ 0
  • 1 Pa·s = 10 Poise; 1 centipose (cP) = 10⁻³ Pa·s; viscosity of water at 20°C ≈ 1.002 cP
  • Terminal velocity v_t ∝ r²: doubling radius increases v_t by 4 times
  • For a soap bubble of radius R in equilibrium: excess pressure = 4T/R
  • Work done in blowing a soap bubble of radius R: W = T × ΔA = T × 8πR² (two surfaces)
  • Capillary rise h is independent of tube shape — depends only on vertical height and radius
  • Surface tension of water at 20°C ≈ 0.0728 N/m; decreases with temperature; becomes zero at critical temperature
  • Angle of contact θ = 0° for pure water-clean glass; θ = 0° implies perfect wetting
  • In Bernoulli's equation, P + ½ρv² + ρgh = constant — each term has dimensions of pressure
  • Energy stored in stretched wire: U = ½FΔL = ½stress×strain×volume
  • For a liquid drop merging: total surface area decreases → surface energy released as heat
  • Reynolds number is dimensionless; critical value ~2000 for pipe flow transition

Memory aids

  • याद रखें: 'YBG' — Young, Bulk, Shear all modulus have same units (Pa) but different physical meanings — एक तीनों में stress/strain का अनुपात ही आधार है
  • 'TERMINAL' for v_t formula: v_t = 2r²g(ρ_s-ρ_f)/9η → याद रखें: 'radius squared, density difference, viscosity on bottom'
  • '2T/R' drop, '4T/R' bubble — bubble में दो सतहें (भीतर+बाहर) → double the effect
  • कैपिलरी सूत्र: h = 2Tcosθ/ρgr → 'टॉप में T और cosθ, नीचे ρgr' — कोण बढ़ने पर h घटती है
  • bernoulli = BEAN: B(er) + E(nergy/½ρv²) + A(ltitude/ρgh) = constant
  • पॉइसन अनुपात σ: 'सीमा 0.5, रबर आधा' — ruber ≈ 0.49 (लगभग असंपीड्य), cork = 0 (कोई lateral strain नहीं)
  • स्टोक्स बल: F = 6πηrv → 'छह इट्टी पिछली रे' — r और v दोनों के साथ रैखिक, η के साथ सीधा अनुपात
  • Re < 2000 laminar, 2000-4000 transition, > 4000 turbulent — '2-2-4 rule' याद रखें

Exam tips

  • JEE Main में इस अध्याय से 1-2 questions आते हैं — mostly direct formula application or single-concept numerical
  • Elasticity के numericals में पहले सभी quantities को SI में बदलें (mm → m, cm → m, etc.)
  • Terminal velocity question में देखें: ball solid है या hollow, fluid कितना viscous — ρ_s vs ρ_f compare करें
  • Surface tension question में 'soap film' vs 'soap bubble' vs 'liquid drop' — तीनों के formulas अलग हैं
  • Bernoulli question: सबसे पहले streamline identify करें, फिर दो points चुनें जहाँ P, v, h ज्ञात हों
  • Capillarity question में angle of contact दिया हो तो cosθ calculate करें; न दिया हो तो pure water-glass = 0° मानें
  • Reynolds number numericals में diameter = 2r या d? — प्रश्न के अनुसार ध्यान दें
  • Elastic energy numericals में ΔL छोटा होता है — calculator use करें, approximation से गलती न हो
  • Multiple formulas में अंतर: Y = FL/AΔL, B = PV/ΔV, G = FL/AΔx — हर बार देखें कि kon sa strain use हो रहा है
  • Quick verification: terminal velocity का unit m/s आना चाहिए; excess pressure का unit Pa; capillary rise का unit m

Common traps

  • Soap bubble में excess pressure 4T/R होता है, drop में 2T/R — इस भ्रम में अक्सर परीक्षा में फंसते हैं
  • Terminal velocity formula में (ρ_s - ρ_f) है, न कि ρ_s अकेले — nếu ball floats (ρ_s < ρ_f), v_t ऋणात्मक आएगा (ऊपर की ओर), लेकिन magnitude उसी सूत्र से
  • Capillary rise h का सूत्र तब लागू होता है जब θ < 90°; mercury के लिए cosθ ऋणात्मक → ह्रास होता है, 'rise' नहीं
  • Elastic potential energy = ½×stress×strain×volume — volume की गलत गिनती (लंबाई × क्षेत्रफल) करने से ऊर्जा दस गुना त्रुटि हो सकती है
  • Bernoulli समीकरण में all three terms must have same dimensions — sometimes students miss ρgh term when height changes
  • Viscosity का CGS unit Poise है; 1 Pa·s = 10 Poise — इकाई बदलते समय 10 का factor याद रखें
  • पॉइसन अनुपात σ > 0.5 नहीं हो सकता (सैद्धांतिक रूप से) — यदि किसी प्रश्न में ऐसा मिले तो त्रुटि जाँचें
  • Stokes' law केवल spherical body के लिए है; cylindrical/flat bodies के लिए अलग drag coefficient
  • Elastic modulus temperature के साथ घटता है — कई students reverse सोचते हैं कि तनाव बढ़ने पर Y बढ़ता है
  • Torricelli का v = √(2gh) तब लागू जब hole का क्षेत्रफल tank cross-section से नगण्य हो; वरना continuity equation से modify करना पड़ता है
  • Fluid at rest: pressure same at same horizontal level — oblique surfaces पर pressure still normal, magnitude ρgh from free surface
  • Work to break a drop into n smaller drops: W = T × ΔA = T × 4πR²(n^(1/3) - 1)n^(2/3)
