Dual Nature of Matter and Radiation — Quick Revision (JEE Main, Physics)

JEE MainHindiQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Photoelectric effect: Jab kisi dhatu ya padarth par uchch nishankta vaali prakash radiations padti hain, tab us se electrons nirgat hote hain jinhein photoelectrons kehte hain. Yeh prakirya first time Heinrich Hertz ne observe ki thi aur Philipp Lenard ne systematically study ki.
  • Work function (φ₀ ya W): Kisi dhatu ki satah se ek electron ko nikalne ke liye avashyak nyuntam shakti. Iska SI ekam Joule (J) ya electron-volt (eV) hai. 1 eV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J.
  • Stopping potential (V₀): Photoelectric dhara ko zero karne ke liye lagayi jaane wali nyuntam negetive retarding potential antar. V₀ se maximum kinetic energy: K_max = eV₀.
  • Photon: Prakash ka nyuntam energia packet, jiska shunya rest mass hota hai aur jo hamesha roshni ki gati (c) se gata hai. Iski energia E = hν = hc/λ hoti hai.
  • De Broglie wavelength: Kisi bhi gata huye kashik (particle) se judi hui tarang lambai, jo λ = h/p = h/mv se di jati hai. Yeh kashik-tarang samadvait (wave-particle duality) ka mukhya upadhog hai.
  • Sensitivity of a photocell: Prakash pradur (intensity) badhane par photoelectric dhara kitna badhti hai, ise sensitivity kehte hain. Yeh katip (cathode) ke kshetra fal (surface area) aur prakash ki nishankta (frequency) dono par nirbhar karti hai.

Key concepts

  • Einstein ka Photoelectric Samikaran: K_max = hν - φ₀ ya eV₀ = hν - φ₀. Isme hν photon ki puri energia hai, φ₀ dhatu ka work function hai, aur bachi hui energia electron ki gatij energia ban jati hai. Yeh samikaran shodashik (classical) tarang vichar se samajh nahi aata — iske liye prakash ke kashik (photon) mahatva ko samajhna avashyak hai.
  • Photoelectric effect ke niyantran — Prakash ki nishankta (intensity): Nishankta badhane se sirf photoelectronon ki sankhya badhti hai, lekin unki adhiktam gatij energia nahi badhti. K_max sirf prakash ki bartantala (frequency) ν par nirbhar karti hai, nishankta par nahi.
  • Photoelectric effect ke niyantran — Prakash ki bartantala (frequency): K_max ∝ (ν - ν₀), jahan ν₀ threshold frequency hai. Agar ν < ν₀ ho, to koi bhi photoelectric emission nahi hoga, chahe nishankta kitni hi adhik kyun na ho.
  • Threshold frequency (ν₀): Nyuntam bartantala jiske liye photoelectric emission shuru hota hai. ν₀ = φ₀/h. Isse kam bartantala vala prakash kisi bhi dhatu se photoelectrons nahi nikal sakta.
  • Instantaneous emission: Photoelectric emission prakasit prabhav mein hoti hai (~10⁻⁹ sec). Classical theory yeh vyakhya nahi de pati thi kyunki uske anusar energia jama hone mein samay lagta.
  • Compton Effect: Jab X-rays kisi elyak (target) par padte hain, to parivartit X-ray ki bartantala badh jati hai (λ' > λ).λ' - λ = (h/m₀c)(1 - cos θ), jahan θ skattan kona (scattering angle) hai. Yeh photon ke momentum ke astitva ka praman hai.
  • De Broglie wavelength ke kishor anuprate (Special Cases): (a) Kinetik energia se: λ = h/√(2mK) — jokismein kashik ko sthiravastha se praveg dete hain. (b) Potential antar V se praveg kit: λ = h/√(2mqV). (c) Thermal neutron: λ = h/√(3mk_BT). (d) Photon: λ = h/p = hc/E.
  • Davisson–Germer Prayog: Clinton Davisson aur Lester Germer ne 1927 mein nickel crystal se electronon ke skattan ke dwara de Broglie kalpana ka pratyaksh praman diya. Unka niyamyak kon (Bragg angle) θ aur wavelength sandharsh thi: nλ = 2d sin θ, jahan d crystalantar drkhal (interplanar spacing) hai. Yeh pratyayog kashik-tarang samadvait ka praman hai.
  • Momentum of photon: Photon ke paas sthirta massa nahi hai, phir bhi uska momentum hota hai: p = E/c = hν/c = h/λ. Yeh Compton effect aur radiation pressure ke adhar mein hai.
  • Photocell ke prakár: (1) Photoemissive cell — prakash padne par emission. (2) Photoconductive cell — prakash se sandhayakta badhti hai. (3) Photovoltaic cell — prakash se EMF utpann hota hai (solar cell).

Important facts

  • Planck constant: h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s = 4.136 × 10⁻¹⁵ eV·s. Yeh prakriti ka ek mool bhautik niyata (fundamental constant) hai.
  • Adhiktam Gatij Energia: K_max = h(ν - ν₀) = eV₀. Isliye V₀ aur ν ke beech ka graaf ek seedhi rekha hoti hai jiska kal (slope) = h/e hota hai.
  • Photoelectric emission ke liye sharat: hν ≥ φ₀ ya ν ≥ ν₀ = φ₀/h.
  • Common work functions (eV): Cesium (Cs) ≈ 2.14, Potassium (K) ≈ 2.30, Sodium (Na) ≈ 2.75, Calcium (Ca) ≈ 3.20, Copper (Cu) ≈ 4.70, Platinum (Pt) ≈ 6.35. Cs aur K ki work function sabse kam hai, isliye ve photoelectric ke liye uchit dhatu hain.
  • Wavelength range threshold: Red light (~700 nm) ke liye threshold frequency ~4.3 × 10¹⁴ Hz hoti hai. UV radiation se photoelectric effect aasani se hota hai kyunki iski bartantala adhik hoti hai.
  • De Broglie wavelength ka mass par nivaran: ḍhīle kashikon (electron, proton, alpha) ke liye, same V par electron ki λ sabse adhik hoti hai kyunki λ ∝ 1/√m aur electron ka massa sabse kam hai.
  • JEE Main frequently tests: kinetic energy vs frequency graph (straight line, slope = h/e, x-intercept = ν₀, y-intercept = -φ₀), wavelength comparison questions, stopping potential numericals.
  • One eV definition: Ek electron ko 1 volt ke potential antar se praveg dete samay prisiddh gyatij energia = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J.
  • hc = 1240 eV·nm ≈ 12400 eV·Å. Yeh value photoelectric aur de Broglie numericals mein bahut kaam aati hai. Jald hisab ke liye yaad rakhein.
  • Photons do ek saath: Do photons ka koi bhi prabhav sirf ek photon ke barabar nahi hota. Har photon apni energia alag se transfer karta hai — yahi photoelectric effect ke ek-ek photon–ek-ek electron mahatva ka aadhar hai.

Memory aids

  • Einstein Photoelectric Samikaran yaad rakhne ke liye: 'Energy Aati Hai → Work Nikalta Hai → Bachi Hoti Hai Gati Mein' = hν = φ₀ + K_max.
  • De Broglie formula yaad rakhne ke liye: 'Lambda chhota, velocity zyada' — jaldi chale waha wavelength chhoti. Formula: λ = h/mv.
  • hc = 1240 eV·nm ko yaad rakhne ke liye: 'HC 1240, yaad rakho bhai' — simple jingle.
  • Compton shift formula yaad rakhne ke liye: 'Delta lambda h/mc (1 minus cos theta)' — shabd-matra ke saath gaao.
  • Work function order yaad rakhne ke liye: 'CS KA NA CA PE' — Cs < K < Na < Ca < Cu < Pt (kam se adhik kram mein).
  • Threshold condition: 'Frequency niche → Kuch nahi, Frequency upar → Electron bahar'. Threshold ke niche to bilkul kuch nahi hota, chahe intensity kitni bhi ho.

Exam tips

  • JEE Main mein Photoelectric effect se 1–2 direct numerical aate hain — K_max, V₀, ν₀, λ nikalne ke. Formulas ko yaad rakhein aur hc = 1240 eV·nm ka prayog karein taaki jaldi calculation ho.
  • Graph-based questions common hain: V₀ vs ν ka graaf — slope = h/e, x-intercept = ν₀, y-intercept = -φ₀/e. In sab points ko pehchan sakein.
  • De Broglie wavelength comparison question mein yaad rakhein: same velocity par heavy particle ki λ chhoti; same KE par heavy particle ki λ chhoti; same V par electron ki λ sabse adhik.
  • Assertion-Reason type questions mein 'Intensity affects number of photoelectrons, not their kinetic energy' ye statement bahut common hai — sahi assertion, sahi reason dono.
  • Numerical solve karte samay units ka dhyan rakhein: frequency Hz mein, wavelength meter ya nm mein, energy eV ya J mein.hc = 1240 eV·nm use karke eV aur nm mein seedha hisab lagayein.
  • Davisson-Germer experiment se related question kam parantu important hain — crystal diffraction, Bragg's law, de Broglie wavelength ka experimental praman. Inhe briefly revise karein.
  • Agar question mein 'Which metal has the largest stopping potential for same frequency?' type ka ho, to work function jo kam hoga, uski stopping potential zyada hogi — kyunki V₀ = (hν - φ₀)/e.
  • Quick Revision ke liye sabse important equations: (1) K_max = hν - φ₀ (2) eV₀ = hν - φ₀ (3) λ = h/mv (4) λ = h/√(2mqV) (5) Δλ = (h/m₀c)(1 - cos θ). In 5 ko yaad rakhein to pura chapter cover ho jata hai.

Common traps

  • Trap 1: intensity badhane se K_max badhta hai — ye galat hai. Intensity sirf photoelectronon ki sankhya badhati hai, K_max sirf frequency par nirbhar karti hai.
  • Trap 2: agar prakash ki nishankta bahut adhik hai to threshold se neeche bhi emission ho sakti hai — galat. Threshold frequency se neeche kabhi emission nahi hoti, chahe intensity kitni bhi adhik ho.
  • Trap 3: de Broglie wavelength formual mein m = mv/(1-v²/c²) relativity ke liye zaroori hai, lekin JEE Main ke liye sirf non-relativistic λ = h/√(2mK) ka prayog hota hai.
  • Trap 4: Compton effect mein photon ki energy kam hoti hai lekin momentum bhi kam ho jata hai — galat. Momentum magnitude kam hoti hai lekin direction badal jata hai. Energy kam hona sahi hai (λ badhne ka matlab energy kam).
  • Trap 5: Photoelectron ki gati sirf frequency par nirbhar karti hai — adhart se sahi hai. Gati (speed) v = √(2K/m) hoti hai, aur K frequency par nirbhar karti hai, isliye indirectly speed bhi frequency par nirbhar karti hai. Lekin yeh kehna ki speed sirf frequency se tay hoti hai — galat — kyunki massa bhi asar dalta hai.
  • Trap 6: de Broglie wavelength ka prayog sirf electronon ke liye hota hai — galat. Yeh sabhi gata kashikon (proton, neutron, alpha, bade anu, yahan tak ki cricket ball tak) ke liye lagu hota hai, bas vadiyan itni chhoti hoti hain ki niryah nahi hoti.
  • Trap 7: Photons par Vaidyut kshetra (electric field) ka koi prabhav nahi hota kyunki photons bina charge ke hote hain — ye bilkul sahi hai, iska savdhani se prayog karein.
  • Trap 8: Stopping potential ka man (magnitude) negative hota hai, lekin ise positive value mein diya jata hai. JEE Main mein aksar V₀ = 1.5 V type ke numerics aate hain jahan sign confuse nahi karna.
